EA Sports FC 26

EA Vancouver|Electronic Arts|2025|Sports · Multiplayer · Simulation
8.0162,563 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Released in 2025, EA Sports FC 26 is EA Vancouver's take on modern sports design with football and management at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. EA Vancouver built EA Sports FC 26 as a sports sandbox first and a showcase second, with football and management as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Skill-based timing mechanics that separate the ranks
  • Licensed clubs, athletes and authentic competition structures
  • A full career mode with transfers, training and press duties
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Full stat tracking with match history and replays
  • Tactical depth with roles and player instructions
  • Financial modelling with real consequences

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control tempo, keep shape and take the highest-percentage option available in each phase of play.
  2. 02Play the training drills before your first competitive match so the timing windows are clear.
  3. 03Use the simpler difficulty for one full season to learn the systems without heavy punishment.
  4. 04Study the match stats after each game — possession and pass completion tell the story.
  5. 05Master one skill move or set-piece routine and use it until it becomes automatic.
  6. 06Choose a formation or tactic that matches how you actually play, not the most popular preset.
  7. 07Rotate your squad deliberately; stamina and form matter more than raw ratings.
