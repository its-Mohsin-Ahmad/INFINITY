Kingdom Come: Deliverance

Warhorse Studios|Warhorse Studios|2018|RPG · Open World · Story Rich
8.139,138 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Warhorse Studios built Kingdom Come: Deliverance as a role-playing sandbox first and a showcase second, with historical as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Released in 2018, Kingdom Come: Deliverance is Warhorse Studios's take on modern role-playing design with historical at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Fast travel that unlocks only once a zone has been explored
  • Distinct biomes with their own wildlife, weather and factions
  • Seamless traversal with no loading transitions between regions
  • Archive-quality research and art direction
  • Cross-platform play and progression across every supported storefront

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  3. 03Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  4. 04Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  5. 05Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  6. 06Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  7. 07Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  8. 08Platform note — 5 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
