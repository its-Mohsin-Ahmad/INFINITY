Supervive

Theorycraft Games|Theorycraft Games|2024|Battle Royale · Action · Multiplayer
7.9128,546 reviews
PC

Theorycraft Games built Supervive as a battle royale sandbox first and a showcase second, with hero shooter as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Theorycraft Games returns with Supervive, a battle royale title that leans hard into hero shooter. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Ranked progression with placement matches and decay
  • Squad play with pings, comms and role specialisation
  • Seasonal content drops with map, weapon and mode rotations
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Distinct hero kits with hard counters
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  3. 03Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  4. 04Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  5. 05Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  6. 06Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  7. 07Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
