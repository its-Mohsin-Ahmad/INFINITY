No Man's Sky

Hello Games|Hello Games|2016|Adventure · Survival · Open World · Sandbox
8.595,721 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

First launched in 2016, No Man's Sky remains a reference point for adventure design built on space sim and crafting. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. No Man's Sky is a adventure experience from Hello Games, built around space sim and crafting. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Environmental threats ranging from weather to predators
  • Deep inventory and weight management
  • Co-op scaling that keeps the challenge intact for a full squad
  • Newtonian flight with docking and trade routes
  • Cross-platform play and progression across every supported storefront

Modes

single playercampaignsandboxcross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  3. 03Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  4. 04Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  5. 05Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  6. 06Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  7. 07Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  8. 08Platform note — 6 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
