Sackboy: A Big Adventure

Sumo Digital|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2020|Family · Adventure · Co-op
7.954,744 reviews
PCPS5PS4

Sackboy: A Big Adventure is the rare family-friendly release that treats platformer and party as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. From Sumo Digital, Sackboy: A Big Adventure is a family-friendly game where platformer and party drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Local split-screen and pass-and-play support
  • Family-friendly audio and avatar customisation
  • Generous checkpointing for younger players
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Precision movement with coyote time and air control
  • Custom rules and round modifiers
