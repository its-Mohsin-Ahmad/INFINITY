RimWorld

Ludeon Studios|Ludeon Studios|2018|Simulation · Strategy · Survival
9.1106,790 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

RimWorld is what happens when Ludeon Studios commits fully to simulation and management and crafting. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. RimWorld is the rare simulation release that treats management and crafting as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Long-tail progression measured in hundreds of hours
  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Financial modelling with real consequences
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  3. 03Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  4. 04Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  5. 05Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  6. 06Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  7. 07Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
