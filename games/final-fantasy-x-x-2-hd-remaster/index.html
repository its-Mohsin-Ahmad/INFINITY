Final Fantasy X/X-2 HD Remaster

Square Enix|Square Enix|2016|RPG · Story Rich
9.049,508 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

Final Fantasy X/X-2 HD Remaster pairs role-playing systems with turn-based rpg and fantasy, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Released in 2016, Final Fantasy X/X-2 HD Remaster is Square Enix's take on modern role-playing design with turn-based rpg and fantasy at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Choice systems that alter relationships, not only endings
  • Director commentary and behind-the-scenes features
  • Performance-captured dialogue with a full ensemble cast
  • Party composition with elemental weaknesses
  • Magic schools with distinct resource economies

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  3. 03Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  4. 04Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  5. 05Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  6. 06Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  7. 07Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
