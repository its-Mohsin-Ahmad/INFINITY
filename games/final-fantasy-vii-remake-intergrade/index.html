Final Fantasy VII Remake Intergrade

Square Enix|Square Enix|2021|RPG · Action · Story Rich
9.1102,421 reviews
PCPS5PS4

From Square Enix, Final Fantasy VII Remake Intergrade is a role-playing game where action rpg and sci-fi drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Final Fantasy VII Remake Intergrade is the rare role-playing release that treats action rpg and sci-fi as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Real-time combat with stat-driven damage maths
  • Speculative technology with in-world justification

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  3. 03Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  4. 04Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  5. 05Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  6. 06Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  7. 07Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
