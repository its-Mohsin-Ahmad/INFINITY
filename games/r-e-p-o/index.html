R.E.P.O.

semiwork|semiwork|2025|Horror · Co-op
8.6122,227 reviews
PC

semiwork built R.E.P.O. as a horror sandbox first and a showcase second, with physics puzzle and monster hunt as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. R.E.P.O. pairs horror systems with physics puzzle and monster hunt, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Optional one-life mode for the committed
  • Sanity, light and stamina systems that raise tension
  • Finite resources that make every decision count
  • Friends Pass access so a squad only needs one copy
  • Asymmetric roles that demand genuine coordination
  • Designed from the ground up for two to four players
  • Sandbox levels for experimenting with mechanics
  • Monster AI with sensory perception and stalking behaviour

Modes

single playermultiplayerco oponline coop

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Manage light, noise and inventory so that every encounter is one you chose to pick.
  2. 02Note where threats patrol; routes that were dangerous early often become quiet later.
  3. 03Conserve ammunition by disabling or avoiding enemies where the layout allows.
  4. 04Take breaks. Tension degrades decision-making faster than it degrades the game.
  5. 05Play with headphones. Spatial audio is the primary information channel in this game.
  6. 06Learn the safe room locations and treat them as planning points, not just save points.
  7. 07Check every room for resources, but never linger once you have what you need.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
