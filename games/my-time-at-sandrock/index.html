My Time at Sandrock

Pathea Games|Pathea Games|2023|Simulation · RPG · Co-op
8.460,583 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

In My Time at Sandrock, Pathea Games uses simulation conventions as a foundation and life sim and crafting as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Pathea Games returns with My Time at Sandrock, a simulation title that leans hard into life sim and crafting. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Detailed statistics and analytics dashboards
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Build and interior design tools
  • Co-op progression is fully synced, including achievements and unlocks

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Balance throughput against cost, and expand only as fast as logistics can sustain.
  2. 02Play one long session with a single goal in mind; simulation games reward clear objectives.
  3. 03Set up the first resource chain end to end before adding a second.
  4. 04Keep spare capacity in power, storage and transport so growth is never gated by infrastructure.
  5. 05Save often, and label saves before large redesigns.
  6. 06Once the basics are automatic, increase demand and see which system fails first.
  7. 07Watch the analytics panels to find the bottleneck rather than expanding everything at once.
