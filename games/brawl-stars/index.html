Brawl Stars

Supercell|Supercell|2018|Action · Multiplayer · Family
8.5100,769 reviews
AndroidiOS

Released in 2018, Brawl Stars is Supercell's take on modern action design with hero shooter and arena shooter at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Brawl Stars pairs action systems with hero shooter and arena shooter, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Skill-based matchmaking with transparent rating tiers
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Role queue and clear team-composition feedback
  • Short match formats built for ranked play

  3. 03Assign roles based on what each player enjoys, not on who is objectively best.

  6. 06If someone is struggling, hand them the support role; it is usually the most valuable one.
