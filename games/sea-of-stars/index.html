Sea of Stars

Sabotage Studio|Sabotage Studio|2023|RPG · Adventure
8.755,443 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

From Sabotage Studio, Sea of Stars is a role-playing game where turn-based rpg and fantasy drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Sabotage Studio built Sea of Stars as a role-playing sandbox first and a showcase second, with turn-based rpg and fantasy as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Turn-order manipulation and status stacking
  • Magic schools with distinct resource economies

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  3. 03Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  4. 04Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  5. 05Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  6. 06Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  7. 07Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
