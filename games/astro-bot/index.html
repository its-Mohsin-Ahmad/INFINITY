Astro Bot

Team Asobi|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2024|Action · Adventure · Family
9.497,499 reviews
PS5

Team Asobi returns with Astro Bot, a action title that leans hard into platformer and party. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Astro Bot takes the action blueprint and rebuilds it around platformer and party. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Precision movement with coyote time and air control
  • Custom rules and round modifiers
