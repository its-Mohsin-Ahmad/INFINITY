Dead Space

Motive Studio|Electronic Arts|2023|Horror · Action · Third Person · Story Rich
8.9130,853 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

First launched in 2023, Dead Space remains a reference point for horror design built on survival horror and sci-fi. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Dead Space marks another horror entry from Motive Studio, and survival horror and sci-fi is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Sound design that prioritises spatial cues over cheap jumps
  • Sanity, light and stamina systems that raise tension
  • Persistent threats that learn and reposition
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Safe rooms with item boxes and save stations
  • Hardware, ship and station design language

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  3. 03Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  4. 04Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  5. 05Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  6. 06Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  7. 07Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
