The King of Fighters XV

SNK|SNK|2022|Fighting · Multiplayer · Action
8.262,926 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|S

From SNK, The King of Fighters XV is a fighting game where arena shooter drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. SNK returns with The King of Fighters XV, a fighting title that leans hard into arena shooter. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Rollback netcode with region-aware matchmaking
  • Ranked ladders with per-character rating
  • Full story mode with hand-animated cinematics
  • Full stat tracking with match history and replays
  • Seasonal progression with challenges and rewards
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Map control, pickups and respawn economy
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control spacing and frame advantage, then punish predictable behaviour with a full combo.
  2. 02Watch your own replays and count how often you press a button while minus.
  3. 03Learn the matchup for the character you lose to most before learning a new fighter.
  4. 04Set a ranked goal per session and stop when you hit it, win or lose.
  5. 05Learn one character's normals, one anti-air and one reliable punish.
  6. 06Practise blocking low attacks on reaction, then add delayed tech.
  7. 07Complete the tutorial and the character-specific combo trials before ranked.
