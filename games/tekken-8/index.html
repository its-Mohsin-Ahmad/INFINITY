Tekken 8

Bandai Namco Studios|Bandai Namco Entertainment|2024|Fighting · Multiplayer · Action
8.9124,116 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Tekken 8 takes the fighting blueprint and rebuilds it around arena shooter. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Tekken 8 is what happens when Bandai Namco Studios commits fully to fighting and arena shooter. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Full story mode with hand-animated cinematics
  • Extensive customisation across outfits and colour channels
  • Rollback netcode with region-aware matchmaking
  • Seasonal progression with challenges and rewards
  • Full stat tracking with match history and replays
  • Dedicated server architecture with a 60Hz+ tick rate
  • Map control, pickups and respawn economy
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

single playermultiplayeronline pvpranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control spacing and frame advantage, then punish predictable behaviour with a full combo.
  2. 02Set a ranked goal per session and stop when you hit it, win or lose.
  3. 03Watch your own replays and count how often you press a button while minus.
  4. 04Complete the tutorial and the character-specific combo trials before ranked.
  5. 05Play long sets rather than first-to-one; adaptation is the real skill.
  6. 06Use training mode's frame data display to check whether moves are safe on block.
  7. 07Learn the matchup for the character you lose to most before learning a new fighter.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
