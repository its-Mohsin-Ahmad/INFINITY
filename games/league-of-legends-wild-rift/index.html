League of Legends: Wild Rift

Riot Games|Riot Games|2020|Multiplayer · Action
8.274,385 reviews
AndroidiOS

Released in 2020, League of Legends: Wild Rift is Riot Games's take on modern multiplayer design with moba and hero brawler at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Riot Games returns with League of Legends: Wild Rift, a multiplayer title that leans hard into moba and hero brawler. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Skill-based matchmaking with transparent rating tiers
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Full stat tracking with match history and replays
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Draft and ban phases with role queue
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches
