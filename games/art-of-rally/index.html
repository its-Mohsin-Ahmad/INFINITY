art of rally

Funselektor Labs|Funselektor Labs|2020|Racing · Indie · Simulation
8.345,570 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Funselektor Labs returns with art of rally, a racing title that leans hard into sim racing and idle. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. art of rally marks another racing entry from Funselektor Labs, and sim racing and idle is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Dynamic time of day and weather across the full calendar
  • Tuning suite for gear ratios, suspension, camber and aero
  • Original score with adaptive audio systems
  • Tight, focused runtime built around one strong idea
  • Accessibility options shipped on day one
  • Tyre temperature and fuel-load strategy across race distance
  • Prestige loops with permanent multipliers

single playercross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  3. 03Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  4. 04Race shorter events first so mistakes cost fewer laps.
  5. 05Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  6. 06Drive a single car for ten laps on a single circuit before changing either variable.
  7. 07Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
