Farming Simulator 22

GIANTS Software|GIANTS Software|2021|Simulation · Multiplayer
7.950,992 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

From GIANTS Software, Farming Simulator 22 is a simulation game where management and farming sim drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Farming Simulator 22 takes the simulation blueprint and rebuilds it around management and farming sim. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Dynamic events and disasters that force replanning
  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Skill-based matchmaking with transparent rating tiers
  • Dedicated server architecture with a 60Hz+ tick rate
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Financial modelling with real consequences
  • Vehicle fleet with attachment compatibility

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Balance throughput against cost, and expand only as fast as logistics can sustain.
  2. 02Keep spare capacity in power, storage and transport so growth is never gated by infrastructure.
  3. 03Set up the first resource chain end to end before adding a second.
  4. 04Save often, and label saves before large redesigns.
  5. 05Play one long session with a single goal in mind; simulation games reward clear objectives.
  6. 06Once the basics are automatic, increase demand and see which system fails first.
  7. 07Use blueprints for anything you will build more than twice.
