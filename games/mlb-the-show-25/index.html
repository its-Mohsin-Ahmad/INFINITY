MLB The Show 25

San Diego Studio|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2025|Sports · Simulation · Multiplayer
8.291,415 reviews
PS5PS4Switch

Released in 2025, MLB The Show 25 is San Diego Studio's take on modern sports design with basketball and management at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. San Diego Studio built MLB The Show 25 as a sports sandbox first and a showcase second, with basketball and management as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Configurable tactics, formations and set-piece routines
  • Licensed clubs, athletes and authentic competition structures
  • A full career mode with transfers, training and press duties
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Dynamic events and disasters that force replanning
  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Authentic pace, spacing and playbook systems
  • Staff, morale and logistics across departments

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control tempo, keep shape and take the highest-percentage option available in each phase of play.
  2. 02Study the match stats after each game — possession and pass completion tell the story.
  3. 03Rotate your squad deliberately; stamina and form matter more than raw ratings.
  4. 04Choose a formation or tactic that matches how you actually play, not the most popular preset.
  5. 05Move online once the fundamentals feel routine, and expect the pace to jump.
  6. 06Use the simpler difficulty for one full season to learn the systems without heavy punishment.
  7. 07Master one skill move or set-piece routine and use it until it becomes automatic.
