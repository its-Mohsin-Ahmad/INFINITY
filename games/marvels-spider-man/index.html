Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered

Insomniac Games|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2022|Action · Adventure · Open World
8.9107,857 reviews
PCPS5

Insomniac Games built Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered as a action sandbox first and a showcase second, with superhero and beat 'em up as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered is the rare action release that treats superhero and beat 'em up as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Traversal powers with city-scale freedom
  • Crowd-control combat with juggles and throws

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  3. 03Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  4. 04Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  5. 05Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  6. 06Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  7. 07Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
