Fall Guys

Mediatonic|Epic Games|2020|Family · Multiplayer
7.874,994 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Fall Guys marks another family-friendly entry from Mediatonic, and party and platformer is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Released in 2020, Fall Guys is Mediatonic's take on modern family-friendly design with party and platformer at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Online play is the main event, with dedicated servers and skill-based matchmaking. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Generous checkpointing for younger players
  • Simple onboarding that teaches without tutorials
  • Cross-generation play across living-room platforms
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Dedicated server architecture with a 60Hz+ tick rate
  • Four-player chaos with rubber-banded stakes
  • Precision movement with coyote time and air control
