Surviving Mars

Haemimont Games|Paradox Interactive|2018|Strategy · Simulation
8.050,818 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Surviving Mars marks another strategy entry from Haemimont Games, and management and city builder is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Surviving Mars pairs strategy systems with management and city builder, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Doctrines and tech trees with genuine opportunity costs
  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Detailed statistics and analytics dashboards
  • Financial modelling with real consequences
  • Zoning, traffic and utility simulation at district scale

Modes

single playercross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  3. 03Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  4. 04Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  5. 05Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  6. 06Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  7. 07Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
