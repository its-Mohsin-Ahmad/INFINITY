Inscryption

Daniel Mullins Games|Devolver Digital|2021|Indie · Puzzle · Horror
9.0101,974 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Inscryption is what happens when Daniel Mullins Games commits fully to independent and card battler and deckbuilder. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. First launched in 2021, Inscryption remains a reference point for independent design built on card battler and deckbuilder. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Hand-crafted art direction with a signature visual identity
  • Full controller and Steam Deck verification
  • Regular post-launch updates funded by the community
  • Zero-fail design that keeps the emphasis on thinking
  • Leaderboards, ghosts and speedrun timers
  • Colour-blind safe palette and audio cue alternatives
  • Draft, build and counter enemy archetypes
  • Deck construction with relic and artefact synergy

Modes

single playercross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Manage light, noise and inventory so that every encounter is one you chose to pick.
  2. 02Check every room for resources, but never linger once you have what you need.
  3. 03Learn the safe room locations and treat them as planning points, not just save points.
  4. 04Set brightness deliberately — too dark hides mechanics, too bright kills the atmosphere.
  5. 05Keep your inventory light enough to pick up anything you find.
  6. 06Note where threats patrol; routes that were dangerous early often become quiet later.
  7. 07Take breaks. Tension degrades decision-making faster than it degrades the game.
  8. 08Platform note — 6 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
