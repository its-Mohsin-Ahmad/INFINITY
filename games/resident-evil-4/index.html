Resident Evil 4

Capcom|Capcom|2023|Action · Horror · Story Rich · Third Person
9.2135,439 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneiOS

First launched in 2023, Resident Evil 4 remains a reference point for action design built on survival horror and cover shooter. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Resident Evil 4 is the rare action release that treats survival horror and cover shooter as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Multiple endings driven by in-run choices
  • Optional one-life mode for the committed
  • Finite resources that make every decision count
  • Ammo scarcity and inventory management under pressure
  • Blind fire, snap cover and flank-happy AI

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  3. 03Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  4. 04Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  5. 05Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  6. 06Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  7. 07Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  8. 08Platform note — 6 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
