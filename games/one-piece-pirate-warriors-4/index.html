One Piece: Pirate Warriors 4

Omega Force|Bandai Namco Entertainment|2020|Action · Fighting · Co-op
7.645,234 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

One Piece: Pirate Warriors 4 pairs action systems with hack & slash and arena shooter, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Released in 2020, One Piece: Pirate Warriors 4 is Omega Force's take on modern action design with hack & slash and arena shooter at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Tutorial and combo challenges for every character archetype
  • Extensive customisation across outfits and colour channels
  • Frame data and training tools built into the client
  • Mid-combo weapon and ability switching
  • Short match formats built for ranked play

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control spacing and frame advantage, then punish predictable behaviour with a full combo.
  2. 02Set a ranked goal per session and stop when you hit it, win or lose.
  3. 03Watch your own replays and count how often you press a button while minus.
  4. 04Learn one character's normals, one anti-air and one reliable punish.
  5. 05Complete the tutorial and the character-specific combo trials before ranked.
  6. 06Play long sets rather than first-to-one; adaptation is the real skill.
  7. 07Use training mode's frame data display to check whether moves are safe on block.
