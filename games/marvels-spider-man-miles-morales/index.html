Marvel's Spider-Man: Miles Morales

Insomniac Games|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2020|Action · Adventure · Open World
8.665,211 reviews
PCPS5PS4

Released in 2020, Marvel's Spider-Man: Miles Morales is Insomniac Games's take on modern action design with superhero and beat 'em up at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Insomniac Games built Marvel's Spider-Man: Miles Morales as a action sandbox first and a showcase second, with superhero and beat 'em up as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Dual hero and villain narrative beats
  • Co-op brawling with shared combo meters

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  3. 03Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  4. 04Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  5. 05Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  6. 06Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  7. 07Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
