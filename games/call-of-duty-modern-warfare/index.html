Call of Duty: Modern Warfare

Infinity Ward|Activision|2019|FPS · Action · Multiplayer · Story Rich
8.155,467 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare is the rare first-person shooter release that treats military and tactical shooter as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Infinity Ward returns with Call of Duty: Modern Warfare, a first-person shooter title that leans hard into military and tactical shooter. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Gunplay tuned for controller and mouse alike, with no aim-assist tax
  • Weapon sandbox with distinct recoil, handling and TTK per class
  • Destruction and bullet penetration modelled on real materials
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Economy rounds with buy-phase decisions
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  3. 03Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  4. 04Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  5. 05Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  6. 06Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  7. 07Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
