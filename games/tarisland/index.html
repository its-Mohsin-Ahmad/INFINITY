Tarisland

Loongforce|Level Infinite|2024|MMORPG · RPG
7.080,179 reviews
PCAndroidiOS

Tarisland is what happens when Loongforce commits fully to massively multiplayer and fantasy and tactical rpg. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Released in 2024, Tarisland is Loongforce's take on modern massively multiplayer design with fantasy and tactical rpg at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Social tooling — emotes, housing, guild halls and banks
  • Persistent world with a genuinely player-driven economy
  • Deep character progression with respec flexibility
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Magic schools with distinct resource economies
  • Class change and promotion systems

Modes

single playercampaignmultiplayer

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  3. 03Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  4. 04Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  5. 05Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  6. 06Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  7. 07Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
