The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition

Bethesda Game Studios|Bethesda Softworks|2016|RPG · Open World
9.4126,452 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition is the rare role-playing release that treats fantasy and action rpg as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition marks another role-playing entry from Bethesda Game Studios, and fantasy and action rpg is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Distinct biomes with their own wildlife, weather and factions
  • Cinematic camera and photo mode for capture enthusiasts
  • A living map of dynamic events, convoys and emergent encounters
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours
  • Real-time combat with stat-driven damage maths

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  3. 03Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  4. 04Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  5. 05Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  6. 06Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  7. 07Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
