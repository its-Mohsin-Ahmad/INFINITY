Dune: Awakening

Funcom|Funcom|2025|Survival · MMORPG · Open World
8.5101,607 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

From Funcom, Dune: Awakening is a survival game where crafting drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Funcom returns with Dune: Awakening, a survival title that leans hard into crafting. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Deep inventory and weight management
  • Environmental threats ranging from weather to predators
  • Co-op scaling that keeps the challenge intact for a full squad
  • Deep character progression with respec flexibility
  • Group content from small dungeons to large-format raids
  • Persistent world with a genuinely player-driven economy
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single playercampaignmultiplayer

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  3. 03Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  4. 04Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  5. 05Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  6. 06Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  7. 07Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
