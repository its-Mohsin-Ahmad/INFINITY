iRacing

iRacing.com Motorsport Simulations|iRacing.com|2008|Racing · Simulation · Multiplayer
8.533,311 reviews
PC

Released in 2008, iRacing is iRacing.com Motorsport Simulations's take on modern racing design with sim racing and management at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. First launched in 2008, iRacing remains a reference point for racing design built on sim racing and management. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Ranked racing with licences and a clean-driving rating
  • Replay tooling with camera rigs and telemetry overlays
  • Dynamic time of day and weather across the full calendar
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Detailed statistics and analytics dashboards
  • Dynamic events and disasters that force replanning
  • Force-feedback profiles and telemetry output
  • Staff, morale and logistics across departments

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  3. 03Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
  4. 04Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
  5. 05Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  6. 06Drive a single car for ten laps on a single circuit before changing either variable.
  7. 07Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
