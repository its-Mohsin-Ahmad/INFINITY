SMITE 2

Titan Forge Games|Hi-Rez Studios|2025|Multiplayer · Action
7.895,277 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Titan Forge Games built SMITE 2 as a multiplayer sandbox first and a showcase second, with moba and hero brawler as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. SMITE 2 is what happens when Titan Forge Games commits fully to multiplayer and moba and hero brawler. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Full stat tracking with match history and replays
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Lane maps with neutral objectives and jungle rotations
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches
