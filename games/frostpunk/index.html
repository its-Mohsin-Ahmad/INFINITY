Frostpunk

11 bit studios|11 bit studios|2018|Strategy · Simulation · Survival
8.893,845 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

In Frostpunk, 11 bit studios uses strategy conventions as a foundation and city builder and management as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. 11 bit studios returns with Frostpunk, a strategy title that leans hard into city builder and management. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • Doctrines and tech trees with genuine opportunity costs
  • Dynamic events and disasters that force replanning
  • Long-tail progression measured in hundreds of hours
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Policy and taxation systems with visible effects
  • Financial modelling with real consequences

single playercross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  3. 03Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  4. 04Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  5. 05Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  6. 06Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  7. 07Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  8. 08Platform note — 5 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
