GreedFall

Spiders|Focus Home Interactive|2019|RPG · Action · Open World
8.046,528 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Spiders returns with GreedFall, a role-playing title that leans hard into fantasy and action rpg. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Spiders built GreedFall as a role-playing sandbox first and a showcase second, with fantasy and action rpg as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours
  • Loot affixes that reshape entire builds

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  3. 03Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  4. 04Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  5. 05Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  6. 06Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  7. 07Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  8. 08Platform note — 5 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
