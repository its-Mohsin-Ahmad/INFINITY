God of War Ragnarok

Santa Monica Studio|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2022|Action · Adventure · Story Rich
9.4124,096 reviews
PCPS5PS4

God of War Ragnarok is what happens when Santa Monica Studio commits fully to action and hack & slash and fantasy. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Santa Monica Studio built God of War Ragnarok as a action sandbox first and a showcase second, with hack & slash and fantasy as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Mid-combo weapon and ability switching
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  3. 03Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  4. 04Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  5. 05Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  6. 06Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  7. 07Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
