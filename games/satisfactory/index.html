Satisfactory

Coffee Stain Studios|Coffee Stain Publishing|2024|Simulation · Strategy · Co-op · Sandbox
9.2131,522 reviews
PC

Satisfactory pairs simulation systems with management and crafting, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Satisfactory is a simulation experience from Coffee Stain Studios, built around management and crafting. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Long-tail progression measured in hundreds of hours
  • Dynamic events and disasters that force replanning
  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Doctrines and tech trees with genuine opportunity costs
  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Financial modelling with real consequences
  • Co-op progression is fully synced, including achievements and unlocks

Modes

single playermultiplayerco oponline coopsandbox

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  3. 03Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  4. 04Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  5. 05Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  6. 06Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  7. 07Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
