Elden Ring

FromSoftware|Bandai Namco Entertainment|2022|RPG · Action · Open World · Story Rich
9.5107,162 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

From FromSoftware, Elden Ring is a role-playing game where soulslike and fantasy drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. In Elden Ring, FromSoftware uses role-playing conventions as a foundation and soulslike and fantasy as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Lost-currency retrieval and shortcut-driven level design
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  3. 03Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  4. 04Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  5. 05Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  6. 06Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  7. 07Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
