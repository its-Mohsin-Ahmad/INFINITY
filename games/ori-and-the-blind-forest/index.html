Ori and the Blind Forest

Moon Studios|Xbox Game Studios|2015|Adventure · Story Rich
9.046,283 reviews
PCXbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Ori and the Blind Forest is the rare adventure release that treats platformer and metroidvania as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Moon Studios returns with Ori and the Blind Forest, a adventure title that leans hard into platformer and metroidvania. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Chapter select and scene replay after completion
  • Director commentary and behind-the-scenes features
  • Environmental storytelling layered into every set
  • Precision movement with coyote time and air control
  • Hand-drawn map with player-placed pins

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  3. 03Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  4. 04Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  5. 05Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  6. 06Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  7. 07Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
