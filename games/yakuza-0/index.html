Yakuza 0

Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio|Sega|2018|Action · Adventure · Story Rich · Open World
9.059,395 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

Released in 2018, Yakuza 0 is Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio's take on modern action design with beat 'em up and detective at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio built Yakuza 0 as a action sandbox first and a showcase second, with beat 'em up and detective as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Crowd-control combat with juggles and throws
  • Observation and interrogation minigames

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  3. 03Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  4. 04Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  5. 05Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  6. 06Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  7. 07Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
