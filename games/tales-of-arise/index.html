Tales of Arise

Bandai Namco Studios|Bandai Namco Entertainment|2021|RPG · Action · Story Rich
8.6108,320 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

First launched in 2021, Tales of Arise remains a reference point for role-playing design built on action rpg and fantasy. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Tales of Arise is the rare role-playing release that treats action rpg and fantasy as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Real-time combat with stat-driven damage maths
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  3. 03Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  4. 04Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  5. 05Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  6. 06Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  7. 07Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
