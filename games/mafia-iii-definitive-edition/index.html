Mafia III: Definitive Edition

Hangar 13|2K|2020|Action · Open World · Story Rich
7.343,966 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

Mafia III: Definitive Edition marks another action entry from Hangar 13, and cover shooter and historical is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Released in 2020, Mafia III: Definitive Edition is Hangar 13's take on modern action design with cover shooter and historical at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Seamless traversal with no loading transitions between regions
  • Cinematic camera and photo mode for capture enthusiasts
  • Fast travel that unlocks only once a zone has been explored
  • Blind fire, snap cover and flank-happy AI
  • Historically grounded settings, equipment and tactics

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  3. 03Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  4. 04Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  5. 05Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  6. 06Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  7. 07Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
