Avowed

Obsidian Entertainment|Xbox Game Studios|2025|RPG · FPS · Action · Story Rich
8.3122,328 reviews
PCXbox X|S

Avowed marks another role-playing entry from Obsidian Entertainment, and fantasy and action rpg is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Avowed is what happens when Obsidian Entertainment commits fully to role-playing and fantasy and action rpg. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Gunplay tuned for controller and mouse alike, with no aim-assist tax
  • Destruction and bullet penetration modelled on real materials
  • Weapon sandbox with distinct recoil, handling and TTK per class
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours
  • Loot affixes that reshape entire builds

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  3. 03Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  4. 04Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  5. 05Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  6. 06Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  7. 07Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
