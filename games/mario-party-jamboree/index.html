Mario Party Jamboree

NDcube|Nintendo|2024|Family · Multiplayer
8.265,714 reviews
Switch

From NDcube, Mario Party Jamboree is a family-friendly game where party and tabletop drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Mario Party Jamboree is the rare family-friendly release that treats party and tabletop as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Online play is the main event, with dedicated servers and skill-based matchmaking. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Local split-screen and pass-and-play support
  • Assist modes that any player in the room can enable
  • Family-friendly audio and avatar customisation
  • Dedicated server architecture with a 60Hz+ tick rate
  • Full stat tracking with match history and replays
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Custom rules and round modifiers
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches
