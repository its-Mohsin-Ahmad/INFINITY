Call of Duty: Modern Warfare III

Sledgehammer Games|Activision|2023|FPS · Action · Multiplayer
6.949,525 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

In Call of Duty: Modern Warfare III, Sledgehammer Games uses first-person shooter conventions as a foundation and military and tactical shooter as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. First launched in 2023, Call of Duty: Modern Warfare III remains a reference point for first-person shooter design built on military and tactical shooter. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Full-spectrum audio with occlusion and distance falloff
  • Custom loadouts with attachment-level trade-offs
  • Sensitivity, FOV and controller response fully configurable
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Economy rounds with buy-phase decisions
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  3. 03Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  4. 04Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  5. 05Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  6. 06Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  7. 07Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
