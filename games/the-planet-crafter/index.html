The Planet Crafter

Miju Games|Miju Games|2024|Survival · Simulation · Sandbox
8.7106,654 reviews
PC

From Miju Games, The Planet Crafter is a survival game where crafting and city builder drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. The Planet Crafter is what happens when Miju Games commits fully to survival and crafting and city builder. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Co-op scaling that keeps the challenge intact for a full squad
  • Crafting trees spanning tools, shelter, medicine and vehicles
  • Deep inventory and weight management
  • Long-tail progression measured in hundreds of hours
  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Detailed statistics and analytics dashboards
  • Policy and taxation systems with visible effects
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single playersandbox

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Balance throughput against cost, and expand only as fast as logistics can sustain.
  2. 02Once the basics are automatic, increase demand and see which system fails first.
  3. 03Use blueprints for anything you will build more than twice.
  4. 04Watch the analytics panels to find the bottleneck rather than expanding everything at once.
  5. 05Save often, and label saves before large redesigns.
  6. 06Start in sandbox or creative mode to learn the building and automation tools without pressure.
  7. 07Set up the first resource chain end to end before adding a second.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
