Once Human

Starry Studio|NetEase Games|2024|Survival · Open World · Co-op · Horror
7.8116,547 reviews
PCAndroidiOS

First launched in 2024, Once Human remains a reference point for survival design built on post-apocalyptic and crafting. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Once Human takes the survival blueprint and rebuilds it around post-apocalyptic and crafting. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Co-op scaling that keeps the challenge intact for a full squad
  • Deep inventory and weight management
  • Day-night and seasonal cycles that change what is available
  • Seamless traversal with no loading transitions between regions
  • A living map of dynamic events, convoys and emergent encounters
  • Distinct biomes with their own wildlife, weather and factions
  • Improvised gear and radiation systems
  • Co-op progression is fully synced, including achievements and unlocks

single playercampaignmultiplayerco oponline coop

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  3. 03Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  4. 04Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  5. 05Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  6. 06Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  7. 07Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
