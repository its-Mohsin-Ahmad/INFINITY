Naruto Shippuden: Ultimate Ninja Storm 4

CyberConnect2|Bandai Namco Entertainment|2016|Fighting · Action · Adventure
8.236,771 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

First launched in 2016, Naruto Shippuden: Ultimate Ninja Storm 4 remains a reference point for fighting design built on arena shooter. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Released in 2016, Naruto Shippuden: Ultimate Ninja Storm 4 is CyberConnect2's take on modern fighting design with arena shooter at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Ranked ladders with per-character rating
  • Full story mode with hand-animated cinematics
  • Extensive customisation across outfits and colour channels
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Map control, pickups and respawn economy
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control spacing and frame advantage, then punish predictable behaviour with a full combo.
  2. 02Learn one character's normals, one anti-air and one reliable punish.
  3. 03Set a ranked goal per session and stop when you hit it, win or lose.
  4. 04Practise blocking low attacks on reaction, then add delayed tech.
  5. 05Learn the matchup for the character you lose to most before learning a new fighter.
  6. 06Play long sets rather than first-to-one; adaptation is the real skill.
  7. 07Complete the tutorial and the character-specific combo trials before ranked.
