Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope

Ubisoft Milan|Ubisoft|2022|Strategy · Adventure
8.461,551 reviews
Switch

Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope is the rare strategy release that treats tactical rpg and party as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Ubisoft Milan built Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope as a strategy sandbox first and a showcase second, with tactical rpg and party as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Grid-based tactics with height and cover
  • Custom rules and round modifiers

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  3. 03Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  4. 04Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  5. 05Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  6. 06Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  7. 07Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
