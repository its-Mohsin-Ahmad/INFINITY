Assassin's Creed Valhalla

Ubisoft Montreal|Ubisoft|2020|Action · RPG · Open World
8.242,777 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Assassin's Creed Valhalla pairs action systems with historical and action rpg, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Released in 2020, Assassin's Creed Valhalla is Ubisoft Montreal's take on modern action design with historical and action rpg at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Archive-quality research and art direction
  • Real-time combat with stat-driven damage maths

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  3. 03Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  4. 04Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  5. 05Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  6. 06Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  7. 07Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
