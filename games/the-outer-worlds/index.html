The Outer Worlds

Obsidian Entertainment|Private Division|2019|RPG · FPS · Story Rich · Open World
8.442,969 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

In The Outer Worlds, Obsidian Entertainment uses role-playing conventions as a foundation and space rpg and action rpg as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Released in 2019, The Outer Worlds is Obsidian Entertainment's take on modern role-playing design with space rpg and action rpg at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Custom loadouts with attachment-level trade-offs
  • Weapon sandbox with distinct recoil, handling and TTK per class
  • Gunplay tuned for controller and mouse alike, with no aim-assist tax
  • Real-time combat with stat-driven damage maths
  • Cross-platform play and progression across every supported storefront

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  3. 03Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  4. 04Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  5. 05Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  6. 06Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  7. 07Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  8. 08Platform note — 6 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
