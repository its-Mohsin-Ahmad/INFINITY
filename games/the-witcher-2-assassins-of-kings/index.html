The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings

CD Projekt Red|CD Projekt|2011|RPG · Story Rich
8.639,053 reviews
PCXbox One

In The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings, CD Projekt Red uses role-playing conventions as a foundation and fantasy and action rpg as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. CD Projekt Red built The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings as a role-playing sandbox first and a showcase second, with fantasy and action rpg as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Choice systems that alter relationships, not only endings
  • Director commentary and behind-the-scenes features
  • Chapter select and scene replay after completion
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours
  • Real-time combat with stat-driven damage maths

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  3. 03Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  4. 04Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  5. 05Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  6. 06Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  7. 07Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
