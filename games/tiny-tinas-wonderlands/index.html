Tiny Tina's Wonderlands

Gearbox Software|2K|2022|FPS · RPG
7.848,931 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

From Gearbox Software, Tiny Tina's Wonderlands is a first-person shooter game where looter shooter and fantasy drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Gearbox Software built Tiny Tina's Wonderlands as a first-person shooter sandbox first and a showcase second, with looter shooter and fantasy as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Custom loadouts with attachment-level trade-offs
  • Gunplay tuned for controller and mouse alike, with no aim-assist tax
  • Sensitivity, FOV and controller response fully configurable
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Rarity and affix systems with endgame crafting
  • Magic schools with distinct resource economies

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  3. 03Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  4. 04Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  5. 05Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  6. 06Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  7. 07Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
