Phasmophobia

Kinetic Games|Kinetic Games|2020|Horror · Co-op · Multiplayer
8.6124,217 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

First launched in 2020, Phasmophobia remains a reference point for horror design built on monster hunt and party. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. In Phasmophobia, Kinetic Games uses horror conventions as a foundation and monster hunt and party as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Sound design that prioritises spatial cues over cheap jumps
  • Optional one-life mode for the committed
  • Finite resources that make every decision count
  • Designed from the ground up for two to four players
  • Drop-in and drop-out with join-in-progress checkpoints
  • Difficulty that scales with player count
  • Monster AI with sensory perception and stalking behaviour
  • Custom rules and round modifiers

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Manage light, noise and inventory so that every encounter is one you chose to pick.
  2. 02Set brightness deliberately — too dark hides mechanics, too bright kills the atmosphere.
  3. 03Take breaks. Tension degrades decision-making faster than it degrades the game.
  4. 04Note where threats patrol; routes that were dangerous early often become quiet later.
  5. 05Play with headphones. Spatial audio is the primary information channel in this game.
  6. 06Keep your inventory light enough to pick up anything you find.
  7. 07Check every room for resources, but never linger once you have what you need.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
