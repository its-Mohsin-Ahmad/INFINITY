Diablo IV

Blizzard Entertainment|Blizzard Entertainment|2023|RPG · Action · MMORPG
8.4144,172 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Blizzard Entertainment returns with Diablo IV, a role-playing title that leans hard into action rpg and looter shooter. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Diablo IV takes the role-playing blueprint and rebuilds it around action rpg and looter shooter. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Real-time combat with stat-driven damage maths
  • Rarity and affix systems with endgame crafting

single playercampaignmultiplayercross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  3. 03Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  4. 04Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  5. 05Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  6. 06Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  7. 07Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
