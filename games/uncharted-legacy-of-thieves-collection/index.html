Uncharted: Legacy of Thieves Collection

Naughty Dog|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2022|Action · Adventure · Story Rich
8.951,924 reviews
PCPS5

First launched in 2022, Uncharted: Legacy of Thieves Collection remains a reference point for action design built on cover shooter and stealth. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Naughty Dog returns with Uncharted: Legacy of Thieves Collection, a action title that leans hard into cover shooter and stealth. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Squad commands issued on the fly
  • Detection cones, disguises and social stealth

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  3. 03Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  4. 04Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  5. 05Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  6. 06Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  7. 07Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
