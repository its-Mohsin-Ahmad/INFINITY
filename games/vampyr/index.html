Vampyr

Dontnod Entertainment|Focus Home Interactive|2018|RPG · Action · Horror
7.835,963 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

First launched in 2018, Vampyr remains a reference point for role-playing design built on psychological horror and action rpg. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. In Vampyr, Dontnod Entertainment uses role-playing conventions as a foundation and psychological horror and action rpg as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • No-combat stretches that rely on atmosphere
  • Real-time combat with stat-driven damage maths

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Manage light, noise and inventory so that every encounter is one you chose to pick.
  2. 02Note where threats patrol; routes that were dangerous early often become quiet later.
  3. 03Learn the safe room locations and treat them as planning points, not just save points.
  4. 04Conserve ammunition by disabling or avoiding enemies where the layout allows.
  5. 05Set brightness deliberately — too dark hides mechanics, too bright kills the atmosphere.
  6. 06Keep your inventory light enough to pick up anything you find.
  7. 07Check every room for resources, but never linger once you have what you need.
  8. 08Platform note — 4 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
