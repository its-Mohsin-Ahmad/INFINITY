Conan Exiles

Funcom|Funcom|2018|Survival · Open World · Co-op
7.341,644 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

Funcom built Conan Exiles as a survival sandbox first and a showcase second, with crafting and fantasy as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Conan Exiles is what happens when Funcom commits fully to survival and crafting and fantasy. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Base building with structural and defensive layers
  • Day-night and seasonal cycles that change what is available
  • Crafting trees spanning tools, shelter, medicine and vehicles
  • Discovery-led design — landmarks are the map marker
  • Fast travel that unlocks only once a zone has been explored
  • Cinematic camera and photo mode for capture enthusiasts
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours
  • Co-op progression is fully synced, including achievements and unlocks

Modes

single playercampaignmultiplayerco oponline coop

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  3. 03Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  4. 04Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  5. 05Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  6. 06Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  7. 07Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
