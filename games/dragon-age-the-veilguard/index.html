Dragon Age: The Veilguard

BioWare|Electronic Arts|2024|RPG · Action · Story Rich
7.446,431 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Dragon Age: The Veilguard marks another role-playing entry from BioWare, and fantasy and action rpg is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Released in 2024, Dragon Age: The Veilguard is BioWare's take on modern role-playing design with fantasy and action rpg at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours
  • Real-time combat with stat-driven damage maths

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  3. 03Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  4. 04Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  5. 05Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  6. 06Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  7. 07Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
