The Talos Principle 2

Croteam|Devolver Digital|2023|Puzzle · Adventure · Story Rich
8.9109,308 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

From Croteam, The Talos Principle 2 is a puzzle game where sci-fi and physics puzzle drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. In The Talos Principle 2, Croteam uses puzzle conventions as a foundation and sci-fi and physics puzzle as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Zero-fail design that keeps the emphasis on thinking
  • Leaderboards, ghosts and speedrun timers
  • Mechanics introduced one at a time, then combined
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Hardware, ship and station design language
  • Systems-first puzzles with many valid solutions

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  3. 03Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  4. 04Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  5. 05Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  6. 06Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  7. 07Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
