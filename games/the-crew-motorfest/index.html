The Crew Motorfest

Ivory Tower|Ubisoft|2023|Racing · Open World · Multiplayer
7.853,440 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

The Crew Motorfest is what happens when Ivory Tower commits fully to racing and arcade racing. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. In The Crew Motorfest, Ivory Tower uses racing conventions as a foundation and arcade racing as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Rewind, ghost and rival-lap systems for repeat attempts
  • Replay tooling with camera rigs and telemetry overlays
  • Ranked racing with licences and a clean-driving rating
  • Distinct biomes with their own wildlife, weather and factions
  • Fast travel that unlocks only once a zone has been explored
  • A living map of dynamic events, convoys and emergent encounters
  • Ghost races against friends and rivals
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

single playercampaignmultiplayeronline pvprankedcross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  3. 03Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  4. 04Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  5. 05Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  6. 06Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
  7. 07Race shorter events first so mistakes cost fewer laps.
  8. 08Platform note — 5 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
