Cocoon

Geometric Interactive|Annapurna Interactive|2023|Indie · Puzzle · Adventure
8.792,259 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Cocoon pairs independent systems with physics puzzle and escape room, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Cocoon is a independent experience from Geometric Interactive, built around physics puzzle and escape room. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Tight, focused runtime built around one strong idea
  • Regular post-launch updates funded by the community
  • Accessibility options shipped on day one
  • Colour-blind safe palette and audio cue alternatives
  • Hint system that escalates without giving the answer away
  • Leaderboards, ghosts and speedrun timers
  • Systems-first puzzles with many valid solutions
  • Environmental clues that reward observation

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  3. 03Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  4. 04Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  5. 05Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  6. 06Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  7. 07Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
