Genshin Impact

HoYoverse|HoYoverse|2020|RPG · Open World · Action · Multiplayer
8.6157,027 reviews
PCPS5PS4AndroidiOS

First launched in 2020, Genshin Impact remains a reference point for role-playing design built on action rpg and fantasy. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Genshin Impact marks another role-playing entry from HoYoverse, and action rpg and fantasy is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Fast travel that unlocks only once a zone has been explored
  • Distinct biomes with their own wildlife, weather and factions
  • A living map of dynamic events, convoys and emergent encounters
  • Loot affixes that reshape entire builds
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  3. 03Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  4. 04Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  5. 05Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  6. 06Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  7. 07Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
