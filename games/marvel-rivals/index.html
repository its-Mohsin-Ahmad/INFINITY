Marvel Rivals

NetEase Games|NetEase Games|2024|Third Person · Multiplayer · Action
8.4150,684 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

From NetEase Games, Marvel Rivals is a third-person action game where hero shooter and arena shooter drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Marvel Rivals takes the third-person action blueprint and rebuilds it around hero shooter and arena shooter. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Camera tuned for both close-range melee and long-range shooting
  • Cover-driven combat with contextual vaults, slides and dodges
  • Squad and companion commands on a dedicated radial
  • Full stat tracking with match history and replays
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Skill-based matchmaking with transparent rating tiers
  • Role queue and clear team-composition feedback
  • Short match formats built for ranked play

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  3. 03Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  4. 04Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  5. 05Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  6. 06Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  7. 07Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
