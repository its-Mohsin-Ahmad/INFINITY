Gang Beasts

Boneloaf|Boneloaf|2017|Fighting · Co-op · Family
7.673,115 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Gang Beasts takes the fighting blueprint and rebuilds it around party and physics puzzle. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Gang Beasts is a fighting experience from Boneloaf, built around party and physics puzzle. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Online play is the main event, with dedicated servers and skill-based matchmaking. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Frame data and training tools built into the client
  • Rollback netcode with region-aware matchmaking
  • Full story mode with hand-animated cinematics
  • Proximity voice with optional speech-to-text
  • Friends Pass access so a squad only needs one copy
  • Drop-in and drop-out with join-in-progress checkpoints
  • Four-player chaos with rubber-banded stakes
  • Sandbox levels for experimenting with mechanics

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control spacing and frame advantage, then punish predictable behaviour with a full combo.
  2. 02Watch your own replays and count how often you press a button while minus.
  3. 03Complete the tutorial and the character-specific combo trials before ranked.
  4. 04Set a ranked goal per session and stop when you hit it, win or lose.
  5. 05Use training mode's frame data display to check whether moves are safe on block.
  6. 06Play long sets rather than first-to-one; adaptation is the real skill.
  7. 07Learn the matchup for the character you lose to most before learning a new fighter.
