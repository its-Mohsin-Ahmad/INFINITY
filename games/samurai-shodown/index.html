Samurai Shodown

SNK|Athlon Games|2019|Fighting · Multiplayer · Action
7.945,715 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

In Samurai Shodown, SNK uses fighting conventions as a foundation and arena shooter and historical as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Released in 2019, Samurai Shodown is SNK's take on modern fighting design with arena shooter and historical at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Ranked ladders with per-character rating
  • Full story mode with hand-animated cinematics
  • Tutorial and combo challenges for every character archetype
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Dedicated server architecture with a 60Hz+ tick rate
  • Short match formats built for ranked play
  • Archive-quality research and art direction

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control spacing and frame advantage, then punish predictable behaviour with a full combo.
  2. 02Learn one character's normals, one anti-air and one reliable punish.
  3. 03Set a ranked goal per session and stop when you hit it, win or lose.
  4. 04Learn the matchup for the character you lose to most before learning a new fighter.
  5. 05Watch your own replays and count how often you press a button while minus.
  6. 06Use training mode's frame data display to check whether moves are safe on block.
  7. 07Complete the tutorial and the character-specific combo trials before ranked.
