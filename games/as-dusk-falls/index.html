As Dusk Falls

Interior Night|Xbox Game Studios|2022|Adventure · Story Rich · Co-op
8.157,298 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|SXbox One

As Dusk Falls marks another adventure entry from Interior Night, and mystery and party is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. As Dusk Falls is a adventure experience from Interior Night, built around mystery and party. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Choice systems that alter relationships, not only endings
  • Narrative difficulty options for combat-averse players
  • Environmental storytelling layered into every set
  • Clue chains that can be solved out of order
  • Custom rules and round modifiers
