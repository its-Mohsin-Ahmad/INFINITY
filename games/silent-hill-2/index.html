Silent Hill 2

Bloober Team|Konami|2024|Horror · Story Rich · Adventure
8.9150,050 reviews
PCPS5

In Silent Hill 2, Bloober Team uses horror conventions as a foundation and psychological horror and survival horror as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. First launched in 2024, Silent Hill 2 remains a reference point for horror design built on psychological horror and survival horror. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Optional one-life mode for the committed
  • Finite resources that make every decision count
  • Sound design that prioritises spatial cues over cheap jumps
  • Narrative difficulty options for combat-averse players
  • Choice systems that alter relationships, not only endings
  • Performance-captured dialogue with a full ensemble cast
  • No-combat stretches that rely on atmosphere
  • Ammo scarcity and inventory management under pressure

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Manage light, noise and inventory so that every encounter is one you chose to pick.
  2. 02Check every room for resources, but never linger once you have what you need.
  3. 03Keep your inventory light enough to pick up anything you find.
  4. 04Take breaks. Tension degrades decision-making faster than it degrades the game.
  5. 05Note where threats patrol; routes that were dangerous early often become quiet later.
  6. 06Conserve ammunition by disabling or avoiding enemies where the layout allows.
  7. 07Play with headphones. Spatial audio is the primary information channel in this game.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
