Assetto Corsa Competizione

Kunos Simulazioni|505 Games|2019|Racing · Simulation
8.681,751 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Assetto Corsa Competizione marks another racing entry from Kunos Simulazioni, and sim racing and management is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. From Kunos Simulazioni, Assetto Corsa Competizione is a racing game where sim racing and management drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Replay tooling with camera rigs and telemetry overlays
  • Rewind, ghost and rival-lap systems for repeat attempts
  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Detailed statistics and analytics dashboards
  • Force-feedback profiles and telemetry output
  • Financial modelling with real consequences

single playercross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  3. 03Drive a single car for ten laps on a single circuit before changing either variable.
  4. 04Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  5. 05Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
  6. 06Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  7. 07Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  8. 08Platform note — 5 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
