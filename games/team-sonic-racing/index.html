Team Sonic Racing

Sumo Digital|Sega|2019|Racing · Family · Multiplayer
7.441,692 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

Team Sonic Racing pairs racing systems with kart racing and party, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Sumo Digital built Team Sonic Racing as a racing sandbox first and a showcase second, with kart racing and party as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Rewind, ghost and rival-lap systems for repeat attempts
  • Replay tooling with camera rigs and telemetry overlays
  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Generous checkpointing for younger players
  • Simple onboarding that teaches without tutorials
  • Family-friendly audio and avatar customisation
  • Power-up economy balanced for comeback racing
  • Four-player chaos with rubber-banded stakes

single playermultiplayeronline pvplocal cooprankedcross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
  3. 03Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  4. 04Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  5. 05Drive a single car for ten laps on a single circuit before changing either variable.
  6. 06Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  7. 07Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
