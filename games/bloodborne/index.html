Bloodborne

FromSoftware|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2015|RPG · Action · Horror
9.279,105 reviews
PS4

In Bloodborne, FromSoftware uses role-playing conventions as a foundation and soulslike and monster hunt as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Bloodborne pairs role-playing systems with soulslike and monster hunt, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Stamina-driven combat with precise dodge timing
  • Monster AI with sensory perception and stalking behaviour

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Manage light, noise and inventory so that every encounter is one you chose to pick.
  2. 02Keep your inventory light enough to pick up anything you find.
  3. 03Note where threats patrol; routes that were dangerous early often become quiet later.
  4. 04Set brightness deliberately — too dark hides mechanics, too bright kills the atmosphere.
  5. 05Learn the safe room locations and treat them as planning points, not just save points.
  6. 06Conserve ammunition by disabling or avoiding enemies where the layout allows.
  7. 07Check every room for resources, but never linger once you have what you need.
