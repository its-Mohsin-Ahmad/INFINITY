Resident Evil Village

Capcom|Capcom|2021|Horror · Action · FPS · Story Rich
8.6102,686 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitchiOS

Resident Evil Village takes the horror blueprint and rebuilds it around survival horror and monster hunt. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Released in 2021, Resident Evil Village is Capcom's take on modern horror design with survival horror and monster hunt at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Multiple endings driven by in-run choices
  • Finite resources that make every decision count
  • Sound design that prioritises spatial cues over cheap jumps
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Safe rooms with item boxes and save stations
  • Creature design grounded in practical-effect horror

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  3. 03Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  4. 04Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  5. 05Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  6. 06Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  7. 07Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
