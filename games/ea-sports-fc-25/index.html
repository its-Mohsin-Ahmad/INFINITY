EA Sports FC 25

EA Vancouver|Electronic Arts|2024|Sports · Multiplayer · Simulation
7.493,403 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

EA Vancouver returns with EA Sports FC 25, a sports title that leans hard into football and management. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. EA Sports FC 25 marks another sports entry from EA Vancouver, and football and management is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Configurable tactics, formations and set-piece routines
  • Online seasons with promotion and relegation ladders
  • Skill-based timing mechanics that separate the ranks
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Seasonal progression with challenges and rewards
  • Skill-based matchmaking with transparent rating tiers
  • Squad building with chemistry and evolution
  • Staff, morale and logistics across departments

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control tempo, keep shape and take the highest-percentage option available in each phase of play.
  2. 02Play the training drills before your first competitive match so the timing windows are clear.
  3. 03Move online once the fundamentals feel routine, and expect the pace to jump.
  4. 04Study the match stats after each game — possession and pass completion tell the story.
  5. 05Adjust sliders gradually, one value per match, to find your preferred balance.
  6. 06Rotate your squad deliberately; stamina and form matter more than raw ratings.
  7. 07Use the simpler difficulty for one full season to learn the systems without heavy punishment.
