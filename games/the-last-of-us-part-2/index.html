The Last of Us Part II Remastered

Naughty Dog|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2024|Action · Adventure · Story Rich · Horror
9.2113,492 reviews
PS5PC

The Last of Us Part II Remastered is the rare action release that treats post-apocalyptic and stealth as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Released in 2024, The Last of Us Part II Remastered is Naughty Dog's take on modern action design with post-apocalyptic and stealth at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Improvised gear and radiation systems
  • Ghost, assault and mixed approach scoring

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Manage light, noise and inventory so that every encounter is one you chose to pick.
  2. 02Set brightness deliberately — too dark hides mechanics, too bright kills the atmosphere.
  3. 03Check every room for resources, but never linger once you have what you need.
  4. 04Conserve ammunition by disabling or avoiding enemies where the layout allows.
  5. 05Keep your inventory light enough to pick up anything you find.
  6. 06Take breaks. Tension degrades decision-making faster than it degrades the game.
  7. 07Learn the safe room locations and treat them as planning points, not just save points.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
