Deltarune

Toby Fox|Toby Fox|2018|Indie · RPG · Story Rich
9.079,113 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Deltarune marks another independent entry from Toby Fox, and turn-based rpg and bullet hell is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. From Toby Fox, Deltarune is a independent game where turn-based rpg and bullet hell drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Regular post-launch updates funded by the community
  • Hand-crafted art direction with a signature visual identity
  • Accessibility options shipped on day one
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Party composition with elemental weaknesses
  • Pattern reading with tight, forgiving hitboxes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  3. 03Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  4. 04Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  5. 05Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  6. 06Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  7. 07Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
