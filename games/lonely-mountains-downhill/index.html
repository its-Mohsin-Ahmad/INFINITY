Lonely Mountains: Downhill

Megagon Industries|Thunderful Publishing|2019|Sports · Racing · Indie
8.245,914 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

Lonely Mountains: Downhill is what happens when Megagon Industries commits fully to sports and sim racing and physics puzzle. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. First launched in 2019, Lonely Mountains: Downhill remains a reference point for sports design built on sim racing and physics puzzle. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Online seasons with promotion and relegation ladders
  • Broadcast-quality presentation with commentary packages
  • Licensed clubs, athletes and authentic competition structures
  • Ranked racing with licences and a clean-driving rating
  • Tuning suite for gear ratios, suspension, camber and aero
  • Replay tooling with camera rigs and telemetry overlays
  • Tyre temperature and fuel-load strategy across race distance
  • Systems-first puzzles with many valid solutions

Modes

single playerrankedcross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  3. 03Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  4. 04Drive a single car for ten laps on a single circuit before changing either variable.
  5. 05Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
  6. 06Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  7. 07Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
