Stellar Blade

Shift Up|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2024|Action · Adventure · RPG
8.4107,855 reviews
PCPS5

Stellar Blade is what happens when Shift Up commits fully to action and hack & slash and sci-fi. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Stellar Blade takes the action blueprint and rebuilds it around hack & slash and sci-fi. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Combo chains with style-ranking feedback
  • Hardware, ship and station design language

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  3. 03Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  4. 04Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  5. 05Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  6. 06Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  7. 07Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
