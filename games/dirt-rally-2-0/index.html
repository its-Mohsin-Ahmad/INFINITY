DiRT Rally 2.0

Codemasters|Codemasters|2019|Racing · Simulation
8.991,825 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

Codemasters returns with DiRT Rally 2.0, a racing title that leans hard into sim racing and management. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. DiRT Rally 2.0 is what happens when Codemasters commits fully to racing and sim racing and management. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Tuning suite for gear ratios, suspension, camber and aero
  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Replay tooling with camera rigs and telemetry overlays
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Tyre temperature and fuel-load strategy across race distance
  • Financial modelling with real consequences

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
  3. 03Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
  4. 04Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  5. 05Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  6. 06Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  7. 07Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
