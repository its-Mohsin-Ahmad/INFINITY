Riders Republic

Ubisoft Annecy|Ubisoft|2021|Sports · Racing · Open World
7.841,114 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

First launched in 2021, Riders Republic remains a reference point for sports design built on arcade racing and party. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Released in 2021, Riders Republic is Ubisoft Annecy's take on modern sports design with arcade racing and party at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Broadcast-quality presentation with commentary packages
  • Licensed clubs, athletes and authentic competition structures
  • Skill-based timing mechanics that separate the ranks
  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Ranked racing with licences and a clean-driving rating
  • Tuning suite for gear ratios, suspension, camber and aero
  • Ghost races against friends and rivals
  • Custom rules and round modifiers

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  3. 03Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  4. 04Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  5. 05Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  6. 06Race shorter events first so mistakes cost fewer laps.
  7. 07Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
