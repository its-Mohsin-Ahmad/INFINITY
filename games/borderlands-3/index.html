Borderlands 3

Gearbox Software|2K|2019|FPS · RPG · Co-op
8.295,104 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

First launched in 2019, Borderlands 3 remains a reference point for first-person shooter design built on looter shooter. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Borderlands 3 pairs first-person shooter systems with looter shooter, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Gunplay tuned for controller and mouse alike, with no aim-assist tax
  • Sensitivity, FOV and controller response fully configurable
  • Full-spectrum audio with occlusion and distance falloff
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Rarity and affix systems with endgame crafting
  • Co-op progression is fully synced, including achievements and unlocks

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  3. 03Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  4. 04Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  5. 05Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  6. 06Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  7. 07Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
