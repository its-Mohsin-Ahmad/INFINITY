Halo Wars 2

Creative Assembly|Xbox Game Studios|2017|Strategy · Action
8.238,972 reviews
PCXbox One

Halo Wars 2 is a strategy experience from Creative Assembly, built around real-time strategy and sci-fi. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Creative Assembly built Halo Wars 2 as a strategy sandbox first and a showcase second, with real-time strategy and sci-fi as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • Mod support with exposed data files and tooling
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Control groups, hotkeys and formation orders
  • Hardware, ship and station design language

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  3. 03Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  4. 04Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  5. 05Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  6. 06Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  7. 07Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
