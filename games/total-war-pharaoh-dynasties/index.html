Total War: Pharaoh Dynasties

Creative Assembly|Sega|2024|Strategy
7.957,088 reviews
PC

Creative Assembly returns with Total War: Pharaoh Dynasties, a strategy title that leans hard into historical and real-time strategy. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. From Creative Assembly, Total War: Pharaoh Dynasties is a strategy game where historical and real-time strategy drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Archive-quality research and art direction
  • Base building and unit composition in real time
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  3. 03Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  4. 04Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  5. 05Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  6. 06Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  7. 07Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
