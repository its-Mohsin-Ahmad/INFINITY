The First Descendant

Nexon Games|Nexon|2024|Third Person · Co-op
7.5105,189 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

In The First Descendant, Nexon Games uses third-person action conventions as a foundation and looter shooter and sci-fi as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. The First Descendant is a third-person action experience from Nexon Games, built around looter shooter and sci-fi. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Cover-driven combat with contextual vaults, slides and dodges
  • Gear progression that changes the visual loadout as well as stats
  • Camera tuned for both close-range melee and long-range shooting
  • Difficulty that scales with player count
  • Friends Pass access so a squad only needs one copy
  • Designed from the ground up for two to four players
  • Build-defining weapon and gear sets
  • Hardware, ship and station design language

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  3. 03Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  4. 04Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  5. 05Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  6. 06Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  7. 07Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
