Fatal Frame: Mask of the Lunar Eclipse

Koei Tecmo|Koei Tecmo|2023|Horror · Adventure · Story Rich
7.859,240 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Fatal Frame: Mask of the Lunar Eclipse is the rare horror release that treats psychological horror and detective as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Koei Tecmo returns with Fatal Frame: Mask of the Lunar Eclipse, a horror title that leans hard into psychological horror and detective. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Persistent threats that learn and reposition
  • Sound design that prioritises spatial cues over cheap jumps
  • Multiple endings driven by in-run choices
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Unreliable narration and shifting environments
  • Observation and interrogation minigames

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Manage light, noise and inventory so that every encounter is one you chose to pick.
  2. 02Play with headphones. Spatial audio is the primary information channel in this game.
  3. 03Set brightness deliberately — too dark hides mechanics, too bright kills the atmosphere.
  4. 04Conserve ammunition by disabling or avoiding enemies where the layout allows.
  5. 05Note where threats patrol; routes that were dangerous early often become quiet later.
  6. 06Learn the safe room locations and treat them as planning points, not just save points.
  7. 07Keep your inventory light enough to pick up anything you find.
