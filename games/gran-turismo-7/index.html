Gran Turismo 7

Polyphony Digital|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2022|Racing · Simulation · Sports
8.6102,094 reviews
PS5PS4

Gran Turismo 7 is the rare racing release that treats sim racing and management as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Gran Turismo 7 is what happens when Polyphony Digital commits fully to racing and sim racing and management. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Ranked racing with licences and a clean-driving rating
  • Dynamic time of day and weather across the full calendar
  • Replay tooling with camera rigs and telemetry overlays
  • Dynamic events and disasters that force replanning
  • Detailed statistics and analytics dashboards
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Tyre temperature and fuel-load strategy across race distance
  • Financial modelling with real consequences

Modes

single playerranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
  3. 03Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
  4. 04Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  5. 05Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  6. 06Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  7. 07Drive a single car for ten laps on a single circuit before changing either variable.
