Forza Horizon 5

Playground Games|Xbox Game Studios|2021|Racing · Open World · Multiplayer
9.2141,762 reviews
PCXbox X|SXbox One

Released in 2021, Forza Horizon 5 is Playground Games's take on modern racing design with arcade racing at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Forza Horizon 5 pairs racing systems with arcade racing, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Rewind, ghost and rival-lap systems for repeat attempts
  • Dynamic time of day and weather across the full calendar
  • Discovery-led design — landmarks are the map marker
  • Seamless traversal with no loading transitions between regions
  • Distinct biomes with their own wildlife, weather and factions
  • Ghost races against friends and rivals
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

single playercampaignmultiplayeronline pvpranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  3. 03Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  4. 04Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
  5. 05Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  6. 06Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
  7. 07Drive a single car for ten laps on a single circuit before changing either variable.
