Kirby and the Forgotten Land

HAL Laboratory|Nintendo|2022|Family · Co-op
8.666,829 reviews
Switch

Kirby and the Forgotten Land is what happens when HAL Laboratory commits fully to family-friendly and platformer and party. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Kirby and the Forgotten Land is a family-friendly experience from HAL Laboratory, built around platformer and party. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Online play is the main event, with dedicated servers and skill-based matchmaking. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Simple onboarding that teaches without tutorials
  • Assist modes that any player in the room can enable
  • Generous checkpointing for younger players
  • Difficulty that scales with player count
  • Drop-in and drop-out with join-in-progress checkpoints
  • Designed from the ground up for two to four players
  • Precision movement with coyote time and air control
  • Four-player chaos with rubber-banded stakes
