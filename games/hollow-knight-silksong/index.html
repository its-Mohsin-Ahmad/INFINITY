Hollow Knight: Silksong

Team Cherry|Team Cherry|2025|Indie · Action · Adventure
9.2179,300 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Hollow Knight: Silksong is a independent experience from Team Cherry, built around platformer and metroidvania. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Team Cherry built Hollow Knight: Silksong as a independent sandbox first and a showcase second, with platformer and metroidvania as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Regular post-launch updates funded by the community
  • Hand-crafted art direction with a signature visual identity
  • Original score with adaptive audio systems
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Collectibles and time trials per stage
  • Hand-drawn map with player-placed pins

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  3. 03Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  4. 04Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  5. 05Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  6. 06Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  7. 07Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
