Monopoly GO!

Scopely|Scopely|2023|Family
7.4100,757 reviews
AndroidiOS

Monopoly GO! is what happens when Scopely commits fully to family-friendly and tabletop and idle. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Scopely built Monopoly GO! as a family-friendly sandbox first and a showcase second, with tabletop and idle as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Local split-screen co-op is supported on top of the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Assist modes that any player in the room can enable
  • Cross-generation play across living-room platforms
  • Family-friendly audio and avatar customisation
  • Prestige loops with permanent multipliers
  • Photo, replay or capture tooling built in
  • Performance and quality modes on current-generation hardware

local coop

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Keep the group laughing while quietly engineering the round-ending play.
  2. 02Screenshot the results screen. You will want them later.
  3. 03Keep sessions to ninety minutes or less; comedy has diminishing returns.
  4. 04Rotate hosts so everyone keeps their progression.
  5. 05Play the tutorial stage with everyone in the room so nobody is learning alone.
  6. 06Use voice or proximity chat — these games are built on communication.
  7. 07Assign roles based on what each player enjoys, not on who is objectively best.
