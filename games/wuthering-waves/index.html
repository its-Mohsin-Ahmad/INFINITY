Wuthering Waves

Kuro Games|Kuro Games|2024|Action · RPG · Open World
8.3135,185 reviews
PCPS5AndroidiOS

Released in 2024, Wuthering Waves is Kuro Games's take on modern action design with action rpg and post-apocalyptic at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Kuro Games returns with Wuthering Waves, a action title that leans hard into action rpg and post-apocalyptic. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Loot affixes that reshape entire builds
  • Scavenging in ruined landscapes under faction pressure

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  3. 03Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  4. 04Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  5. 05Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  6. 06Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  7. 07Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
