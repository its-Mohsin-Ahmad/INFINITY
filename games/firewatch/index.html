Firewatch

Campo Santo|Panic|2016|Adventure · Story Rich
8.248,563 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Firewatch pairs adventure systems with mystery and narrative adventure, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Released in 2016, Firewatch is Campo Santo's take on modern adventure design with mystery and narrative adventure at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Performance-captured dialogue with a full ensemble cast
  • Chapter select and scene replay after completion
  • Director commentary and behind-the-scenes features
  • Red herrings that respect player intelligence
  • Diegetic UI and in-world documentation

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  3. 03Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  4. 04Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  5. 05Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  6. 06Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  7. 07Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
