The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom

Nintendo EPD|Nintendo|2023|Adventure · Open World · RPG
9.6146,746 reviews
Switch

First launched in 2023, The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom remains a reference point for adventure design built on fantasy and physics puzzle. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. From Nintendo EPD, The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom is a adventure game where fantasy and physics puzzle drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Distinct biomes with their own wildlife, weather and factions
  • Cinematic camera and photo mode for capture enthusiasts
  • Discovery-led design — landmarks are the map marker
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours
  • Sandbox levels for experimenting with mechanics

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  3. 03Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  4. 04Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  5. 05Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  6. 06Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  7. 07Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
