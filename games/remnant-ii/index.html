Remnant II

Gunfire Games|Gearbox Publishing|2023|Third Person · Action · Co-op
8.261,982 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Remnant II pairs third-person action systems with roguelike and soulslike, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Remnant II is the rare third-person action release that treats roguelike and soulslike as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Cover-driven combat with contextual vaults, slides and dodges
  • Cinematic camera work that never takes control away
  • Squad and companion commands on a dedicated radial
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Procedurally arranged runs built from hand-authored templates
  • Stamina-driven combat with precise dodge timing

single playermultiplayerco oponline coop

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  3. 03Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  4. 04Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  5. 05Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  6. 06Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  7. 07Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
