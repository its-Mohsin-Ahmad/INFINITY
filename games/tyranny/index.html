Tyranny

Obsidian Entertainment|Paradox Interactive|2016|RPG · Strategy · Story Rich
8.046,000 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

From Obsidian Entertainment, Tyranny is a role-playing game where turn-based rpg and fantasy drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Tyranny takes the role-playing blueprint and rebuilds it around turn-based rpg and fantasy. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Mod support with exposed data files and tooling
  • Turn-order manipulation and status stacking
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  3. 03Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  4. 04Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  5. 05Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  6. 06Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  7. 07Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
