South of Midnight

Compulsion Games|Xbox Game Studios|2025|Action · Adventure · Story Rich
8.073,260 reviews
PCXbox X|S

In South of Midnight, Compulsion Games uses action conventions as a foundation and fantasy and narrative adventure as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. South of Midnight is what happens when Compulsion Games commits fully to action and fantasy and narrative adventure. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours
  • Diegetic UI and in-world documentation

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  3. 03Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  4. 04Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  5. 05Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  6. 06Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  7. 07Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
