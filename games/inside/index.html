Inside

Playdead|Playdead|2016|Indie · Adventure · Puzzle
9.079,695 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

First launched in 2016, Inside remains a reference point for independent design built on platformer and psychological horror. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Playdead returns with Inside, a independent title that leans hard into platformer and psychological horror. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Accessibility options shipped on day one
  • Original score with adaptive audio systems
  • Full controller and Steam Deck verification
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Precision movement with coyote time and air control
  • No-combat stretches that rely on atmosphere

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  3. 03Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  4. 04Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  5. 05Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  6. 06Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  7. 07Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
