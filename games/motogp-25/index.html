MotoGP 25

Milestone|Milestone|2025|Racing · Simulation · Sports
8.079,260 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Milestone returns with MotoGP 25, a racing title that leans hard into sim racing and management. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. MotoGP 25 is the rare racing release that treats sim racing and management as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Rewind, ghost and rival-lap systems for repeat attempts
  • Ranked racing with licences and a clean-driving rating
  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Tyre temperature and fuel-load strategy across race distance
  • Staff, morale and logistics across departments

single playerrankedcross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  3. 03Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  4. 04Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  5. 05Race shorter events first so mistakes cost fewer laps.
  6. 06Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
  7. 07Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
