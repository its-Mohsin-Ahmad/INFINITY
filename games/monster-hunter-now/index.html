Monster Hunter Now

Niantic|Niantic|2023|Action · RPG · Multiplayer
7.768,860 reviews
AndroidiOS

Monster Hunter Now is the rare action release that treats monster hunt and action rpg as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Monster Hunter Now is a action experience from Niantic, built around monster hunt and action rpg. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Monster AI with sensory perception and stalking behaviour
  • Loot affixes that reshape entire builds
