Mario & Luigi: Brothership

Acquire|Nintendo|2024|RPG · Adventure · Family
7.447,777 reviews
Switch

Mario & Luigi: Brothership takes the role-playing blueprint and rebuilds it around turn-based rpg and party. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Mario & Luigi: Brothership is the rare role-playing release that treats turn-based rpg and party as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Party composition with elemental weaknesses
  • Four-player chaos with rubber-banded stakes
