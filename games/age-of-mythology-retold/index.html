Age of Mythology: Retold

World's Edge|Xbox Game Studios|2024|Strategy · Simulation
8.555,912 reviews
PCXbox X|S

In Age of Mythology: Retold, World's Edge uses strategy conventions as a foundation and real-time strategy and fantasy as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Age of Mythology: Retold pairs strategy systems with real-time strategy and fantasy, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • Long-tail progression measured in hundreds of hours
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Control groups, hotkeys and formation orders
  • Magic schools with distinct resource economies

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  3. 03Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  4. 04Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  5. 05Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  6. 06Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  7. 07Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
