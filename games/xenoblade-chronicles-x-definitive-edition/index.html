Xenoblade Chronicles X: Definitive Edition

Monolith Soft|Nintendo|2025|RPG · Open World
8.695,926 reviews
Switch

Monolith Soft built Xenoblade Chronicles X: Definitive Edition as a role-playing sandbox first and a showcase second, with sci-fi and space rpg as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Xenoblade Chronicles X: Definitive Edition is a role-playing experience from Monolith Soft, built around sci-fi and space rpg. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Cinematic camera and photo mode for capture enthusiasts
  • A living map of dynamic events, convoys and emergent encounters
  • Distinct biomes with their own wildlife, weather and factions
  • Hardware, ship and station design language
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  3. 03Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  4. 04Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  5. 05Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  6. 06Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  7. 07Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
