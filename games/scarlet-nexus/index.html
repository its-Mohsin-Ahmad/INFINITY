Scarlet Nexus

Bandai Namco Studios|Bandai Namco Entertainment|2021|Action · RPG · Story Rich
8.048,162 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Bandai Namco Studios built Scarlet Nexus as a action sandbox first and a showcase second, with hack & slash and sci-fi as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. In Scarlet Nexus, Bandai Namco Studios uses action conventions as a foundation and hack & slash and sci-fi as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Mid-combo weapon and ability switching
  • Hardware, ship and station design language

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  3. 03Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  4. 04Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  5. 05Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  6. 06Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  7. 07Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  8. 08Platform note — 5 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
