Homeworld 3

Blackbird Interactive|Gearbox Publishing|2024|Strategy
6.952,985 reviews
PC

Homeworld 3 takes the strategy blueprint and rebuilds it around space sim and real-time strategy. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. In Homeworld 3, Blackbird Interactive uses strategy conventions as a foundation and space sim and real-time strategy as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Mod support with exposed data files and tooling
  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • System-scale maps with orbital mechanics
  • Control groups, hotkeys and formation orders
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  3. 03Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  4. 04Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  5. 05Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  6. 06Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  7. 07Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
