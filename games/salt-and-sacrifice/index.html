Salt and Sacrifice

Ska Studios|Ska Studios|2022|RPG · Action · Co-op
7.441,928 reviews
PCPS5PS4

Salt and Sacrifice takes the role-playing blueprint and rebuilds it around metroidvania and soulslike. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Salt and Sacrifice is the rare role-playing release that treats metroidvania and soulslike as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Hand-drawn map with player-placed pins
  • Stamina-driven combat with precise dodge timing
