Borderlands 4

Gearbox Software|2K|2025|FPS · RPG · Co-op
8.5161,615 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Gearbox Software returns with Borderlands 4, a first-person shooter title that leans hard into looter shooter and sci-fi. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Borderlands 4 pairs first-person shooter systems with looter shooter and sci-fi, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Weapon sandbox with distinct recoil, handling and TTK per class
  • Custom loadouts with attachment-level trade-offs
  • Full-spectrum audio with occlusion and distance falloff
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Rarity and affix systems with endgame crafting
  • Speculative technology with in-world justification

Modes

single playercampaignmultiplayerco oponline coop

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  3. 03Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  4. 04Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  5. 05Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  6. 06Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  7. 07Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
