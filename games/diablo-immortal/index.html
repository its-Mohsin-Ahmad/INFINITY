Diablo Immortal

Blizzard Entertainment|Blizzard Entertainment|2022|RPG · Action · MMORPG
6.967,648 reviews
PCAndroidiOS

First launched in 2022, Diablo Immortal remains a reference point for role-playing design built on looter shooter and action rpg. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Released in 2022, Diablo Immortal is Blizzard Entertainment's take on modern role-playing design with looter shooter and action rpg at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Rarity and affix systems with endgame crafting
  • Real-time combat with stat-driven damage maths

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  3. 03Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  4. 04Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  5. 05Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  6. 06Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  7. 07Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
