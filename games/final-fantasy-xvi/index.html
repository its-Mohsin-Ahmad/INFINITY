Final Fantasy XVI

Square Enix|Square Enix|2023|RPG · Action · Story Rich
8.5113,648 reviews
PCPS5

From Square Enix, Final Fantasy XVI is a role-playing game where action rpg and fantasy drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. First launched in 2023, Final Fantasy XVI remains a reference point for role-playing design built on action rpg and fantasy. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Loot affixes that reshape entire builds
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  3. 03Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  4. 04Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  5. 05Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  6. 06Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  7. 07Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
