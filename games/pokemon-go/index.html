Pokemon GO

Niantic|Niantic|2016|Adventure · Family · Multiplayer
7.465,105 reviews
AndroidiOS

Niantic returns with Pokemon GO, a adventure title that leans hard into monster hunt and narrative adventure. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. In Pokemon GO, Niantic uses adventure conventions as a foundation and monster hunt and narrative adventure as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Cross-generation play across living-room platforms
  • Local split-screen and pass-and-play support
  • Generous checkpointing for younger players
  • Creature design grounded in practical-effect horror
  • Exploration-first pacing with no fail states

  1. 01Objective — Keep the group laughing while quietly engineering the round-ending play.

  3. 03Rotate hosts so everyone keeps their progression.

  5. 05Screenshot the results screen. You will want them later.
  6. 06Try one full round with default rules before adding modifiers.
