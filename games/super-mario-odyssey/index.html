Super Mario Odyssey

Nintendo EPD|Nintendo|2017|Adventure · Family
10.0101,871 reviews
Switch

Super Mario Odyssey is the rare adventure release that treats platformer and party as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Super Mario Odyssey marks another adventure entry from Nintendo EPD, and platformer and party is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Cross-generation play across living-room platforms
  • Family-friendly audio and avatar customisation
  • Generous checkpointing for younger players
  • Precision movement with coyote time and air control
  • Custom rules and round modifiers
