Dark Souls: Remastered

FromSoftware|Bandai Namco Entertainment|2018|RPG · Action · Horror
8.956,279 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

Dark Souls: Remastered pairs role-playing systems with soulslike and fantasy, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. From FromSoftware, Dark Souls: Remastered is a role-playing game where soulslike and fantasy drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Lost-currency retrieval and shortcut-driven level design
  • Magic schools with distinct resource economies

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Manage light, noise and inventory so that every encounter is one you chose to pick.
  2. 02Play with headphones. Spatial audio is the primary information channel in this game.
  3. 03Learn the safe room locations and treat them as planning points, not just save points.
  4. 04Set brightness deliberately — too dark hides mechanics, too bright kills the atmosphere.
  5. 05Keep your inventory light enough to pick up anything you find.
  6. 06Check every room for resources, but never linger once you have what you need.
  7. 07Take breaks. Tension degrades decision-making faster than it degrades the game.
  8. 08Platform note — 4 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
