MultiVersus

Player First Games|Warner Bros. Games|2024|Fighting · Multiplayer · Family
7.694,907 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

MultiVersus pairs fighting systems with arena shooter and party, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. MultiVersus is what happens when Player First Games commits fully to fighting and arena shooter and party. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Online play is the main event, with dedicated servers and skill-based matchmaking. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Full story mode with hand-animated cinematics
  • Frame data and training tools built into the client
  • Rollback netcode with region-aware matchmaking
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Seasonal progression with challenges and rewards
  • Short match formats built for ranked play
  • Custom rules and round modifiers

multiplayeronline pvplocal cooprankedcross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control spacing and frame advantage, then punish predictable behaviour with a full combo.
  2. 02Use training mode's frame data display to check whether moves are safe on block.
  3. 03Play long sets rather than first-to-one; adaptation is the real skill.
  4. 04Set a ranked goal per session and stop when you hit it, win or lose.
  5. 05Learn the matchup for the character you lose to most before learning a new fighter.
  6. 06Complete the tutorial and the character-specific combo trials before ranked.
  7. 07Learn one character's normals, one anti-air and one reliable punish.
