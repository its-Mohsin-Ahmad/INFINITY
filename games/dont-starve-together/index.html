Don't Starve Together

Klei Entertainment|Klei Entertainment|2016|Survival · Co-op · Sandbox
8.9132,186 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

Don't Starve Together is a survival experience from Klei Entertainment, built around crafting and party. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Released in 2016, Don't Starve Together is Klei Entertainment's take on modern survival design with crafting and party at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Online play is the main event, with dedicated servers and skill-based matchmaking. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Crafting trees spanning tools, shelter, medicine and vehicles
  • Deep inventory and weight management
  • Day-night and seasonal cycles that change what is available
  • Difficulty that scales with player count
  • Friends Pass access so a squad only needs one copy
  • Asymmetric roles that demand genuine coordination
  • Four-player chaos with rubber-banded stakes
  • Co-op progression is fully synced, including achievements and unlocks

Modes

multiplayerco oponline coopsandboxcross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  3. 03Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  4. 04Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  5. 05Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  6. 06Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  7. 07Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
