Luigi's Mansion 3

Next Level Games|Nintendo|2019|Adventure · Family · Puzzle
8.653,840 reviews
Switch

From Next Level Games, Luigi's Mansion 3 is a adventure game where physics puzzle and monster hunt drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Released in 2019, Luigi's Mansion 3 is Next Level Games's take on modern adventure design with physics puzzle and monster hunt at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Family-friendly audio and avatar customisation
  • Local split-screen and pass-and-play support
  • Generous checkpointing for younger players
  • Systems-first puzzles with many valid solutions
  • Creature design grounded in practical-effect horror
