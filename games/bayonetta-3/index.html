Bayonetta 3

PlatinumGames|Nintendo|2022|Action · Adventure
8.561,971 reviews
Switch

In Bayonetta 3, PlatinumGames uses action conventions as a foundation and hack & slash and fantasy as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. From PlatinumGames, Bayonetta 3 is a action game where hack & slash and fantasy drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Combo chains with style-ranking feedback
  • Magic schools with distinct resource economies

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  3. 03Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  4. 04Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  5. 05Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  6. 06Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  7. 07Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
