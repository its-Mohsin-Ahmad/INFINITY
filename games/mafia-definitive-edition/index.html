Mafia: Definitive Edition

Hangar 13|2K|2020|Action · Adventure · Open World · Story Rich
8.254,123 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

Mafia: Definitive Edition pairs action systems with cover shooter and historical, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Released in 2020, Mafia: Definitive Edition is Hangar 13's take on modern action design with cover shooter and historical at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Squad commands issued on the fly
  • Historically grounded settings, equipment and tactics

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  3. 03Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  4. 04Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  5. 05Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  6. 06Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  7. 07Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
