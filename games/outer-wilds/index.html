Outer Wilds

Mobius Digital|Annapurna Interactive|2019|Adventure · Puzzle
9.3139,766 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Outer Wilds takes the adventure blueprint and rebuilds it around mystery and sci-fi. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Outer Wilds is a adventure experience from Mobius Digital, built around mystery and sci-fi. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Level editor with sharing and curated picks
  • Mechanics introduced one at a time, then combined
  • Colour-blind safe palette and audio cue alternatives
  • Clue chains that can be solved out of order
  • Speculative technology with in-world justification

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.

  6. 06Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
