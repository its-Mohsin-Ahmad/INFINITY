Scrap Mechanic

Axolot Games|Axolot Games|2016|Sandbox · Survival · Co-op
8.478,369 reviews
PC

Scrap Mechanic is a sandbox experience from Axolot Games, built around sandbox craft and physics puzzle. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Scrap Mechanic is the rare sandbox release that treats sandbox craft and physics puzzle as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Online play is the main event, with dedicated servers and skill-based matchmaking. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Logic, wiring and scripting systems for advanced creators
  • Progression-free play where creativity is the reward
  • Moderation and reporting tooling for shared spaces
  • Crafting trees spanning tools, shelter, medicine and vehicles
  • Environmental threats ranging from weather to predators
  • Base building with structural and defensive layers
  • Creative and survival modes with world seeds
  • Systems-first puzzles with many valid solutions

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  3. 03Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  4. 04Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  5. 05Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  6. 06Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  7. 07Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
