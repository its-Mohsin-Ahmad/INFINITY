Legends of Runeterra

Riot Games|Riot Games|2020|Strategy · Multiplayer
8.374,698 reviews
PCAndroidiOS

Legends of Runeterra takes the strategy blueprint and rebuilds it around card battler and deckbuilder. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. From Riot Games, Legends of Runeterra is a strategy game where card battler and deckbuilder drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Full stat tracking with match history and replays
  • Dedicated server architecture with a 60Hz+ tick rate
  • Draft, build and counter enemy archetypes
  • Ascension-style modifiers for repeat runs

Modes

single playermultiplayeronline pvpranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  3. 03Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  4. 04Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  5. 05Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  6. 06Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  7. 07Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
