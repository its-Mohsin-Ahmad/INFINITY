WARNO

Eugen Systems|Eugen Systems|2024|Strategy
7.862,511 reviews
PC

Released in 2024, WARNO is Eugen Systems's take on modern strategy design with military sim and real-time strategy at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. WARNO marks another strategy entry from Eugen Systems, and military sim and real-time strategy is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Doctrines and tech trees with genuine opportunity costs
  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Authentic ballistics, suppression and stamina
  • Control groups, hotkeys and formation orders
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  3. 03Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  4. 04Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  5. 05Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  6. 06Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  7. 07Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
