inZOI

inZOI Studio|Krafton|2025|Simulation · Sandbox
7.6126,430 reviews
PC

inZOI takes the simulation blueprint and rebuilds it around life sim and management. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. inZOI pairs simulation systems with life sim and management, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Long-tail progression measured in hundreds of hours
  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Cross-platform worlds with host controls
  • Creative tools with sharing and remix support
  • Community showcase feeds and featured builds
  • Needs, relationships and career simulation
  • Financial modelling with real consequences

Modes

single playersandbox

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Balance throughput against cost, and expand only as fast as logistics can sustain.
  2. 02Keep spare capacity in power, storage and transport so growth is never gated by infrastructure.
  3. 03Save often, and label saves before large redesigns.
  4. 04Set up the first resource chain end to end before adding a second.
  5. 05Play one long session with a single goal in mind; simulation games reward clear objectives.
  6. 06Start in sandbox or creative mode to learn the building and automation tools without pressure.
  7. 07Watch the analytics panels to find the bottleneck rather than expanding everything at once.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
