Necesse

Mads Skovgaard|Mads Skovgaard|2023|Survival · Sandbox · Co-op
8.352,544 reviews
PC

Necesse is the rare survival release that treats crafting and management as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Necesse is what happens when Mads Skovgaard commits fully to survival and crafting and management. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Online play is the main event, with dedicated servers and skill-based matchmaking. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Environmental threats ranging from weather to predators
  • Crafting trees spanning tools, shelter, medicine and vehicles
  • Deep inventory and weight management
  • Moderation and reporting tooling for shared spaces
  • Community showcase feeds and featured builds
  • Creative tools with sharing and remix support
  • Financial modelling with real consequences
  • Co-op progression is fully synced, including achievements and unlocks

Modes

multiplayerco oponline coopsandbox

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  3. 03Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  4. 04Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  5. 05Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  6. 06Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  7. 07Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
