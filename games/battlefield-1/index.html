Battlefield 1

DICE|Electronic Arts|2016|FPS · Multiplayer · Action · Story Rich
8.946,468 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

Battlefield 1 takes the first-person shooter blueprint and rebuilds it around military and historical. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. DICE returns with Battlefield 1, a first-person shooter title that leans hard into military and historical. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Sensitivity, FOV and controller response fully configurable
  • Weapon sandbox with distinct recoil, handling and TTK per class
  • Gunplay tuned for controller and mouse alike, with no aim-assist tax
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Skill-based matchmaking with transparent rating tiers
  • Seasonal progression with challenges and rewards
  • Historically grounded settings, equipment and tactics
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

Modes

single playercampaignmultiplayeronline pvpranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  3. 03Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  4. 04Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  5. 05Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  6. 06Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  7. 07Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
