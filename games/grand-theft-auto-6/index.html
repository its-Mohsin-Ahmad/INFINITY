Grand Theft Auto VI

Rockstar Games|Rockstar Games|2026|Action · Open World · Third Person · Story Rich
7.5129,784 reviews
PS5Xbox X|S

Released in 2026, Grand Theft Auto VI is Rockstar Games's take on modern action design with heist and mystery at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Rockstar Games built Grand Theft Auto VI as a action sandbox first and a showcase second, with heist and mystery as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is available to wishlist on INFINITY ahead of launch, with platform pre-load details to follow.

Highlights

  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Seamless traversal with no loading transitions between regions
  • Cinematic camera and photo mode for capture enthusiasts
  • Distinct biomes with their own wildlife, weather and factions
  • Multi-stage operations with planning, infiltration and escape
  • Red herrings that respect player intelligence

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  3. 03Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  4. 04Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  5. 05Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  6. 06Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  7. 07Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
