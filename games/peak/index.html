PEAK

Aggro Crab|Aggro Crab|2025|Co-op · Survival · Adventure
8.8146,686 reviews
PC

Aggro Crab returns with PEAK, a co-operative title that leans hard into physics puzzle. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. PEAK takes the co-operative blueprint and rebuilds it around physics puzzle. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Difficulty that scales with player count
  • Proximity voice with optional speech-to-text
  • Asymmetric roles that demand genuine coordination
  • Environmental threats ranging from weather to predators
  • Crafting trees spanning tools, shelter, medicine and vehicles
  • Base building with structural and defensive layers
  • Sandbox levels for experimenting with mechanics
  • Co-op progression is fully synced, including achievements and unlocks

Modes

single playercampaignmultiplayerco oponline coop

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Establish shelter and a resource loop before pushing into higher-risk regions.
  2. 02Store duplicate resources — the inventory you lose on death is the one that hurts.
  3. 03Prioritise food, water and shelter in the first hour; everything else is optional.
  4. 04Play with one friend early; co-op scales difficulty but doubles carrying capacity.
  5. 05Track day-night and weather cycles; both change which threats are active.
  6. 06Bring a dedicated combat loadout when gathering, and a gathering loadout when fighting.
  7. 07Craft the basic tool set before exploring further than you can walk back.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
