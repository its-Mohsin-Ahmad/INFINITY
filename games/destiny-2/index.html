Destiny 2

Bungie|Bungie|2017|FPS · RPG · Multiplayer · MMORPG
8.4109,605 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Bungie built Destiny 2 as a first-person shooter sandbox first and a showcase second, with looter shooter and sci-fi as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Bungie returns with Destiny 2, a first-person shooter title that leans hard into looter shooter and sci-fi. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Sensitivity, FOV and controller response fully configurable
  • Weapon sandbox with distinct recoil, handling and TTK per class
  • Custom loadouts with attachment-level trade-offs
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Build-defining weapon and gear sets
  • Speculative technology with in-world justification

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  3. 03Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  4. 04Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  5. 05Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  6. 06Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  7. 07Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
