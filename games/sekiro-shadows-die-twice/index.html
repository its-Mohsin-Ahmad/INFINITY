Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice

FromSoftware|Activision|2019|Action · Adventure · Story Rich
9.070,258 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

From FromSoftware, Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice is a action game where soulslike and stealth drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. FromSoftware returns with Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice, a action title that leans hard into soulslike and stealth. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Lost-currency retrieval and shortcut-driven level design
  • Ghost, assault and mixed approach scoring

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  3. 03Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  4. 04Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  5. 05Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  6. 06Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  7. 07Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
