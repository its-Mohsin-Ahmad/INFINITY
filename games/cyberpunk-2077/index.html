Cyberpunk 2077

CD Projekt Red|CD Projekt|2020|RPG · Open World · FPS · Story Rich
9.1145,827 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Released in 2020, Cyberpunk 2077 is CD Projekt Red's take on modern role-playing design with cyberpunk and action rpg at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Cyberpunk 2077 pairs role-playing systems with cyberpunk and action rpg, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Cinematic camera and photo mode for capture enthusiasts
  • Fast travel that unlocks only once a zone has been explored
  • Discovery-led design — landmarks are the map marker
  • Neon-drenched vertical urban environments
  • Loot affixes that reshape entire builds

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  3. 03Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  4. 04Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  5. 05Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  6. 06Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  7. 07Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
