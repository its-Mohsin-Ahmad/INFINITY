Tunic

Andrew Shouldice|Finji|2022|Indie · Adventure · Puzzle · Action
8.663,037 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Tunic is the rare independent release that treats metroidvania and detective as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Tunic marks another independent entry from Andrew Shouldice, and metroidvania and detective is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Hand-crafted art direction with a signature visual identity
  • Regular post-launch updates funded by the community
  • Tight, focused runtime built around one strong idea
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Ability-gated world that loops back on itself
  • Observation and interrogation minigames

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  3. 03Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  4. 04Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  5. 05Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  6. 06Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  7. 07Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
