Lost Ark

Smilegate RPG|Amazon Games|2022|MMORPG · Action · RPG
7.387,366 reviews
PC

Smilegate RPG returns with Lost Ark, a massively multiplayer title that leans hard into action rpg and looter shooter. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Lost Ark is a massively multiplayer experience from Smilegate RPG, built around action rpg and looter shooter. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Persistent world with a genuinely player-driven economy
  • Faction, guild and territory systems with real stakes
  • Deep character progression with respec flexibility
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Real-time combat with stat-driven damage maths
  • Build-defining weapon and gear sets

Modes

single playercampaignmultiplayer

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  3. 03Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  4. 04Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  5. 05Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  6. 06Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  7. 07Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
