Visage

SadSquare Studio|SadSquare Studio|2020|Horror · Adventure · Puzzle
8.242,200 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

Visage is what happens when SadSquare Studio commits fully to horror and psychological horror and escape room. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. SadSquare Studio returns with Visage, a horror title that leans hard into psychological horror and escape room. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Sound design that prioritises spatial cues over cheap jumps
  • Multiple endings driven by in-run choices
  • Persistent threats that learn and reposition
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • No-combat stretches that rely on atmosphere
  • Environmental clues that reward observation

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Manage light, noise and inventory so that every encounter is one you chose to pick.
  2. 02Conserve ammunition by disabling or avoiding enemies where the layout allows.
  3. 03Note where threats patrol; routes that were dangerous early often become quiet later.
  4. 04Take breaks. Tension degrades decision-making faster than it degrades the game.
  5. 05Keep your inventory light enough to pick up anything you find.
  6. 06Check every room for resources, but never linger once you have what you need.
  7. 07Play with headphones. Spatial audio is the primary information channel in this game.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
