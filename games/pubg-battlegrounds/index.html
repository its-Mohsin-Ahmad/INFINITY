PUBG: Battlegrounds

PUBG Corporation|Krafton|2017|Battle Royale · FPS · Multiplayer
8.2123,424 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneAndroidiOS

PUBG: Battlegrounds is a battle royale experience from PUBG Corporation, built around military and extraction. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. PUBG: Battlegrounds pairs battle royale systems with military and extraction, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Ranked progression with placement matches and decay
  • Cross-play with input-based matchmaking pools
  • Squad play with pings, comms and role specialisation
  • Gunplay tuned for controller and mouse alike, with no aim-assist tax
  • Weapon sandbox with distinct recoil, handling and TTK per class
  • Custom loadouts with attachment-level trade-offs
  • Player-market pricing on every extracted item
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  3. 03Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  4. 04Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  5. 05Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  6. 06Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  7. 07Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
