Battle Brothers

Overhype Studios|Overhype Studios|2017|Strategy · RPG
8.451,128 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

Battle Brothers is what happens when Overhype Studios commits fully to strategy and tactical rpg and fantasy. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. In Battle Brothers, Overhype Studios uses strategy conventions as a foundation and tactical rpg and fantasy as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Mod support with exposed data files and tooling
  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Grid-based tactics with height and cover
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  3. 03Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  4. 04Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  5. 05Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  6. 06Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  7. 07Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  8. 08Platform note — 4 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
