Helldivers 2

Arrowhead Game Studios|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2024|Third Person · Co-op · Multiplayer · Action
9.0106,342 reviews
PCPS5

Helldivers 2 is what happens when Arrowhead Game Studios commits fully to third-person action and cover shooter and sci-fi. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Arrowhead Game Studios returns with Helldivers 2, a third-person action title that leans hard into cover shooter and sci-fi. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Photo mode with pose, lens and lighting controls
  • Camera tuned for both close-range melee and long-range shooting
  • Squad and companion commands on a dedicated radial
  • Friends Pass access so a squad only needs one copy
  • Asymmetric roles that demand genuine coordination
  • Difficulty that scales with player count
  • Blind fire, snap cover and flank-happy AI
  • Speculative technology with in-world justification

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  3. 03Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  4. 04Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  5. 05Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  6. 06Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  7. 07Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
