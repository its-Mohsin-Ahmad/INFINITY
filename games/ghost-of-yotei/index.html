Ghost of Yotei

Sucker Punch Productions|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2025|Action · Open World · Story Rich
8.8125,654 reviews
PS5

In Ghost of Yotei, Sucker Punch Productions uses action conventions as a foundation and historical and stealth as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Sucker Punch Productions built Ghost of Yotei as a action sandbox first and a showcase second, with historical and stealth as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Distinct biomes with their own wildlife, weather and factions
  • A living map of dynamic events, convoys and emergent encounters
  • Cinematic camera and photo mode for capture enthusiasts
  • Archive-quality research and art direction
  • Detection cones, disguises and social stealth

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  3. 03Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  4. 04Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  5. 05Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  6. 06Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  7. 07Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
