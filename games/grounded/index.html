Grounded

Obsidian Entertainment|Xbox Game Studios|2022|Survival · Co-op · Adventure
8.4102,686 reviews
PCXbox X|SXbox One

Grounded marks another survival entry from Obsidian Entertainment, and crafting and party is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Obsidian Entertainment built Grounded as a survival sandbox first and a showcase second, with crafting and party as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Deep inventory and weight management
  • Co-op scaling that keeps the challenge intact for a full squad
  • Base building with structural and defensive layers
  • Proximity voice with optional speech-to-text
  • Asymmetric roles that demand genuine coordination
  • Drop-in and drop-out with join-in-progress checkpoints
  • Four-player chaos with rubber-banded stakes
  • Co-op progression is fully synced, including achievements and unlocks

Modes

single playercampaignmultiplayerco oponline coop

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Establish shelter and a resource loop before pushing into higher-risk regions.
  2. 02Play with one friend early; co-op scales difficulty but doubles carrying capacity.
  3. 03Track day-night and weather cycles; both change which threats are active.
  4. 04Bring a dedicated combat loadout when gathering, and a gathering loadout when fighting.
  5. 05Craft the basic tool set before exploring further than you can walk back.
  6. 06Prioritise food, water and shelter in the first hour; everything else is optional.
  7. 07Do not fight every fight. Running is a valid response to a bad engagement.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
