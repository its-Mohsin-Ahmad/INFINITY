XCOM: Chimera Squad

Firaxis Games|2K|2020|Strategy
7.538,941 reviews
PC

XCOM: Chimera Squad is what happens when Firaxis Games commits fully to strategy and tactical rpg and sci-fi. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. XCOM: Chimera Squad is a strategy experience from Firaxis Games, built around tactical rpg and sci-fi. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Doctrines and tech trees with genuine opportunity costs
  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Grid-based tactics with height and cover
  • Speculative technology with in-world justification
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  3. 03Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  4. 04Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  5. 05Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  6. 06Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  7. 07Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
