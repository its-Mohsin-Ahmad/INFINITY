Tekken 7

Bandai Namco Studios|Bandai Namco Entertainment|2017|Fighting · Multiplayer · Action
8.649,237 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

Released in 2017, Tekken 7 is Bandai Namco Studios's take on modern fighting design with arena shooter at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Bandai Namco Studios built Tekken 7 as a fighting sandbox first and a showcase second, with arena shooter as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Rollback netcode with region-aware matchmaking
  • Frame data and training tools built into the client
  • Full story mode with hand-animated cinematics
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Seasonal progression with challenges and rewards
  • Full stat tracking with match history and replays
  • Map control, pickups and respawn economy
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

single playermultiplayeronline pvpranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control spacing and frame advantage, then punish predictable behaviour with a full combo.
  2. 02Set a ranked goal per session and stop when you hit it, win or lose.
  3. 03Learn one character's normals, one anti-air and one reliable punish.
  4. 04Complete the tutorial and the character-specific combo trials before ranked.
  5. 05Learn the matchup for the character you lose to most before learning a new fighter.
  6. 06Practise blocking low attacks on reaction, then add delayed tech.
  7. 07Use training mode's frame data display to check whether moves are safe on block.
