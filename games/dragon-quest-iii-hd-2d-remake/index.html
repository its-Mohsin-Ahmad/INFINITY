Dragon Quest III HD-2D Remake

Artdink|Square Enix|2024|RPG
8.772,972 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|SSwitch

Dragon Quest III HD-2D Remake takes the role-playing blueprint and rebuilds it around fantasy and turn-based rpg. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Artdink built Dragon Quest III HD-2D Remake as a role-playing sandbox first and a showcase second, with fantasy and turn-based rpg as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours
  • Party composition with elemental weaknesses
  • Cross-platform play and progression across every supported storefront
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  3. 03Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  4. 04Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  5. 05Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  6. 06Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  7. 07Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  8. 08Platform note — 4 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
