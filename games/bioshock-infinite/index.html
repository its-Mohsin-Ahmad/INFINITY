BioShock Infinite

Irrational Games|2K|2013|FPS · Story Rich · Action
9.051,495 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

Irrational Games built BioShock Infinite as a first-person shooter sandbox first and a showcase second, with sci-fi and cover shooter as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. BioShock Infinite pairs first-person shooter systems with sci-fi and cover shooter, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Sensitivity, FOV and controller response fully configurable
  • Custom loadouts with attachment-level trade-offs
  • Full-spectrum audio with occlusion and distance falloff
  • Narrative difficulty options for combat-averse players
  • Choice systems that alter relationships, not only endings
  • Environmental storytelling layered into every set
  • Hardware, ship and station design language
  • Blind fire, snap cover and flank-happy AI

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  3. 03Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  4. 04Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  5. 05Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  6. 06Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  7. 07Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
