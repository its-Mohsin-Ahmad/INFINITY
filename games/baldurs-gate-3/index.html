Baldur's Gate 3

Larian Studios|Larian Studios|2023|RPG · Strategy · Story Rich · Co-op
9.6129,964 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Released in 2023, Baldur's Gate 3 is Larian Studios's take on modern role-playing design with turn-based rpg and fantasy at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. From Larian Studios, Baldur's Gate 3 is a role-playing game where turn-based rpg and fantasy drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Mod support with exposed data files and tooling
  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Turn-order manipulation and status stacking
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  3. 03Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  4. 04Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  5. 05Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  6. 06Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  7. 07Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
