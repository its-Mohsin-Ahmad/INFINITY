Black Desert

Pearl Abyss|Pearl Abyss|2017|MMORPG · Action · Open World
7.945,858 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Black Desert is the rare massively multiplayer release that treats action rpg and life sim as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Black Desert is a massively multiplayer experience from Pearl Abyss, built around action rpg and life sim. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Group content from small dungeons to large-format raids
  • Social tooling — emotes, housing, guild halls and banks
  • Faction, guild and territory systems with real stakes
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Real-time combat with stat-driven damage maths
  • Build and interior design tools

single playercampaignmultiplayercross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  3. 03Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  4. 04Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  5. 05Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  6. 06Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  7. 07Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  8. 08Platform note — 5 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
