Paladins

Evil Mojo Games|Hi-Rez Studios|2018|FPS · Multiplayer
7.773,831 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Paladins takes the first-person shooter blueprint and rebuilds it around hero shooter and arena shooter. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Evil Mojo Games built Paladins as a first-person shooter sandbox first and a showcase second, with hero shooter and arena shooter as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Full-spectrum audio with occlusion and distance falloff
  • Sensitivity, FOV and controller response fully configurable
  • Custom loadouts with attachment-level trade-offs
  • Skill-based matchmaking with transparent rating tiers
  • Seasonal progression with challenges and rewards
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Distinct hero kits with hard counters
  • Map control, pickups and respawn economy

single playermultiplayeronline pvprankedcross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  3. 03Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  4. 04Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  5. 05Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  6. 06Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  7. 07Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
