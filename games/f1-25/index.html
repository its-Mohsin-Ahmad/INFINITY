F1 25

Codemasters|Electronic Arts|2025|Racing · Simulation · Sports
8.482,948 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

F1 25 takes the racing blueprint and rebuilds it around sim racing and management. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Codemasters built F1 25 as a racing sandbox first and a showcase second, with sim racing and management as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Dynamic time of day and weather across the full calendar
  • Rewind, ghost and rival-lap systems for repeat attempts
  • Dynamic events and disasters that force replanning
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Detailed statistics and analytics dashboards
  • Tyre temperature and fuel-load strategy across race distance
  • Staff, morale and logistics across departments

Modes

single playerranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  3. 03Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  4. 04Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
  5. 05Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  6. 06Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  7. 07Race shorter events first so mistakes cost fewer laps.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
