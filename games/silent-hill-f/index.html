Silent Hill f

NeoBards Entertainment|Konami|2025|Horror · Story Rich · Adventure
8.176,615 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Released in 2025, Silent Hill f is NeoBards Entertainment's take on modern horror design with psychological horror and historical at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. NeoBards Entertainment built Silent Hill f as a horror sandbox first and a showcase second, with psychological horror and historical as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Multiple endings driven by in-run choices
  • Optional one-life mode for the committed
  • Sound design that prioritises spatial cues over cheap jumps
  • Choice systems that alter relationships, not only endings
  • Narrative difficulty options for combat-averse players
  • Performance-captured dialogue with a full ensemble cast
  • No-combat stretches that rely on atmosphere
  • Historically grounded settings, equipment and tactics

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Manage light, noise and inventory so that every encounter is one you chose to pick.
  2. 02Play with headphones. Spatial audio is the primary information channel in this game.
  3. 03Set brightness deliberately — too dark hides mechanics, too bright kills the atmosphere.
  4. 04Note where threats patrol; routes that were dangerous early often become quiet later.
  5. 05Conserve ammunition by disabling or avoiding enemies where the layout allows.
  6. 06Learn the safe room locations and treat them as planning points, not just save points.
  7. 07Take breaks. Tension degrades decision-making faster than it degrades the game.
