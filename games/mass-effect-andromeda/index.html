Mass Effect: Andromeda

BioWare Montreal|Electronic Arts|2017|RPG · Third Person · Open World
7.332,996 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

Mass Effect: Andromeda marks another role-playing entry from BioWare Montreal, and space rpg and cover shooter is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. From BioWare Montreal, Mass Effect: Andromeda is a role-playing game where space rpg and cover shooter drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Cinematic camera work that never takes control away
  • Cover-driven combat with contextual vaults, slides and dodges
  • Gear progression that changes the visual loadout as well as stats
  • Squad commands issued on the fly
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  3. 03Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  4. 04Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  5. 05Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  6. 06Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  7. 07Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
