Divinity: Original Sin 2

Larian Studios|Larian Studios|2017|RPG · Strategy · Story Rich · Co-op
9.4108,600 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

Divinity: Original Sin 2 pairs role-playing systems with turn-based rpg and fantasy, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Divinity: Original Sin 2 is the rare role-playing release that treats turn-based rpg and fantasy as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • Doctrines and tech trees with genuine opportunity costs
  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Turn-order manipulation and status stacking
  • Magic schools with distinct resource economies

single playercampaignmultiplayerco oponline coopcross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  3. 03Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  4. 04Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  5. 05Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  6. 06Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  7. 07Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  8. 08Platform note — 4 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
