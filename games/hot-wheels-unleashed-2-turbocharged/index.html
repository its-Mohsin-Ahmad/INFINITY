Hot Wheels Unleashed 2: Turbocharged

Milestone|Milestone|2023|Racing · Family · Multiplayer
8.164,704 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Milestone built Hot Wheels Unleashed 2: Turbocharged as a racing sandbox first and a showcase second, with kart racing and party as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Hot Wheels Unleashed 2: Turbocharged takes the racing blueprint and rebuilds it around kart racing and party. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Replay tooling with camera rigs and telemetry overlays
  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Rewind, ghost and rival-lap systems for repeat attempts
  • Generous checkpointing for younger players
  • Cross-generation play across living-room platforms
  • Assist modes that any player in the room can enable
  • Grand Prix cups and time-trial leaderboards
  • Four-player chaos with rubber-banded stakes

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
  3. 03Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  4. 04Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  5. 05Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  6. 06Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
  7. 07Race shorter events first so mistakes cost fewer laps.
