The Finals

Embark Studios|Embark Studios|2023|FPS · Multiplayer · Action
8.3116,666 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

From Embark Studios, The Finals is a first-person shooter game where arena shooter and extraction drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. The Finals is what happens when Embark Studios commits fully to first-person shooter and arena shooter and extraction. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Custom loadouts with attachment-level trade-offs
  • Destruction and bullet penetration modelled on real materials
  • Gunplay tuned for controller and mouse alike, with no aim-assist tax
  • Skill-based matchmaking with transparent rating tiers
  • Full stat tracking with match history and replays
  • Dedicated server architecture with a 60Hz+ tick rate
  • Short match formats built for ranked play
  • Player-market pricing on every extracted item

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  3. 03Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  4. 04Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  5. 05Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  6. 06Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  7. 07Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
