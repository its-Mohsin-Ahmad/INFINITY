Clash Royale

Supercell|Supercell|2016|Strategy · Multiplayer
7.998,358 reviews
AndroidiOS

In Clash Royale, Supercell uses strategy conventions as a foundation and card battler and tower defense as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Clash Royale is a strategy experience from Supercell, built around card battler and tower defense. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Full stat tracking with match history and replays
  • Dedicated server architecture with a 60Hz+ tick rate
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Seasonal card sets with regular balance patches
  • Wave planning with upgrade branching

single playermultiplayeronline pvpranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  3. 03Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  4. 04Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  5. 05Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  6. 06Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  7. 07Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
