EA Sports PGA Tour

EA Tiburon|Electronic Arts|2023|Sports · Simulation
7.454,037 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

In EA Sports PGA Tour, EA Tiburon uses sports conventions as a foundation and golf and management as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. First launched in 2023, EA Sports PGA Tour remains a reference point for sports design built on golf and management. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Broadcast-quality presentation with commentary packages
  • Licensed clubs, athletes and authentic competition structures
  • A full career mode with transfers, training and press duties
  • Dynamic events and disasters that force replanning
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Detailed statistics and analytics dashboards
  • Course editor with community sharing
  • Staff, morale and logistics across departments

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control tempo, keep shape and take the highest-percentage option available in each phase of play.
  2. 02Play the training drills before your first competitive match so the timing windows are clear.
  3. 03Choose a formation or tactic that matches how you actually play, not the most popular preset.
  4. 04Adjust sliders gradually, one value per match, to find your preferred balance.
  5. 05Rotate your squad deliberately; stamina and form matter more than raw ratings.
  6. 06Study the match stats after each game — possession and pass completion tell the story.
  7. 07Move online once the fundamentals feel routine, and expect the pace to jump.
