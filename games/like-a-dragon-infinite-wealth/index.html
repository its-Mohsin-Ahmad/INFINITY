Like a Dragon: Infinite Wealth

Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio|Sega|2024|RPG · Adventure · Story Rich
9.096,584 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

First launched in 2024, Like a Dragon: Infinite Wealth remains a reference point for role-playing design built on turn-based rpg and detective. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Like a Dragon: Infinite Wealth takes the role-playing blueprint and rebuilds it around turn-based rpg and detective. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Turn-order manipulation and status stacking
  • Observation and interrogation minigames

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  3. 03Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  4. 04Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  5. 05Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  6. 06Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  7. 07Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
