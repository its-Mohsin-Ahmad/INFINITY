ARK: Survival Evolved

Studio Wildcard|Studio Wildcard|2017|Survival · Open World · Co-op
7.438,197 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitchAndroidiOS

ARK: Survival Evolved is what happens when Studio Wildcard commits fully to survival and monster hunt and crafting. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Released in 2017, ARK: Survival Evolved is Studio Wildcard's take on modern survival design with monster hunt and crafting at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Deep inventory and weight management
  • Day-night and seasonal cycles that change what is available
  • Base building with structural and defensive layers
  • Fast travel that unlocks only once a zone has been explored
  • Cinematic camera and photo mode for capture enthusiasts
  • Seamless traversal with no loading transitions between regions
  • Monster AI with sensory perception and stalking behaviour
  • Co-op progression is fully synced, including achievements and unlocks

single playercampaignmultiplayerco oponline coopcross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  3. 03Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  4. 04Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  5. 05Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  6. 06Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  7. 07Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
