Golf It!

Perfektans|Perfektans|2018|Sports · Family
7.836,448 reviews
PC

In Golf It!, Perfektans uses sports conventions as a foundation and party and golf as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. First launched in 2018, Golf It! remains a reference point for sports design built on party and golf. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Local split-screen co-op is supported on top of the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Broadcast-quality presentation with commentary packages
  • Configurable tactics, formations and set-piece routines
  • Online seasons with promotion and relegation ladders
  • Generous checkpointing for younger players
  • Local split-screen and pass-and-play support
  • Family-friendly audio and avatar customisation
  • Four-player chaos with rubber-banded stakes
  • Course editor with community sharing

local coopranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control tempo, keep shape and take the highest-percentage option available in each phase of play.
  2. 02Master one skill move or set-piece routine and use it until it becomes automatic.
  3. 03Adjust sliders gradually, one value per match, to find your preferred balance.
  4. 04Use the simpler difficulty for one full season to learn the systems without heavy punishment.
  5. 05Study the match stats after each game — possession and pass completion tell the story.
  6. 06Play the training drills before your first competitive match so the timing windows are clear.
  7. 07Choose a formation or tactic that matches how you actually play, not the most popular preset.
