Age of Empires IV

Relic Entertainment|Xbox Game Studios|2021|Strategy · Simulation
8.679,797 reviews
PCXbox X|SXbox One

In Age of Empires IV, Relic Entertainment uses strategy conventions as a foundation and real-time strategy and historical as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Age of Empires IV is a strategy experience from Relic Entertainment, built around real-time strategy and historical. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Doctrines and tech trees with genuine opportunity costs
  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Control groups, hotkeys and formation orders
  • Historically grounded settings, equipment and tactics

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  3. 03Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  4. 04Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  5. 05Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  6. 06Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  7. 07Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
