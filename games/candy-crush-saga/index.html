Candy Crush Saga

King|King|2012|Puzzle · Family
7.852,886 reviews
PCAndroidiOS

First launched in 2012, Candy Crush Saga remains a reference point for puzzle design built on physics puzzle and idle. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. King built Candy Crush Saga as a puzzle sandbox first and a showcase second, with physics puzzle and idle as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Hint system that escalates without giving the answer away
  • Zero-fail design that keeps the emphasis on thinking
  • Mechanics introduced one at a time, then combined
  • Simple onboarding that teaches without tutorials
  • Family-friendly audio and avatar customisation
  • Assist modes that any player in the room can enable
  • Sandbox levels for experimenting with mechanics
  • Prestige loops with permanent multipliers
