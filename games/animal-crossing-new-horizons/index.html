Animal Crossing: New Horizons

Nintendo EPD|Nintendo|2020|Simulation · Family
9.084,304 reviews
Switch

Animal Crossing: New Horizons is a simulation experience from Nintendo EPD, built around life sim and party. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Animal Crossing: New Horizons marks another simulation entry from Nintendo EPD, and life sim and party is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Detailed statistics and analytics dashboards
  • Long-tail progression measured in hundreds of hours
  • Local split-screen and pass-and-play support
  • Cross-generation play across living-room platforms
  • Simple onboarding that teaches without tutorials
  • Build and interior design tools
  • Custom rules and round modifiers

Modes

single playerlocal coop

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Balance throughput against cost, and expand only as fast as logistics can sustain.
  2. 02Start in sandbox or creative mode to learn the building and automation tools without pressure.
  3. 03Watch the analytics panels to find the bottleneck rather than expanding everything at once.
  4. 04Use blueprints for anything you will build more than twice.
  5. 05Set up the first resource chain end to end before adding a second.
  6. 06Keep spare capacity in power, storage and transport so growth is never gated by infrastructure.
  7. 07Play one long session with a single goal in mind; simulation games reward clear objectives.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
