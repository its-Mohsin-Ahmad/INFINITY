Until Dawn

Ballistic Moon|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2024|Horror · Adventure · Story Rich
7.896,680 reviews
PCPS5

From Ballistic Moon, Until Dawn is a horror game where monster hunt and visual novel drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Released in 2024, Until Dawn is Ballistic Moon's take on modern horror design with monster hunt and visual novel at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Finite resources that make every decision count
  • Optional one-life mode for the committed
  • Sanity, light and stamina systems that raise tension
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Creature design grounded in practical-effect horror
  • Multiple voiced routes and gallery unlocks

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Manage light, noise and inventory so that every encounter is one you chose to pick.
  2. 02Learn the safe room locations and treat them as planning points, not just save points.
  3. 03Keep your inventory light enough to pick up anything you find.
  4. 04Take breaks. Tension degrades decision-making faster than it degrades the game.
  5. 05Conserve ammunition by disabling or avoiding enemies where the layout allows.
  6. 06Set brightness deliberately — too dark hides mechanics, too bright kills the atmosphere.
  7. 07Note where threats patrol; routes that were dangerous early often become quiet later.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
