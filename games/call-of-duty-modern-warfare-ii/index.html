Call of Duty: Modern Warfare II

Infinity Ward|Activision|2022|FPS · Action · Multiplayer · Story Rich
7.879,253 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare II marks another first-person shooter entry from Infinity Ward, and military and tactical shooter is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Infinity Ward built Call of Duty: Modern Warfare II as a first-person shooter sandbox first and a showcase second, with military and tactical shooter as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Destruction and bullet penetration modelled on real materials
  • Full-spectrum audio with occlusion and distance falloff
  • Weapon sandbox with distinct recoil, handling and TTK per class
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Utility-first combat where information beats raw aim
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  3. 03Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  4. 04Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  5. 05Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  6. 06Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  7. 07Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
