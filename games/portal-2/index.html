Portal 2

Valve|Valve|2011|Puzzle · Adventure · Co-op
9.583,271 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

Portal 2 is the rare puzzle release that treats sci-fi and physics puzzle as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Portal 2 is what happens when Valve commits fully to puzzle and sci-fi and physics puzzle. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Zero-fail design that keeps the emphasis on thinking
  • Hint system that escalates without giving the answer away
  • Colour-blind safe palette and audio cue alternatives
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Hardware, ship and station design language
  • Systems-first puzzles with many valid solutions
