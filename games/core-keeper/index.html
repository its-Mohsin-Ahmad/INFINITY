Core Keeper

Pugstorm|Fireshine Games|2024|Survival · Co-op · Sandbox
8.457,554 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Pugstorm returns with Core Keeper, a survival title that leans hard into crafting and metroidvania. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Core Keeper is the rare survival release that treats crafting and metroidvania as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Online play is the main event, with dedicated servers and skill-based matchmaking. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Environmental threats ranging from weather to predators
  • Deep inventory and weight management
  • Co-op scaling that keeps the challenge intact for a full squad
  • Difficulty that scales with player count
  • Friends Pass access so a squad only needs one copy
  • Drop-in and drop-out with join-in-progress checkpoints
  • Ability-gated world that loops back on itself
  • Co-op progression is fully synced, including achievements and unlocks

Modes

multiplayerco oponline coopsandboxcross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  3. 03Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  4. 04Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  5. 05Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  6. 06Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  7. 07Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
