Elden Ring Nightreign

FromSoftware|Bandai Namco Entertainment|2025|RPG · Action · Co-op
8.2115,511 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Elden Ring Nightreign pairs role-playing systems with roguelike and soulslike, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. From FromSoftware, Elden Ring Nightreign is a role-playing game where roguelike and soulslike drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Procedurally arranged runs built from hand-authored templates
  • Lost-currency retrieval and shortcut-driven level design
