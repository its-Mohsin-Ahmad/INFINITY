Skate.

Full Circle|Electronic Arts|2025|Sports · Open World · Multiplayer
7.6123,777 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Skate. is the rare sports release that treats party and arcade racing as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Released in 2025, Skate. is Full Circle's take on modern sports design with party and arcade racing at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Online seasons with promotion and relegation ladders
  • Skill-based timing mechanics that separate the ranks
  • Broadcast-quality presentation with commentary packages
  • Fast travel that unlocks only once a zone has been explored
  • A living map of dynamic events, convoys and emergent encounters
  • Seamless traversal with no loading transitions between regions
  • Custom rules and round modifiers
  • Ghost races against friends and rivals

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control tempo, keep shape and take the highest-percentage option available in each phase of play.
  2. 02Master one skill move or set-piece routine and use it until it becomes automatic.
  3. 03Study the match stats after each game — possession and pass completion tell the story.
  4. 04Move online once the fundamentals feel routine, and expect the pace to jump.
  5. 05Use the simpler difficulty for one full season to learn the systems without heavy punishment.
  6. 06Adjust sliders gradually, one value per match, to find your preferred balance.
  7. 07Play the training drills before your first competitive match so the timing windows are clear.
