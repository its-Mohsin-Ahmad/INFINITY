Pentiment

Obsidian Entertainment|Xbox Game Studios|2022|Adventure · Story Rich
8.857,554 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Pentiment is a adventure experience from Obsidian Entertainment, built around mystery and detective. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Released in 2022, Pentiment is Obsidian Entertainment's take on modern adventure design with mystery and detective at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Choice systems that alter relationships, not only endings
  • Narrative difficulty options for combat-averse players
  • Environmental storytelling layered into every set
  • Red herrings that respect player intelligence
  • Evidence board with deduction linking

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  3. 03Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  4. 04Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  5. 05Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  6. 06Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  7. 07Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
