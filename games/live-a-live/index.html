Live A Live

Historia|Square Enix|2022|RPG · Story Rich
8.263,342 reviews
PCPS5PS4Switch

Live A Live marks another role-playing entry from Historia, and turn-based rpg and historical is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. First launched in 2022, Live A Live remains a reference point for role-playing design built on turn-based rpg and historical. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Director commentary and behind-the-scenes features
  • Chapter select and scene replay after completion
  • Environmental storytelling layered into every set
  • Turn-order manipulation and status stacking
  • Archive-quality research and art direction

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  3. 03Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  4. 04Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  5. 05Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  6. 06Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  7. 07Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  8. 08Platform note — 4 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
