Halo Infinite

343 Industries|Xbox Game Studios|2021|FPS · Multiplayer · Action
8.3121,897 reviews
PCXbox X|SXbox One

Halo Infinite is a first-person shooter experience from 343 Industries, built around arena shooter and sci-fi. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. From 343 Industries, Halo Infinite is a first-person shooter game where arena shooter and sci-fi drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Custom loadouts with attachment-level trade-offs
  • Destruction and bullet penetration modelled on real materials
  • Weapon sandbox with distinct recoil, handling and TTK per class
  • Dedicated server architecture with a 60Hz+ tick rate
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Short match formats built for ranked play
  • Hardware, ship and station design language

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  3. 03Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  4. 04Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  5. 05Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  6. 06Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  7. 07Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
