Trackmania

Nadeo|Ubisoft|2020|Racing · Multiplayer
8.495,529 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Trackmania is the rare racing release that treats arcade racing and physics puzzle as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Released in 2020, Trackmania is Nadeo's take on modern racing design with arcade racing and physics puzzle at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Rewind, ghost and rival-lap systems for repeat attempts
  • Replay tooling with camera rigs and telemetry overlays
  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Dedicated server architecture with a 60Hz+ tick rate
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Seasonal progression with challenges and rewards
  • Ghost races against friends and rivals
  • Sandbox levels for experimenting with mechanics

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  3. 03Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  4. 04Race shorter events first so mistakes cost fewer laps.
  5. 05Drive a single car for ten laps on a single circuit before changing either variable.
  6. 06Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  7. 07Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
