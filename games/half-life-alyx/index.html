Half-Life: Alyx

Valve|Valve|2020|FPS · Adventure · Horror
9.0102,384 reviews
PC

In Half-Life: Alyx, Valve uses first-person shooter conventions as a foundation and survival horror and sci-fi as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. From Valve, Half-Life: Alyx is a first-person shooter game where survival horror and sci-fi drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Weapon sandbox with distinct recoil, handling and TTK per class
  • Destruction and bullet penetration modelled on real materials
  • Custom loadouts with attachment-level trade-offs
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Safe rooms with item boxes and save stations
  • Hardware, ship and station design language

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  3. 03Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  4. 04Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  5. 05Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  6. 06Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  7. 07Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
