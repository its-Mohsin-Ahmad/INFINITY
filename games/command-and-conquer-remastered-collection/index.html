Command & Conquer Remastered Collection

Petroglyph Games|Electronic Arts|2020|Strategy
8.884,884 reviews
PC

Command & Conquer Remastered Collection marks another strategy entry from Petroglyph Games, and real-time strategy and historical is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Released in 2020, Command & Conquer Remastered Collection is Petroglyph Games's take on modern strategy design with real-time strategy and historical at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Mod support with exposed data files and tooling
  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Base building and unit composition in real time
  • Archive-quality research and art direction
  • Speculative technology with in-world justification
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  3. 03Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  4. 04Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  5. 05Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  6. 06Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  7. 07Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
