Teamfight Tactics

Riot Games|Riot Games|2019|Strategy · Multiplayer
8.4115,292 reviews
PCAndroidiOS

In Teamfight Tactics, Riot Games uses strategy conventions as a foundation and card battler and moba as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Teamfight Tactics is the rare strategy release that treats card battler and moba as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Skill-based matchmaking with transparent rating tiers
  • Dedicated server architecture with a 60Hz+ tick rate
  • Seasonal card sets with regular balance patches
  • Draft and ban phases with role queue

Modes

single playermultiplayeronline pvpranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  3. 03Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  4. 04Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  5. 05Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  6. 06Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  7. 07Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
