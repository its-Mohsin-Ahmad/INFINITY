Gears Tactics

Splash Damage|Xbox Game Studios|2020|Strategy · Action
8.054,406 reviews
PCXbox X|SXbox One

Splash Damage returns with Gears Tactics, a strategy title that leans hard into tactical rpg and sci-fi. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Gears Tactics takes the strategy blueprint and rebuilds it around tactical rpg and sci-fi. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Doctrines and tech trees with genuine opportunity costs
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Grid-based tactics with height and cover
  • Hardware, ship and station design language

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  3. 03Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  4. 04Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  5. 05Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  6. 06Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  7. 07Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
