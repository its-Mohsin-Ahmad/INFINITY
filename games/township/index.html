Township

Playrix|Playrix|2013|Simulation · Family
7.459,883 reviews
AndroidiOS

Township pairs simulation systems with city builder and farming sim, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. In Township, Playrix uses simulation conventions as a foundation and city builder and farming sim as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Dynamic events and disasters that force replanning
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Detailed statistics and analytics dashboards
  • Assist modes that any player in the room can enable
  • Family-friendly audio and avatar customisation
  • Generous checkpointing for younger players
  • Zoning, traffic and utility simulation at district scale
  • Seasonal crop, livestock and contract loops

Modes

single playerlocal coop

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Balance throughput against cost, and expand only as fast as logistics can sustain.
  2. 02Set up the first resource chain end to end before adding a second.
  3. 03Save often, and label saves before large redesigns.
  4. 04Start in sandbox or creative mode to learn the building and automation tools without pressure.
  5. 05Once the basics are automatic, increase demand and see which system fails first.
  6. 06Watch the analytics panels to find the bottleneck rather than expanding everything at once.
  7. 07Use blueprints for anything you will build more than twice.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
