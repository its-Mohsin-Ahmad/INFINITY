Slay the Spire

Mega Crit Games|Mega Crit Games|2019|Indie · Strategy
9.1104,465 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitchAndroidiOS

Slay the Spire is what happens when Mega Crit Games commits fully to independent and card battler and roguelike. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. In Slay the Spire, Mega Crit Games uses independent conventions as a foundation and card battler and roguelike as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Original score with adaptive audio systems
  • Full controller and Steam Deck verification
  • Hand-crafted art direction with a signature visual identity
  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Doctrines and tech trees with genuine opportunity costs
  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Seasonal card sets with regular balance patches
  • Procedurally arranged runs built from hand-authored templates

single playercross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  3. 03Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  4. 04Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  5. 05Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  6. 06Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  7. 07Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  8. 08Platform note — 8 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
