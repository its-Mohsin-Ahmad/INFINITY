Little Nightmares III

Supermassive Games|Bandai Namco Entertainment|2025|Horror · Adventure · Puzzle · Co-op
7.975,594 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Little Nightmares III is a horror experience from Supermassive Games, built around psychological horror and platformer. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Little Nightmares III is what happens when Supermassive Games commits fully to horror and psychological horror and platformer. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Persistent threats that learn and reposition
  • Sound design that prioritises spatial cues over cheap jumps
  • Finite resources that make every decision count
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • No-combat stretches that rely on atmosphere
  • Collectibles and time trials per stage

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Manage light, noise and inventory so that every encounter is one you chose to pick.
  2. 02Set brightness deliberately — too dark hides mechanics, too bright kills the atmosphere.
  3. 03Conserve ammunition by disabling or avoiding enemies where the layout allows.
  4. 04Take breaks. Tension degrades decision-making faster than it degrades the game.
  5. 05Play with headphones. Spatial audio is the primary information channel in this game.
  6. 06Note where threats patrol; routes that were dangerous early often become quiet later.
  7. 07Learn the safe room locations and treat them as planning points, not just save points.
  8. 08Platform note — 6 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
