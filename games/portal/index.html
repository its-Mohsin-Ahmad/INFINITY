Portal

Valve|Valve|2007|Puzzle · Adventure
9.440,794 reviews
PCSwitch

Portal pairs puzzle systems with sci-fi and physics puzzle, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. First launched in 2007, Portal remains a reference point for puzzle design built on sci-fi and physics puzzle. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Colour-blind safe palette and audio cue alternatives
  • Leaderboards, ghosts and speedrun timers
  • Mechanics introduced one at a time, then combined
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Hardware, ship and station design language
  • Sandbox levels for experimenting with mechanics

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  3. 03Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  4. 04Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  5. 05Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  6. 06Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  7. 07Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
