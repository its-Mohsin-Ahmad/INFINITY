Detroit: Become Human

Quantic Dream|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2018|Adventure · Story Rich
8.672,913 reviews
PCPS4

Quantic Dream built Detroit: Become Human as a adventure sandbox first and a showcase second, with mystery and sci-fi as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Detroit: Become Human is the rare adventure release that treats mystery and sci-fi as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Environmental storytelling layered into every set
  • Performance-captured dialogue with a full ensemble cast
  • Narrative difficulty options for combat-averse players
  • Red herrings that respect player intelligence
  • Speculative technology with in-world justification

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  3. 03Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  4. 04Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  5. 05Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  6. 06Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  7. 07Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
