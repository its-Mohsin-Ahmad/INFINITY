Sea of Thieves

Rare|Xbox Game Studios|2018|Adventure · Multiplayer · Co-op · Open World
8.2109,505 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|SXbox One

Sea of Thieves takes the adventure blueprint and rebuilds it around naval and party. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Sea of Thieves pairs adventure systems with naval and party, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Dedicated server architecture with a 60Hz+ tick rate
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Skill-based matchmaking with transparent rating tiers
  • Four-player chaos with rubber-banded stakes
  • Co-op progression is fully synced, including achievements and unlocks

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  3. 03Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  4. 04Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  5. 05Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  6. 06Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  7. 07Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  8. 08Platform note — 4 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
