Celeste

Maddy Makes Games|Maddy Makes Games|2018|Indie · Story Rich
9.159,838 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Celeste is the rare independent release that treats platformer and physics puzzle as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Celeste is what happens when Maddy Makes Games commits fully to independent and platformer and physics puzzle. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Tight, focused runtime built around one strong idea
  • Accessibility options shipped on day one
  • Hand-crafted art direction with a signature visual identity
  • Environmental storytelling layered into every set
  • Director commentary and behind-the-scenes features
  • Chapter select and scene replay after completion
  • Collectibles and time trials per stage
  • Systems-first puzzles with many valid solutions
