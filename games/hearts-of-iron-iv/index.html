Hearts of Iron IV

Paradox Development Studio|Paradox Interactive|2016|Strategy · Simulation
8.591,112 reviews
PC

Hearts of Iron IV is a strategy experience from Paradox Development Studio, built around historical and military sim. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Hearts of Iron IV takes the strategy blueprint and rebuilds it around historical and military sim. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Doctrines and tech trees with genuine opportunity costs
  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Long-tail progression measured in hundreds of hours
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Historically grounded settings, equipment and tactics
  • Squad-level radio orders and command hierarchy

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  3. 03Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  4. 04Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  5. 05Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  6. 06Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  7. 07Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
