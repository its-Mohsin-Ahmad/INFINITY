Battlefield V

DICE|Electronic Arts|2018|FPS · Multiplayer · Action
7.647,231 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

From DICE, Battlefield V is a first-person shooter game where military and historical drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Battlefield V is the rare first-person shooter release that treats military and historical as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Custom loadouts with attachment-level trade-offs
  • Sensitivity, FOV and controller response fully configurable
  • Weapon sandbox with distinct recoil, handling and TTK per class
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Full stat tracking with match history and replays
  • Seasonal progression with challenges and rewards
  • Historically grounded settings, equipment and tactics
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  3. 03Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  4. 04Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  5. 05Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  6. 06Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  7. 07Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
