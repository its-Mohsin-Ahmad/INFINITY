Shin Megami Tensei V: Vengeance

Atlus|Sega|2024|RPG
8.855,895 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Shin Megami Tensei V: Vengeance is a role-playing experience from Atlus, built around fantasy and turn-based rpg. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Shin Megami Tensei V: Vengeance is the rare role-playing release that treats fantasy and turn-based rpg as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours
  • Turn-order manipulation and status stacking
  • Creature design grounded in practical-effect horror
  • Cross-platform play and progression across every supported storefront
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  3. 03Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  4. 04Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  5. 05Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  6. 06Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  7. 07Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  8. 08Platform note — 6 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
