Tell Me Why

Dontnod Entertainment|Xbox Game Studios|2020|Adventure · Story Rich
7.740,869 reviews
PCXbox X|SXbox One

In Tell Me Why, Dontnod Entertainment uses adventure conventions as a foundation and mystery and narrative adventure as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Tell Me Why is a adventure experience from Dontnod Entertainment, built around mystery and narrative adventure. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Choice systems that alter relationships, not only endings
  • Environmental storytelling layered into every set
  • Chapter select and scene replay after completion
  • Red herrings that respect player intelligence
  • Diegetic UI and in-world documentation

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  3. 03Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  4. 04Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  5. 05Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  6. 06Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  7. 07Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
