Splitgate 2

1047 Games|1047 Games|2025|FPS · Multiplayer · Action
8.095,016 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Splitgate 2 is the rare first-person shooter release that treats arena shooter as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Released in 2025, Splitgate 2 is 1047 Games's take on modern first-person shooter design with arena shooter at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Full-spectrum audio with occlusion and distance falloff
  • Sensitivity, FOV and controller response fully configurable
  • Custom loadouts with attachment-level trade-offs
  • Seasonal progression with challenges and rewards
  • Skill-based matchmaking with transparent rating tiers
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Map control, pickups and respawn economy
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

single playermultiplayeronline pvpranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  3. 03Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  4. 04Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  5. 05Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  6. 06Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  7. 07Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
