Undisputed

Steel City Interactive|Deep Silver|2024|Sports · Fighting · Simulation
7.272,129 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Undisputed is what happens when Steel City Interactive commits fully to sports and combat sports and management. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Undisputed is the rare sports release that treats combat sports and management as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Configurable tactics, formations and set-piece routines
  • Skill-based timing mechanics that separate the ranks
  • Licensed clubs, athletes and authentic competition structures
  • Extensive customisation across outfits and colour channels
  • Frame data and training tools built into the client
  • Rollback netcode with region-aware matchmaking
  • Career ladder across sanctioned promotions
  • Staff, morale and logistics across departments

Modes

single playerranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control spacing and frame advantage, then punish predictable behaviour with a full combo.
  2. 02Play long sets rather than first-to-one; adaptation is the real skill.
  3. 03Learn the matchup for the character you lose to most before learning a new fighter.
  4. 04Set a ranked goal per session and stop when you hit it, win or lose.
  5. 05Watch your own replays and count how often you press a button while minus.
  6. 06Use training mode's frame data display to check whether moves are safe on block.
  7. 07Learn one character's normals, one anti-air and one reliable punish.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
