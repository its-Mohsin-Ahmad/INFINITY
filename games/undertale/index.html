Undertale

Toby Fox|Toby Fox|2015|Indie · RPG · Story Rich
9.481,743 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

Undertale marks another independent entry from Toby Fox, and turn-based rpg is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. In Undertale, Toby Fox uses independent conventions as a foundation and turn-based rpg as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Full controller and Steam Deck verification
  • Original score with adaptive audio systems
  • Accessibility options shipped on day one
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Party composition with elemental weaknesses
  • Cross-platform play and progression across every supported storefront

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  3. 03Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  4. 04Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  5. 05Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  6. 06Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  7. 07Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  8. 08Platform note — 4 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
