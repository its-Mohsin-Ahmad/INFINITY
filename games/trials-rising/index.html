Trials Rising

RedLynx|Ubisoft|2019|Racing · Family
8.147,152 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

RedLynx returns with Trials Rising, a racing title that leans hard into physics puzzle and arcade racing. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. In Trials Rising, RedLynx uses racing conventions as a foundation and physics puzzle and arcade racing as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Dynamic time of day and weather across the full calendar
  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Tuning suite for gear ratios, suspension, camber and aero
  • Cross-generation play across living-room platforms
  • Family-friendly audio and avatar customisation
  • Local split-screen and pass-and-play support
  • Sandbox levels for experimenting with mechanics
  • Boost, drift and nitro mechanics tuned for instant fun

single playerlocal coopcross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  3. 03Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  4. 04Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
  5. 05Race shorter events first so mistakes cost fewer laps.
  6. 06Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  7. 07Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
  8. 08Platform note — 4 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
