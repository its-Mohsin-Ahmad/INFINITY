Star Citizen

Cloud Imperium Games|Cloud Imperium Games|2017|Simulation · Multiplayer
7.030,886 reviews
PC

Cloud Imperium Games built Star Citizen as a simulation sandbox first and a showcase second, with space sim and flight sim as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Star Citizen is the rare simulation release that treats space sim and flight sim as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Long-tail progression measured in hundreds of hours
  • Dynamic events and disasters that force replanning
  • Detailed statistics and analytics dashboards
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Skill-based matchmaking with transparent rating tiers
  • Newtonian flight with docking and trade routes
  • Real-world terrain with live weather injection

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Balance throughput against cost, and expand only as fast as logistics can sustain.
  2. 02Set up the first resource chain end to end before adding a second.
  3. 03Use blueprints for anything you will build more than twice.
  4. 04Save often, and label saves before large redesigns.
  5. 05Start in sandbox or creative mode to learn the building and automation tools without pressure.
  6. 06Watch the analytics panels to find the bottleneck rather than expanding everything at once.
  7. 07Play one long session with a single goal in mind; simulation games reward clear objectives.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
