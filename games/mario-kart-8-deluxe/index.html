Mario Kart 8 Deluxe

Nintendo EPD|Nintendo|2017|Racing · Family · Multiplayer
9.3141,957 reviews
Switch

First launched in 2017, Mario Kart 8 Deluxe remains a reference point for racing design built on kart racing and party. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Nintendo EPD returns with Mario Kart 8 Deluxe, a racing title that leans hard into kart racing and party. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Tuning suite for gear ratios, suspension, camber and aero
  • Ranked racing with licences and a clean-driving rating
  • Simple onboarding that teaches without tutorials
  • Assist modes that any player in the room can enable
  • Local split-screen and pass-and-play support
  • Power-up economy balanced for comeback racing
  • Four-player chaos with rubber-banded stakes

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
  3. 03Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  4. 04Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
  5. 05Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  6. 06Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  7. 07Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
