Pokemon Legends: Arceus

Game Freak|Nintendo|2022|RPG · Adventure · Open World
8.383,466 reviews
Switch

Pokemon Legends: Arceus is the rare role-playing release that treats monster hunt and action rpg as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. In Pokemon Legends: Arceus, Game Freak uses role-playing conventions as a foundation and monster hunt and action rpg as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Creature design grounded in practical-effect horror
  • Loot affixes that reshape entire builds

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  3. 03Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  4. 04Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  5. 05Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  6. 06Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  7. 07Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
