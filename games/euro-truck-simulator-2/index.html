Euro Truck Simulator 2

SCS Software|SCS Software|2013|Simulation · Racing · Open World
9.262,464 reviews
PC

First launched in 2013, Euro Truck Simulator 2 remains a reference point for simulation design built on management. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Euro Truck Simulator 2 is the rare simulation release that treats management as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Detailed statistics and analytics dashboards
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Dynamic time of day and weather across the full calendar
  • Tuning suite for gear ratios, suspension, camber and aero
  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Staff, morale and logistics across departments
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Race shorter events first so mistakes cost fewer laps.
  3. 03Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
  4. 04Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  5. 05Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  6. 06Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  7. 07Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
