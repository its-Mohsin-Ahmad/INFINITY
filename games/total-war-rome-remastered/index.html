Total War: Rome Remastered

Creative Assembly|Sega|2021|Strategy
8.261,640 reviews
PC

Creative Assembly returns with Total War: Rome Remastered, a strategy title that leans hard into historical and real-time strategy. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Creative Assembly built Total War: Rome Remastered as a strategy sandbox first and a showcase second, with historical and real-time strategy as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Historically grounded settings, equipment and tactics
  • Control groups, hotkeys and formation orders
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  3. 03Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  4. 04Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  5. 05Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  6. 06Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  7. 07Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
