Roblox

Roblox Corporation|Roblox Corporation|2006|Sandbox · Multiplayer · Family
7.9114,739 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneAndroidiOS

In Roblox, Roblox Corporation uses sandbox conventions as a foundation and sandbox craft and party as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Roblox takes the sandbox blueprint and rebuilds it around sandbox craft and party. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Online play is the main event, with dedicated servers and skill-based matchmaking. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Community showcase feeds and featured builds
  • Creative tools with sharing and remix support
  • Progression-free play where creativity is the reward
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Dedicated server architecture with a 60Hz+ tick rate
  • Full stat tracking with match history and replays
  • Logic, wiring and automation systems
  • Custom rules and round modifiers

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  3. 03Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  4. 04Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  5. 05Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  6. 06Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  7. 07Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
