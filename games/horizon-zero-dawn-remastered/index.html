Horizon Zero Dawn Remastered

Guerrilla Games|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2024|Action · RPG · Open World · Story Rich
8.792,250 reviews
PCPS5

From Guerrilla Games, Horizon Zero Dawn Remastered is a action game where sci-fi and action rpg drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Horizon Zero Dawn Remastered marks another action entry from Guerrilla Games, and sci-fi and action rpg is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Hardware, ship and station design language
  • Real-time combat with stat-driven damage maths

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  3. 03Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  4. 04Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  5. 05Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  6. 06Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  7. 07Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
