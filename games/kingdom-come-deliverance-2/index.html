Kingdom Come: Deliverance II

Warhorse Studios|Deep Silver|2025|RPG · Open World · Story Rich
9.0150,382 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

In Kingdom Come: Deliverance II, Warhorse Studios uses role-playing conventions as a foundation and historical and soulslike as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Kingdom Come: Deliverance II takes the role-playing blueprint and rebuilds it around historical and soulslike. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • A living map of dynamic events, convoys and emergent encounters
  • Distinct biomes with their own wildlife, weather and factions
  • Discovery-led design — landmarks are the map marker
  • Historically grounded settings, equipment and tactics
  • Lost-currency retrieval and shortcut-driven level design

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  3. 03Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  4. 04Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  5. 05Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  6. 06Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  7. 07Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
