Balatro

LocalThunk|Playstack|2024|Indie · Puzzle
9.0115,219 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitchAndroidiOS

In Balatro, LocalThunk uses independent conventions as a foundation and roguelike and card battler as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Balatro pairs independent systems with roguelike and card battler, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Regular post-launch updates funded by the community
  • Original score with adaptive audio systems
  • Full controller and Steam Deck verification
  • Colour-blind safe palette and audio cue alternatives
  • Zero-fail design that keeps the emphasis on thinking
  • Leaderboards, ghosts and speedrun timers
  • Procedurally arranged runs built from hand-authored templates
  • Seasonal card sets with regular balance patches
