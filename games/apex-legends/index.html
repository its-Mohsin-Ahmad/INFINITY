Apex Legends

Respawn Entertainment|Electronic Arts|2019|Battle Royale · FPS · Multiplayer
8.6135,064 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Respawn Entertainment returns with Apex Legends, a battle royale title that leans hard into hero shooter and extraction. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Apex Legends marks another battle royale entry from Respawn Entertainment, and hero shooter and extraction is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Squad play with pings, comms and role specialisation
  • Large-scale matches with drop-in, respawn and revival systems
  • Ranked progression with placement matches and decay
  • Gunplay tuned for controller and mouse alike, with no aim-assist tax
  • Weapon sandbox with distinct recoil, handling and TTK per class
  • Destruction and bullet penetration modelled on real materials
  • Role queue and clear team-composition feedback
  • Player-market pricing on every extracted item

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  3. 03Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  4. 04Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  5. 05Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  6. 06Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  7. 07Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
