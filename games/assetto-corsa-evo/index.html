Assetto Corsa EVO

Kunos Simulazioni|505 Games|2025|Racing · Simulation
8.268,646 reviews
PC

Kunos Simulazioni returns with Assetto Corsa EVO, a racing title that leans hard into sim racing and management. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Assetto Corsa EVO is the rare racing release that treats sim racing and management as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Tuning suite for gear ratios, suspension, camber and aero
  • Rewind, ghost and rival-lap systems for repeat attempts
  • Replay tooling with camera rigs and telemetry overlays
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Dynamic events and disasters that force replanning
  • Force-feedback profiles and telemetry output
  • Staff, morale and logistics across departments

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Drive a single car for ten laps on a single circuit before changing either variable.
  3. 03Race shorter events first so mistakes cost fewer laps.
  4. 04Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  5. 05Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  6. 06Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  7. 07Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
