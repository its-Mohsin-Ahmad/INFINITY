Pacific Drive

Ironwood Studios|Kepler Interactive|2024|Survival · Horror
8.382,009 reviews
PCPS5

Released in 2024, Pacific Drive is Ironwood Studios's take on modern survival design with physics puzzle and post-apocalyptic at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. In Pacific Drive, Ironwood Studios uses survival conventions as a foundation and physics puzzle and post-apocalyptic as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Day-night and seasonal cycles that change what is available
  • Deep inventory and weight management
  • Base building with structural and defensive layers
  • Sound design that prioritises spatial cues over cheap jumps
  • Optional one-life mode for the committed
  • Persistent threats that learn and reposition
  • Systems-first puzzles with many valid solutions
  • Scavenging in ruined landscapes under faction pressure

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Establish shelter and a resource loop before pushing into higher-risk regions.
  2. 02Play with one friend early; co-op scales difficulty but doubles carrying capacity.
  3. 03Track day-night and weather cycles; both change which threats are active.
  4. 04Build a small base near resources but away from high-traffic enemy paths.
  5. 05Do not fight every fight. Running is a valid response to a bad engagement.
  6. 06Bring a dedicated combat loadout when gathering, and a gathering loadout when fighting.
  7. 07Craft the basic tool set before exploring further than you can walk back.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
