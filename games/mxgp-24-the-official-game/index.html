MXGP 24: The Official Game

Milestone|Milestone|2024|Racing · Simulation · Sports
7.476,100 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Released in 2024, MXGP 24: The Official Game is Milestone's take on modern racing design with sim racing and management at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Milestone built MXGP 24: The Official Game as a racing sandbox first and a showcase second, with sim racing and management as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Dynamic time of day and weather across the full calendar
  • Tuning suite for gear ratios, suspension, camber and aero
  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Tyre temperature and fuel-load strategy across race distance
  • Staff, morale and logistics across departments

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  3. 03Race shorter events first so mistakes cost fewer laps.
  4. 04Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
  5. 05Drive a single car for ten laps on a single circuit before changing either variable.
  6. 06Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  7. 07Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
