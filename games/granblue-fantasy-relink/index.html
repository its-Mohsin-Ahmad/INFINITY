Granblue Fantasy: Relink

Cygames Osaka|Cygames|2024|Action · RPG · Co-op
8.251,883 reviews
PCPS5PS4

Granblue Fantasy: Relink is what happens when Cygames Osaka commits fully to action and action rpg and fantasy. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Cygames Osaka returns with Granblue Fantasy: Relink, a action title that leans hard into action rpg and fantasy. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Real-time combat with stat-driven damage maths
  • Magic schools with distinct resource economies
