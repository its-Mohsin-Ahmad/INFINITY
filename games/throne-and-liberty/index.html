Throne and Liberty

NCSoft|Amazon Games|2024|MMORPG · Action · Open World
7.088,244 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

NCSoft built Throne and Liberty as a massively multiplayer sandbox first and a showcase second, with action rpg as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. NCSoft returns with Throne and Liberty, a massively multiplayer title that leans hard into action rpg. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Faction, guild and territory systems with real stakes
  • Persistent world with a genuinely player-driven economy
  • Deep character progression with respec flexibility
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Real-time combat with stat-driven damage maths
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

single playercampaignmultiplayer

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  3. 03Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  4. 04Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  5. 05Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  6. 06Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  7. 07Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
