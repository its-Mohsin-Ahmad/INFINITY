Moving Out 2

SMG Studio|Team17|2023|Co-op · Family
7.954,648 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Moving Out 2 pairs co-operative systems with party and physics puzzle, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Moving Out 2 takes the co-operative blueprint and rebuilds it around party and physics puzzle. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Online play is the main event, with dedicated servers and skill-based matchmaking. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Friends Pass access so a squad only needs one copy
  • Asymmetric roles that demand genuine coordination
  • Proximity voice with optional speech-to-text
  • Simple onboarding that teaches without tutorials
  • Family-friendly audio and avatar customisation
  • Generous checkpointing for younger players
  • Custom rules and round modifiers
  • Systems-first puzzles with many valid solutions

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Keep the group laughing while quietly engineering the round-ending play.
  2. 02If someone is struggling, hand them the support role; it is usually the most valuable one.
  3. 03Use voice or proximity chat — these games are built on communication.
  4. 04Keep sessions to ninety minutes or less; comedy has diminishing returns.
  5. 05Rotate hosts so everyone keeps their progression.
  6. 06Assign roles based on what each player enjoys, not on who is objectively best.
  7. 07Play the tutorial stage with everyone in the room so nobody is learning alone.
