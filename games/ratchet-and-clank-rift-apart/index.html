Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart

Insomniac Games|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2021|Action · Adventure · Family
8.971,592 reviews
PCPS5

Insomniac Games built Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart as a action sandbox first and a showcase second, with platformer and sci-fi as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Released in 2021, Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart is Insomniac Games's take on modern action design with platformer and sci-fi at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Precision movement with coyote time and air control
  • Hardware, ship and station design language
