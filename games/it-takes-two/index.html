It Takes Two

Hazelight Studios|Electronic Arts|2021|Co-op · Adventure · Story Rich
9.1133,522 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

It Takes Two is a co-operative experience from Hazelight Studios, built around platformer and party. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. From Hazelight Studios, It Takes Two is a co-operative game where platformer and party drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Designed from the ground up for two to four players
  • Drop-in and drop-out with join-in-progress checkpoints
  • Friends Pass access so a squad only needs one copy
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Precision movement with coyote time and air control
  • Four-player chaos with rubber-banded stakes

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Keep the group laughing while quietly engineering the round-ending play.
  2. 02Screenshot the results screen. You will want them later.
  3. 03Rotate hosts so everyone keeps their progression.
  4. 04Assign roles based on what each player enjoys, not on who is objectively best.
  5. 05Play the tutorial stage with everyone in the room so nobody is learning alone.
  6. 06Try one full round with default rules before adding modifiers.
  7. 07If someone is struggling, hand them the support role; it is usually the most valuable one.
