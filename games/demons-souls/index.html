Demon's Souls

Bluepoint Games|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2020|RPG · Action
9.254,092 reviews
PS5

Demon's Souls marks another role-playing entry from Bluepoint Games, and soulslike and fantasy is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Bluepoint Games built Demon's Souls as a role-playing sandbox first and a showcase second, with soulslike and fantasy as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Lost-currency retrieval and shortcut-driven level design
  • Magic schools with distinct resource economies

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  3. 03Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  4. 04Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  5. 05Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  6. 06Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  7. 07Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
