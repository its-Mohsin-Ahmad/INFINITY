Oxygen Not Included

Klei Entertainment|Klei Entertainment|2019|Simulation · Strategy · Survival
8.848,803 reviews
PC

Oxygen Not Included marks another simulation entry from Klei Entertainment, and management and physics puzzle is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Klei Entertainment built Oxygen Not Included as a simulation sandbox first and a showcase second, with management and physics puzzle as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Dynamic events and disasters that force replanning
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Long-tail progression measured in hundreds of hours
  • Mod support with exposed data files and tooling
  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Financial modelling with real consequences
  • Sandbox levels for experimenting with mechanics

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  3. 03Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  4. 04Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  5. 05Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  6. 06Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  7. 07Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
