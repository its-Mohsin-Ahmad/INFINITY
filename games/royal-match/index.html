Royal Match

Dream Games|Dream Games|2021|Puzzle · Family
8.0105,609 reviews
AndroidiOS

In Royal Match, Dream Games uses puzzle conventions as a foundation and physics puzzle and idle as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Royal Match pairs puzzle systems with physics puzzle and idle, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Mechanics introduced one at a time, then combined
  • Leaderboards, ghosts and speedrun timers
  • Hint system that escalates without giving the answer away
  • Local split-screen and pass-and-play support
  • Cross-generation play across living-room platforms
  • Generous checkpointing for younger players
  • Sandbox levels for experimenting with mechanics
  • Prestige loops with permanent multipliers
