Death Stranding 2: On the Beach

Kojima Productions|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2025|Action · Adventure · Story Rich
8.9120,079 reviews
PS5

Kojima Productions built Death Stranding 2: On the Beach as a action sandbox first and a showcase second, with sci-fi and narrative adventure as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Kojima Productions returns with Death Stranding 2: On the Beach, a action title that leans hard into sci-fi and narrative adventure. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Hardware, ship and station design language
  • Exploration-first pacing with no fail states

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  3. 03Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  4. 04Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  5. 05Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  6. 06Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  7. 07Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
