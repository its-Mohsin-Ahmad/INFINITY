Hades

Supergiant Games|Supergiant Games|2020|Indie · Action · Story Rich
9.3135,417 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Hades is what happens when Supergiant Games commits fully to independent and roguelike and roguelite. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Released in 2020, Hades is Supergiant Games's take on modern independent design with roguelike and roguelite at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Full controller and Steam Deck verification
  • Hand-crafted art direction with a signature visual identity
  • Tight, focused runtime built around one strong idea
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Meta-progression that unlocks new starting options
  • Mutators for player-authored difficulty

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  3. 03Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  4. 04Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  5. 05Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  6. 06Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  7. 07Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
