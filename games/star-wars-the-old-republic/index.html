Star Wars: The Old Republic

BioWare|Electronic Arts|2011|MMORPG · RPG · Story Rich
8.169,989 reviews
PC

Star Wars: The Old Republic is the rare massively multiplayer release that treats space rpg and sci-fi as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. From BioWare, Star Wars: The Old Republic is a massively multiplayer game where space rpg and sci-fi drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Persistent world with a genuinely player-driven economy
  • Deep character progression with respec flexibility
  • Seasonal story arcs with full voice performance
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Hardware, ship and station design language
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single playercampaignmultiplayer

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  3. 03Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  4. 04Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  5. 05Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  6. 06Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  7. 07Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
