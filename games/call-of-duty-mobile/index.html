Call of Duty: Mobile

TiMi Studio Group|Activision|2019|FPS · Battle Royale · Multiplayer
7.871,342 reviews
AndroidiOS

First launched in 2019, Call of Duty: Mobile remains a reference point for first-person shooter design built on military and arena shooter. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. TiMi Studio Group built Call of Duty: Mobile as a first-person shooter sandbox first and a showcase second, with military and arena shooter as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Gunplay tuned for controller and mouse alike, with no aim-assist tax
  • Sensitivity, FOV and controller response fully configurable
  • Weapon sandbox with distinct recoil, handling and TTK per class
  • Ranked progression with placement matches and decay
  • Squad play with pings, comms and role specialisation
  • Cross-play with input-based matchmaking pools
  • Short match formats built for ranked play
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  3. 03Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  4. 04Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  5. 05Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  6. 06Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  7. 07Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
