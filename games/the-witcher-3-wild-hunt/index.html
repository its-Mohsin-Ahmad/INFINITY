The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt

CD Projekt Red|CD Projekt|2015|RPG · Open World · Story Rich · Action
9.4118,585 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

CD Projekt Red built The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt as a role-playing sandbox first and a showcase second, with fantasy and action rpg as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt pairs role-playing systems with fantasy and action rpg, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Distinct biomes with their own wildlife, weather and factions
  • Cinematic camera and photo mode for capture enthusiasts
  • Discovery-led design — landmarks are the map marker
  • Magic schools with distinct resource economies
  • Real-time combat with stat-driven damage maths

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  3. 03Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  4. 04Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  5. 05Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  6. 06Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  7. 07Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  8. 08Platform note — 6 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
