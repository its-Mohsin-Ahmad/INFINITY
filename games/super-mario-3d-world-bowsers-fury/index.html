Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury

Nintendo EPD|Nintendo|2021|Family · Co-op
9.052,938 reviews
Switch

In Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury, Nintendo EPD uses family-friendly conventions as a foundation and platformer and party as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury takes the family-friendly blueprint and rebuilds it around platformer and party. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Online play is the main event, with dedicated servers and skill-based matchmaking. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Cross-generation play across living-room platforms
  • Local split-screen and pass-and-play support
  • Generous checkpointing for younger players
  • Drop-in and drop-out with join-in-progress checkpoints
  • Proximity voice with optional speech-to-text
  • Designed from the ground up for two to four players
  • Collectibles and time trials per stage
  • Custom rules and round modifiers
