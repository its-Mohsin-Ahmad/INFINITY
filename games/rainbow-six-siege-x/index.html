Rainbow Six Siege X

Ubisoft Montreal|Ubisoft|2025|FPS · Multiplayer
8.6156,430 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Rainbow Six Siege X is a first-person shooter experience from Ubisoft Montreal, built around tactical shooter and cover shooter. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Ubisoft Montreal built Rainbow Six Siege X as a first-person shooter sandbox first and a showcase second, with tactical shooter and cover shooter as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Sensitivity, FOV and controller response fully configurable
  • Custom loadouts with attachment-level trade-offs
  • Weapon sandbox with distinct recoil, handling and TTK per class
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Skill-based matchmaking with transparent rating tiers
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Economy rounds with buy-phase decisions
  • Blind fire, snap cover and flank-happy AI

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  3. 03Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  4. 04Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  5. 05Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  6. 06Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  7. 07Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
