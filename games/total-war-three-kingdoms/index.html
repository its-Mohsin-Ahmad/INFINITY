Total War: Three Kingdoms

Creative Assembly|Sega|2019|Strategy
8.443,738 reviews
PC

Total War: Three Kingdoms marks another strategy entry from Creative Assembly, and historical and real-time strategy is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Creative Assembly built Total War: Three Kingdoms as a strategy sandbox first and a showcase second, with historical and real-time strategy as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Archive-quality research and art direction
  • Control groups, hotkeys and formation orders
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  3. 03Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  4. 04Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  5. 05Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  6. 06Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  7. 07Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
