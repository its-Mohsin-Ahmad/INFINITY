The Evil Within 2

Tango Gameworks|Bethesda Softworks|2017|Horror · Action · Third Person · Story Rich
8.251,495 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

Tango Gameworks returns with The Evil Within 2, a horror title that leans hard into psychological horror and monster hunt. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. The Evil Within 2 is the rare horror release that treats psychological horror and monster hunt as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Optional one-life mode for the committed
  • Multiple endings driven by in-run choices
  • Finite resources that make every decision count
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Unreliable narration and shifting environments
  • Creature design grounded in practical-effect horror

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  3. 03Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  4. 04Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  5. 05Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  6. 06Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  7. 07Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
