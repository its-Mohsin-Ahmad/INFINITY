V Rising

Stunlock Studios|Stunlock Studios|2024|Survival · Action · Co-op · Open World
8.489,111 reviews
PCPS5

V Rising is what happens when Stunlock Studios commits fully to survival and monster hunt and crafting. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. V Rising is a survival experience from Stunlock Studios, built around monster hunt and crafting. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Day-night and seasonal cycles that change what is available
  • Co-op scaling that keeps the challenge intact for a full squad
  • Base building with structural and defensive layers
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Creature design grounded in practical-effect horror
  • Co-op progression is fully synced, including achievements and unlocks

Modes

single playercampaignmultiplayerco oponline coop

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  3. 03Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  4. 04Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  5. 05Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  6. 06Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  7. 07Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
