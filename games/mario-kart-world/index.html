Mario Kart World

Nintendo EPD|Nintendo|2025|Racing · Family · Multiplayer
9.1165,334 reviews
Switch

Mario Kart World pairs racing systems with kart racing and party, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Mario Kart World is the rare racing release that treats kart racing and party as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Rewind, ghost and rival-lap systems for repeat attempts
  • Tuning suite for gear ratios, suspension, camber and aero
  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Assist modes that any player in the room can enable
  • Cross-generation play across living-room platforms
  • Generous checkpointing for younger players
  • Grand Prix cups and time-trial leaderboards
  • Four-player chaos with rubber-banded stakes

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  3. 03Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  4. 04Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  5. 05Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  6. 06Race shorter events first so mistakes cost fewer laps.
  7. 07Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
