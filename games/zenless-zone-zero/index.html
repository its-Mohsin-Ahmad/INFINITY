Zenless Zone Zero

HoYoverse|HoYoverse|2024|Action · RPG
8.5133,496 reviews
PCPS5AndroidiOS

First launched in 2024, Zenless Zone Zero remains a reference point for action design built on roguelike and hack & slash. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Zenless Zone Zero is what happens when HoYoverse commits fully to action and roguelike and hack & slash. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Meta-progression that unlocks new starting options
  • Mid-combo weapon and ability switching

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  3. 03Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  4. 04Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  5. 05Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  6. 06Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  7. 07Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
