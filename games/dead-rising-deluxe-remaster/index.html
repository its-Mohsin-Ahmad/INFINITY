Dead Rising Deluxe Remaster

Capcom|Capcom|2024|Action · Horror · Sandbox
8.065,212 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Dead Rising Deluxe Remaster is the rare action release that treats monster hunt and sandbox craft as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Dead Rising Deluxe Remaster pairs action systems with monster hunt and sandbox craft, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Multiple endings driven by in-run choices
  • Persistent threats that learn and reposition
  • Optional one-life mode for the committed
  • Monster AI with sensory perception and stalking behaviour
  • Logic, wiring and automation systems

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  3. 03Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  4. 04Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  5. 05Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  6. 06Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  7. 07Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
