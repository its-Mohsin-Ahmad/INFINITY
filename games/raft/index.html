Raft

Redbeet Interactive|Axolot Games|2022|Survival · Co-op · Adventure
8.680,075 reviews
PC

Raft is a survival experience from Redbeet Interactive, built around crafting and naval. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Raft takes the survival blueprint and rebuilds it around crafting and naval. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Base building with structural and defensive layers
  • Environmental threats ranging from weather to predators
  • Day-night and seasonal cycles that change what is available
  • Friends Pass access so a squad only needs one copy
  • Drop-in and drop-out with join-in-progress checkpoints
  • Asymmetric roles that demand genuine coordination
  • Co-op progression is fully synced, including achievements and unlocks
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single playercampaignmultiplayerco oponline coop

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Establish shelter and a resource loop before pushing into higher-risk regions.
  2. 02Store duplicate resources — the inventory you lose on death is the one that hurts.
  3. 03Do not fight every fight. Running is a valid response to a bad engagement.
  4. 04Build a small base near resources but away from high-traffic enemy paths.
  5. 05Track day-night and weather cycles; both change which threats are active.
  6. 06Craft the basic tool set before exploring further than you can walk back.
  7. 07Prioritise food, water and shelter in the first hour; everything else is optional.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
