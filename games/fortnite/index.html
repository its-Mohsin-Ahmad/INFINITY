Fortnite

Epic Games|Epic Games|2017|Battle Royale · FPS · Multiplayer · Co-op
8.3108,624 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitchAndroid

Fortnite is the rare battle royale release that treats hero shooter and arena shooter as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Fortnite pairs battle royale systems with hero shooter and arena shooter, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Ranked progression with placement matches and decay
  • Squad play with pings, comms and role specialisation
  • Cross-play with input-based matchmaking pools
  • Weapon sandbox with distinct recoil, handling and TTK per class
  • Gunplay tuned for controller and mouse alike, with no aim-assist tax
  • Destruction and bullet penetration modelled on real materials
  • Role queue and clear team-composition feedback
  • Short match formats built for ranked play

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  3. 03Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  4. 04Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  5. 05Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  6. 06Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  7. 07Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
