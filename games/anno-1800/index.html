Anno 1800

Ubisoft Blue Byte|Ubisoft|2019|Strategy · Simulation
8.549,493 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Anno 1800 is a strategy experience from Ubisoft Blue Byte, built around management and city builder. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. First launched in 2019, Anno 1800 remains a reference point for strategy design built on management and city builder. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Doctrines and tech trees with genuine opportunity costs
  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Detailed statistics and analytics dashboards
  • Financial modelling with real consequences
  • Zoning, traffic and utility simulation at district scale

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  3. 03Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  4. 04Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  5. 05Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  6. 06Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  7. 07Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
