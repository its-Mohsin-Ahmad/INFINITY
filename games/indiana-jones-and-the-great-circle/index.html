Indiana Jones and the Great Circle

MachineGames|Bethesda Softworks|2024|Action · Adventure · Story Rich
8.890,494 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Released in 2024, Indiana Jones and the Great Circle is MachineGames's take on modern action design with detective and historical at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Indiana Jones and the Great Circle is what happens when MachineGames commits fully to action and detective and historical. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Observation and interrogation minigames
  • Archive-quality research and art direction

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  3. 03Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  4. 04Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  5. 05Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  6. 06Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  7. 07Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
