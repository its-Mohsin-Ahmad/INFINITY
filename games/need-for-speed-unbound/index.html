Need for Speed Unbound

Criterion Games|Electronic Arts|2022|Racing · Open World · Multiplayer
7.453,958 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Need for Speed Unbound is the rare racing release that treats arcade racing as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Need for Speed Unbound is a racing experience from Criterion Games, built around arcade racing. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Replay tooling with camera rigs and telemetry overlays
  • Ranked racing with licences and a clean-driving rating
  • Tuning suite for gear ratios, suspension, camber and aero
  • Cinematic camera and photo mode for capture enthusiasts
  • Fast travel that unlocks only once a zone has been explored
  • Seamless traversal with no loading transitions between regions
  • Boost, drift and nitro mechanics tuned for instant fun
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
  3. 03Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  4. 04Race shorter events first so mistakes cost fewer laps.
  5. 05Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
  6. 06Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  7. 07Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
