Dota 2

Valve|Valve|2013|Multiplayer · Strategy · Action
8.8153,356 reviews
PC

Dota 2 is the rare multiplayer release that treats moba and hero brawler as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Dota 2 is a multiplayer experience from Valve, built around moba and hero brawler. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Dedicated server architecture with a 60Hz+ tick rate
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Doctrines and tech trees with genuine opportunity costs
  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Draft and ban phases with role queue
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

single playermultiplayeronline pvpranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  3. 03Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  4. 04Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  5. 05Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  6. 06Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  7. 07Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
