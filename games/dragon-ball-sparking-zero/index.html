Dragon Ball: Sparking! Zero

Spike Chunsoft|Bandai Namco Entertainment|2024|Fighting · Action · Multiplayer
8.4100,504 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Dragon Ball: Sparking! Zero pairs fighting systems with arena shooter, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Dragon Ball: Sparking! Zero is the rare fighting release that treats arena shooter as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Full story mode with hand-animated cinematics
  • Ranked ladders with per-character rating
  • Rollback netcode with region-aware matchmaking
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Map control, pickups and respawn economy
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

single playermultiplayeronline pvpranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control spacing and frame advantage, then punish predictable behaviour with a full combo.
  2. 02Learn one character's normals, one anti-air and one reliable punish.
  3. 03Play long sets rather than first-to-one; adaptation is the real skill.
  4. 04Use training mode's frame data display to check whether moves are safe on block.
  5. 05Set a ranked goal per session and stop when you hit it, win or lose.
  6. 06Watch your own replays and count how often you press a button while minus.
  7. 07Practise blocking low attacks on reaction, then add delayed tech.
