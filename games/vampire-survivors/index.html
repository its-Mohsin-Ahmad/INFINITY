Vampire Survivors

poncle|poncle|2022|Indie · Action
8.8104,713 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitchAndroidiOS

Released in 2022, Vampire Survivors is poncle's take on modern independent design with roguelike and bullet hell at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. poncle returns with Vampire Survivors, a independent title that leans hard into roguelike and bullet hell. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Full controller and Steam Deck verification
  • Accessibility options shipped on day one
  • Original score with adaptive audio systems
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Procedurally arranged runs built from hand-authored templates
  • Ranked scoring with graze and chain mechanics

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  3. 03Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  4. 04Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  5. 05Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  6. 06Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  7. 07Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
