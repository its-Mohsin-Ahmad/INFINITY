Marvel's Spider-Man 2

Insomniac Games|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2023|Action · Adventure · Open World
9.0105,188 reviews
PS5PC

Released in 2023, Marvel's Spider-Man 2 is Insomniac Games's take on modern action design with superhero and beat 'em up at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Marvel's Spider-Man 2 is the rare action release that treats superhero and beat 'em up as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Dual hero and villain narrative beats
  • Crowd-control combat with juggles and throws

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  3. 03Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  4. 04Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  5. 05Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  6. 06Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  7. 07Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
