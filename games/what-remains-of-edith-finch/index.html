What Remains of Edith Finch

Giant Sparrow|Annapurna Interactive|2017|Adventure · Story Rich
8.955,770 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Giant Sparrow built What Remains of Edith Finch as a adventure sandbox first and a showcase second, with mystery and narrative adventure as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Giant Sparrow returns with What Remains of Edith Finch, a adventure title that leans hard into mystery and narrative adventure. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Chapter select and scene replay after completion
  • Choice systems that alter relationships, not only endings
  • Narrative difficulty options for combat-averse players
  • Clue chains that can be solved out of order
  • Diegetic UI and in-world documentation

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  3. 03Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  4. 04Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  5. 05Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  6. 06Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  7. 07Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
