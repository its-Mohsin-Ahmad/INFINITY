Wreckfest

Bugbear Entertainment|THQ Nordic|2018|Racing · Simulation · Multiplayer
8.458,098 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Wreckfest is a racing experience from Bugbear Entertainment, built around sim racing and physics puzzle. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Wreckfest takes the racing blueprint and rebuilds it around sim racing and physics puzzle. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Ranked racing with licences and a clean-driving rating
  • Tuning suite for gear ratios, suspension, camber and aero
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Dynamic events and disasters that force replanning
  • Force-feedback profiles and telemetry output
  • Sandbox levels for experimenting with mechanics

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  3. 03Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
  4. 04Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  5. 05Drive a single car for ten laps on a single circuit before changing either variable.
  6. 06Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
  7. 07Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
