The Quarry

Supermassive Games|2K|2022|Horror · Adventure · Story Rich
7.658,098 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Released in 2022, The Quarry is Supermassive Games's take on modern horror design with monster hunt and visual novel at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. First launched in 2022, The Quarry remains a reference point for horror design built on monster hunt and visual novel. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Optional one-life mode for the committed
  • Sanity, light and stamina systems that raise tension
  • Sound design that prioritises spatial cues over cheap jumps
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Creature design grounded in practical-effect horror
  • Multiple voiced routes and gallery unlocks

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Manage light, noise and inventory so that every encounter is one you chose to pick.
  2. 02Keep your inventory light enough to pick up anything you find.
  3. 03Take breaks. Tension degrades decision-making faster than it degrades the game.
  4. 04Play with headphones. Spatial audio is the primary information channel in this game.
  5. 05Set brightness deliberately — too dark hides mechanics, too bright kills the atmosphere.
  6. 06Check every room for resources, but never linger once you have what you need.
  7. 07Note where threats patrol; routes that were dangerous early often become quiet later.
