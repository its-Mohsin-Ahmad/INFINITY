Rise of the Ronin

Team Ninja|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2024|Action · RPG · Open World
7.757,095 reviews
PCPS5

Rise of the Ronin is a action experience from Team Ninja, built around soulslike and historical. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. From Team Ninja, Rise of the Ronin is a action game where soulslike and historical drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Lost-currency retrieval and shortcut-driven level design
  • Historically grounded settings, equipment and tactics

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  3. 03Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  4. 04Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  5. 05Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  6. 06Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  7. 07Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
