Valheim

Iron Gate Studio|Coffee Stain Publishing|2021|Survival · Co-op · Sandbox
9.0115,055 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|SXbox One

Valheim is what happens when Iron Gate Studio commits fully to survival and crafting and fantasy. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Iron Gate Studio built Valheim as a survival sandbox first and a showcase second, with crafting and fantasy as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Online play is the main event, with dedicated servers and skill-based matchmaking. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Crafting trees spanning tools, shelter, medicine and vehicles
  • Deep inventory and weight management
  • Environmental threats ranging from weather to predators
  • Proximity voice with optional speech-to-text
  • Asymmetric roles that demand genuine coordination
  • Difficulty that scales with player count
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours
  • Co-op progression is fully synced, including achievements and unlocks

Modes

multiplayerco oponline coopsandboxcross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  3. 03Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  4. 04Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  5. 05Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  6. 06Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  7. 07Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  8. 08Platform note — 4 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
