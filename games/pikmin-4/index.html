Pikmin 4

Nintendo EPD|Nintendo|2023|Strategy · Adventure · Family
8.870,163 reviews
Switch

In Pikmin 4, Nintendo EPD uses strategy conventions as a foundation and real-time strategy and party as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Pikmin 4 marks another strategy entry from Nintendo EPD, and real-time strategy and party is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Base building and unit composition in real time
  • Four-player chaos with rubber-banded stakes

Modes

single playercampaignlocal coop

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  3. 03Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  4. 04Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  5. 05Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  6. 06Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  7. 07Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
