Dead Cells

Motion Twin|Motion Twin|2018|Indie · Action
9.089,447 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitchAndroidiOS

Dead Cells is what happens when Motion Twin commits fully to independent and roguelike and platformer. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Motion Twin returns with Dead Cells, a independent title that leans hard into roguelike and platformer. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Regular post-launch updates funded by the community
  • Hand-crafted art direction with a signature visual identity
  • Full controller and Steam Deck verification
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Procedurally arranged runs built from hand-authored templates
  • Collectibles and time trials per stage

single playercross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  3. 03Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  4. 04Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  5. 05Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  6. 06Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  7. 07Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
