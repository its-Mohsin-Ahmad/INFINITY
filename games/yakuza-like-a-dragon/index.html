Yakuza: Like a Dragon

Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio|Sega|2020|RPG · Adventure · Story Rich
8.758,992 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio built Yakuza: Like a Dragon as a role-playing sandbox first and a showcase second, with turn-based rpg and beat 'em up as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. From Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio, Yakuza: Like a Dragon is a role-playing game where turn-based rpg and beat 'em up drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Turn-order manipulation and status stacking
  • Co-op brawling with shared combo meters

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  3. 03Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  4. 04Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  5. 05Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  6. 06Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  7. 07Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
