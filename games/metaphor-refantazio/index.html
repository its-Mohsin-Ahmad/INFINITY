Metaphor: ReFantazio

Atlus|Sega|2024|RPG · Story Rich
9.2128,517 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|S

Released in 2024, Metaphor: ReFantazio is Atlus's take on modern role-playing design with fantasy and turn-based rpg at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Metaphor: ReFantazio marks another role-playing entry from Atlus, and fantasy and turn-based rpg is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Performance-captured dialogue with a full ensemble cast
  • Narrative difficulty options for combat-averse players
  • Chapter select and scene replay after completion
  • Magic schools with distinct resource economies
  • Party composition with elemental weaknesses

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  3. 03Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  4. 04Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  5. 05Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  6. 06Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  7. 07Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
