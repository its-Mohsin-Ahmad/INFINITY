Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas

Rockstar North|Rockstar Games|2005|Action · Open World · Story Rich
9.065,380 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneAndroidiOS

Rockstar North returns with Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas, a action title that leans hard into heist and historical. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. First launched in 2005, Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas remains a reference point for action design built on heist and historical. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Discovery-led design — landmarks are the map marker
  • Seamless traversal with no loading transitions between regions
  • Distinct biomes with their own wildlife, weather and factions
  • Loud or quiet approaches with different payouts
  • Historically grounded settings, equipment and tactics

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  3. 03Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  4. 04Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  5. 05Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  6. 06Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  7. 07Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  8. 08Platform note — 5 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
