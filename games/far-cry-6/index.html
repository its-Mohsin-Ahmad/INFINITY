Far Cry 6

Ubisoft Toronto|Ubisoft|2021|Action · FPS · Open World
7.450,818 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Ubisoft Toronto built Far Cry 6 as a action sandbox first and a showcase second, with tactical shooter and military as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Far Cry 6 takes the action blueprint and rebuilds it around tactical shooter and military. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Full-spectrum audio with occlusion and distance falloff
  • Gunplay tuned for controller and mouse alike, with no aim-assist tax
  • Sensitivity, FOV and controller response fully configurable
  • Utility-first combat where information beats raw aim
  • Cross-platform play and progression across every supported storefront

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  3. 03Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  4. 04Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  5. 05Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  6. 06Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  7. 07Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  8. 08Platform note — 5 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
