Encased: A Sci-Fi Post-Apocalyptic RPG

Dark Crystal Games|Prime Matter|2021|RPG · Strategy
7.553,504 reviews
PC

First launched in 2021, Encased: A Sci-Fi Post-Apocalyptic RPG remains a reference point for role-playing design built on post-apocalyptic and turn-based rpg. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. In Encased: A Sci-Fi Post-Apocalyptic RPG, Dark Crystal Games uses role-playing conventions as a foundation and post-apocalyptic and turn-based rpg as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Doctrines and tech trees with genuine opportunity costs
  • Mod support with exposed data files and tooling
  • Improvised gear and radiation systems
  • Turn-order manipulation and status stacking

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  3. 03Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  4. 04Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  5. 05Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  6. 06Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  7. 07Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
