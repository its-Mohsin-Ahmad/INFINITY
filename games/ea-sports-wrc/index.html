EA Sports WRC

Codemasters|Electronic Arts|2023|Racing · Simulation · Sports
8.045,895 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

First launched in 2023, EA Sports WRC remains a reference point for racing design built on sim racing and management. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. EA Sports WRC pairs racing systems with sim racing and management, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Ranked racing with licences and a clean-driving rating
  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Replay tooling with camera rigs and telemetry overlays
  • Detailed statistics and analytics dashboards
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Tyre temperature and fuel-load strategy across race distance
  • Staff, morale and logistics across departments

Modes

single playerranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Drive a single car for ten laps on a single circuit before changing either variable.
  3. 03Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  4. 04Race shorter events first so mistakes cost fewer laps.
  5. 05Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  6. 06Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  7. 07Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
