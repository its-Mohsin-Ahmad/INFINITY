Starfield

Bethesda Game Studios|Bethesda Softworks|2023|RPG · Open World · FPS · Story Rich
7.882,088 reviews
PCXbox X|S

First launched in 2023, Starfield remains a reference point for role-playing design built on space rpg and sci-fi. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Starfield is the rare role-playing release that treats space rpg and sci-fi as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Cinematic camera and photo mode for capture enthusiasts
  • Seamless traversal with no loading transitions between regions
  • Discovery-led design — landmarks are the map marker
  • Hardware, ship and station design language
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  3. 03Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  4. 04Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  5. 05Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  6. 06Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  7. 07Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
