Rise of Kingdoms

Lilith Games|Lilith Games|2018|Strategy · Simulation · Multiplayer
7.262,146 reviews
AndroidiOS

Rise of Kingdoms takes the strategy blueprint and rebuilds it around 4x and historical. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. From Lilith Games, Rise of Kingdoms is a strategy game where 4x and historical drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Doctrines and tech trees with genuine opportunity costs
  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Mod support with exposed data files and tooling
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Detailed statistics and analytics dashboards
  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Diplomacy with treaties, vassals and espionage
  • Historically grounded settings, equipment and tactics

single playermultiplayeronline pvpranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  3. 03Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  4. 04Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  5. 05Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  6. 06Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  7. 07Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
