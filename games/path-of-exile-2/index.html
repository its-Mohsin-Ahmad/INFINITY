Path of Exile 2

Grinding Gear Games|Grinding Gear Games|2024|RPG · Action · MMORPG
8.897,003 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Grinding Gear Games returns with Path of Exile 2, a role-playing title that leans hard into action rpg and looter shooter. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Path of Exile 2 marks another role-playing entry from Grinding Gear Games, and action rpg and looter shooter is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Real-time combat with stat-driven damage maths
  • Build-defining weapon and gear sets

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  3. 03Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  4. 04Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  5. 05Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  6. 06Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  7. 07Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
