Xenoblade Chronicles 3

Monolith Soft|Nintendo|2022|RPG · Story Rich · Open World
9.090,951 reviews
Switch

Xenoblade Chronicles 3 is the rare role-playing release that treats action rpg and fantasy as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. From Monolith Soft, Xenoblade Chronicles 3 is a role-playing game where action rpg and fantasy drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Choice systems that alter relationships, not only endings
  • Environmental storytelling layered into every set
  • Performance-captured dialogue with a full ensemble cast
  • Loot affixes that reshape entire builds
  • Magic schools with distinct resource economies

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  3. 03Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  4. 04Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  5. 05Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  6. 06Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  7. 07Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
