God of War

Santa Monica Studio|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2018|Action · Adventure · Story Rich
9.578,695 reviews
PCPS4

In God of War, Santa Monica Studio uses action conventions as a foundation and hack & slash and fantasy as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. God of War is what happens when Santa Monica Studio commits fully to action and hack & slash and fantasy. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Combo chains with style-ranking feedback
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  3. 03Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  4. 04Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  5. 05Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  6. 06Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  7. 07Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
