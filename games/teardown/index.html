Teardown

Tuxedo Labs|Tuxedo Labs|2022|Sandbox · Puzzle · Action
8.6122,439 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Teardown is the rare sandbox release that treats physics puzzle and heist as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Tuxedo Labs built Teardown as a sandbox sandbox first and a showcase second, with physics puzzle and heist as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Progression-free play where creativity is the reward
  • Logic, wiring and scripting systems for advanced creators
  • Moderation and reporting tooling for shared spaces
  • Level editor with sharing and curated picks
  • Leaderboards, ghosts and speedrun timers
  • Zero-fail design that keeps the emphasis on thinking
  • Systems-first puzzles with many valid solutions
  • Multi-stage operations with planning, infiltration and escape

Modes

single playersandbox

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  3. 03Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  4. 04Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  5. 05Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  6. 06Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  7. 07Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
