Fallout: New Vegas

Obsidian Entertainment|Bethesda Softworks|2010|RPG · Open World · FPS · Story Rich
9.067,205 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

Fallout: New Vegas is what happens when Obsidian Entertainment commits fully to role-playing and post-apocalyptic and action rpg. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Fallout: New Vegas is the rare role-playing release that treats post-apocalyptic and action rpg as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Discovery-led design — landmarks are the map marker
  • A living map of dynamic events, convoys and emergent encounters
  • Distinct biomes with their own wildlife, weather and factions
  • Scavenging in ruined landscapes under faction pressure
  • Loot affixes that reshape entire builds

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  3. 03Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  4. 04Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  5. 05Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  6. 06Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  7. 07Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
