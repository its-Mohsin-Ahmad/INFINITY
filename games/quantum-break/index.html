Quantum Break

Remedy Entertainment|Xbox Game Studios|2016|Action · Third Person · Story Rich
7.940,380 reviews
PCXbox X|SXbox One

Quantum Break takes the action blueprint and rebuilds it around cover shooter and sci-fi. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Quantum Break marks another action entry from Remedy Entertainment, and cover shooter and sci-fi is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Camera tuned for both close-range melee and long-range shooting
  • Cover-driven combat with contextual vaults, slides and dodges
  • Cinematic camera work that never takes control away
  • Squad commands issued on the fly
  • Speculative technology with in-world justification

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  3. 03Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  4. 04Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  5. 05Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  6. 06Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  7. 07Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
