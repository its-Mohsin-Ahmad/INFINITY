NieR: Automata

PlatinumGames|Square Enix|2017|Action · RPG · Story Rich
9.1131,135 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

NieR: Automata takes the action blueprint and rebuilds it around hack & slash and sci-fi. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. From PlatinumGames, NieR: Automata is a action game where hack & slash and sci-fi drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Combo chains with style-ranking feedback
  • Speculative technology with in-world justification

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  3. 03Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  4. 04Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  5. 05Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  6. 06Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  7. 07Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
