The Lords of the Fallen

Hexworks|CI Games|2023|RPG · Action
7.556,457 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

The Lords of the Fallen is a role-playing experience from Hexworks, built around soulslike and fantasy. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. In The Lords of the Fallen, Hexworks uses role-playing conventions as a foundation and soulslike and fantasy as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Lost-currency retrieval and shortcut-driven level design
  • Magic schools with distinct resource economies

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  3. 03Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  4. 04Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  5. 05Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  6. 06Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  7. 07Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
