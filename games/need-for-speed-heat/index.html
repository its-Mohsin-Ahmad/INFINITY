Need for Speed Heat

Ghost Games|Electronic Arts|2019|Racing · Open World
7.639,194 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

Need for Speed Heat is a racing experience from Ghost Games, built around arcade racing. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Need for Speed Heat marks another racing entry from Ghost Games, and arcade racing is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Dynamic time of day and weather across the full calendar
  • Tuning suite for gear ratios, suspension, camber and aero
  • Ranked racing with licences and a clean-driving rating
  • Distinct biomes with their own wildlife, weather and factions
  • Fast travel that unlocks only once a zone has been explored
  • Seamless traversal with no loading transitions between regions
  • Ghost races against friends and rivals
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
  3. 03Drive a single car for ten laps on a single circuit before changing either variable.
  4. 04Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  5. 05Race shorter events first so mistakes cost fewer laps.
  6. 06Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
  7. 07Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
