Persona 5 Royal

Atlus|Sega|2020|RPG · Story Rich
9.5122,666 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Persona 5 Royal takes the role-playing blueprint and rebuilds it around turn-based rpg and visual novel. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. First launched in 2020, Persona 5 Royal remains a reference point for role-playing design built on turn-based rpg and visual novel. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Narrative difficulty options for combat-averse players
  • Chapter select and scene replay after completion
  • Performance-captured dialogue with a full ensemble cast
  • Party composition with elemental weaknesses
  • Multiple voiced routes and gallery unlocks

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  3. 03Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  4. 04Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  5. 05Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  6. 06Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  7. 07Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
