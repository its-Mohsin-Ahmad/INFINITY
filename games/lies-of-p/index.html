Lies of P

Round8 Studio|Neowiz|2023|RPG · Action
8.391,691 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Lies of P marks another role-playing entry from Round8 Studio, and soulslike and stealth is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Lies of P is what happens when Round8 Studio commits fully to role-playing and soulslike and stealth. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Stamina-driven combat with precise dodge timing
  • Ghost, assault and mixed approach scoring

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  3. 03Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  4. 04Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  5. 05Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  6. 06Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  7. 07Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  8. 08Platform note — 5 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
