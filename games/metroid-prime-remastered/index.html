Metroid Prime Remastered

Retro Studios|Nintendo|2023|FPS · Adventure · Action
9.176,000 reviews
Switch

In Metroid Prime Remastered, Retro Studios uses first-person shooter conventions as a foundation and metroidvania and sci-fi as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Metroid Prime Remastered is the rare first-person shooter release that treats metroidvania and sci-fi as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Custom loadouts with attachment-level trade-offs
  • Sensitivity, FOV and controller response fully configurable
  • Gunplay tuned for controller and mouse alike, with no aim-assist tax
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Ability-gated world that loops back on itself
  • Speculative technology with in-world justification

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  3. 03Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  4. 04Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  5. 05Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  6. 06Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  7. 07Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
