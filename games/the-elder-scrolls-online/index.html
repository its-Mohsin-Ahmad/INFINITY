The Elder Scrolls Online

ZeniMax Online Studios|Bethesda Softworks|2014|MMORPG · RPG · Open World
8.141,502 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

The Elder Scrolls Online pairs massively multiplayer systems with fantasy and action rpg, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. From ZeniMax Online Studios, The Elder Scrolls Online is a massively multiplayer game where fantasy and action rpg drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Social tooling — emotes, housing, guild halls and banks
  • Group content from small dungeons to large-format raids
  • Faction, guild and territory systems with real stakes
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Magic schools with distinct resource economies
  • Loot affixes that reshape entire builds

single playercampaignmultiplayercross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  3. 03Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  4. 04Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  5. 05Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  6. 06Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  7. 07Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
