Cricket 24: The Official Game of the Ashes

Big Ant Studios|Nacon|2023|Sports · Simulation · Multiplayer
7.495,746 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Cricket 24: The Official Game of the Ashes takes the sports blueprint and rebuilds it around cricket and management. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Big Ant Studios returns with Cricket 24: The Official Game of the Ashes, a sports title that leans hard into cricket and management. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Skill-based timing mechanics that separate the ranks
  • Configurable tactics, formations and set-piece routines
  • Broadcast-quality presentation with commentary packages
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Detailed statistics and analytics dashboards
  • Ball-by-ball simulation with pitch and weather effects
  • Financial modelling with real consequences

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control tempo, keep shape and take the highest-percentage option available in each phase of play.
  2. 02Move online once the fundamentals feel routine, and expect the pace to jump.
  3. 03Use the simpler difficulty for one full season to learn the systems without heavy punishment.
  4. 04Choose a formation or tactic that matches how you actually play, not the most popular preset.
  5. 05Master one skill move or set-piece routine and use it until it becomes automatic.
  6. 06Study the match stats after each game — possession and pass completion tell the story.
  7. 07Rotate your squad deliberately; stamina and form matter more than raw ratings.
