Tales of Graces f Remastered

Tose|Bandai Namco Entertainment|2025|RPG · Action · Story Rich
8.170,234 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Tales of Graces f Remastered is what happens when Tose commits fully to role-playing and action rpg and fantasy. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Tales of Graces f Remastered pairs role-playing systems with action rpg and fantasy, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Real-time combat with stat-driven damage maths
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  3. 03Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  4. 04Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  5. 05Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  6. 06Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  7. 07Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
