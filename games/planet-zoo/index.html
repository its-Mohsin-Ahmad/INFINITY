Planet Zoo

Frontier Developments|Frontier Developments|2019|Simulation
8.458,738 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Released in 2019, Planet Zoo is Frontier Developments's take on modern simulation design with management and city builder at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Planet Zoo is what happens when Frontier Developments commits fully to simulation and management and city builder. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Long-tail progression measured in hundreds of hours
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Staff, morale and logistics across departments
  • Zoning, traffic and utility simulation at district scale
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Balance throughput against cost, and expand only as fast as logistics can sustain.
  2. 02Set up the first resource chain end to end before adding a second.
  3. 03Watch the analytics panels to find the bottleneck rather than expanding everything at once.
  4. 04Play one long session with a single goal in mind; simulation games reward clear objectives.
  5. 05Save often, and label saves before large redesigns.
  6. 06Once the basics are automatic, increase demand and see which system fails first.
  7. 07Start in sandbox or creative mode to learn the building and automation tools without pressure.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
