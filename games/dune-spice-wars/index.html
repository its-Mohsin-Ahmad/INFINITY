Dune: Spice Wars

Shiro Games|Funcom|2023|Strategy
8.065,418 reviews
PC

Shiro Games returns with Dune: Spice Wars, a strategy title that leans hard into real-time strategy and sci-fi. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Shiro Games built Dune: Spice Wars as a strategy sandbox first and a showcase second, with real-time strategy and sci-fi as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • Mod support with exposed data files and tooling
  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Control groups, hotkeys and formation orders
  • Speculative technology with in-world justification
  • Diplomacy with treaties, vassals and espionage
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  3. 03Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  4. 04Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  5. 05Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  6. 06Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  7. 07Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
