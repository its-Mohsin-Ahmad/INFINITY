Dying Light 2: Stay Human

Techland|Techland|2022|Action · Survival · Horror · Open World
7.996,811 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Dying Light 2: Stay Human marks another action entry from Techland, and monster hunt and post-apocalyptic is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. From Techland, Dying Light 2: Stay Human is a action game where monster hunt and post-apocalyptic drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Deep inventory and weight management
  • Crafting trees spanning tools, shelter, medicine and vehicles
  • Co-op scaling that keeps the challenge intact for a full squad
  • Monster AI with sensory perception and stalking behaviour
  • Scavenging in ruined landscapes under faction pressure

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  3. 03Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  4. 04Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  5. 05Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  6. 06Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  7. 07Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
