Dying Light: The Beast

Techland|Techland|2025|Action · Survival · Horror · Open World
8.3125,018 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Techland built Dying Light: The Beast as a action sandbox first and a showcase second, with monster hunt and post-apocalyptic as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. First launched in 2025, Dying Light: The Beast remains a reference point for action design built on monster hunt and post-apocalyptic. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Co-op scaling that keeps the challenge intact for a full squad
  • Crafting trees spanning tools, shelter, medicine and vehicles
  • Deep inventory and weight management
  • Creature design grounded in practical-effect horror
  • Scavenging in ruined landscapes under faction pressure

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  3. 03Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  4. 04Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  5. 05Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  6. 06Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  7. 07Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
