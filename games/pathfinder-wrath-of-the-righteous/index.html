Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous

Owlcat Games|Owlcat Games|2021|RPG · Strategy · Story Rich
8.446,726 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

In Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous, Owlcat Games uses role-playing conventions as a foundation and turn-based rpg and fantasy as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous takes the role-playing blueprint and rebuilds it around turn-based rpg and fantasy. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • Doctrines and tech trees with genuine opportunity costs
  • Mod support with exposed data files and tooling
  • Party composition with elemental weaknesses
  • Magic schools with distinct resource economies

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  3. 03Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  4. 04Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  5. 05Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  6. 06Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  7. 07Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
