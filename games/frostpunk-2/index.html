Frostpunk 2

11 bit studios|11 bit studios|2024|Strategy · Simulation · Survival
8.480,291 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Frostpunk 2 is what happens when 11 bit studios commits fully to strategy and city builder and management. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Frostpunk 2 takes the strategy blueprint and rebuilds it around city builder and management. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Mod support with exposed data files and tooling
  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Long-tail progression measured in hundreds of hours
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Policy and taxation systems with visible effects
  • Staff, morale and logistics across departments

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  3. 03Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  4. 04Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  5. 05Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  6. 06Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  7. 07Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
