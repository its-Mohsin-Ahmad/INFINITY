Chained Together

Anegar Games|Anegar Games|2024|Co-op
7.964,069 reviews
PC

Chained Together is the rare co-operative release that treats platformer and party as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Chained Together pairs co-operative systems with platformer and party, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Online play is the main event, with dedicated servers and skill-based matchmaking. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Friends Pass access so a squad only needs one copy
  • Difficulty that scales with player count
  • Asymmetric roles that demand genuine coordination
  • Precision movement with coyote time and air control
  • Custom rules and round modifiers
  • Sandbox levels for experimenting with mechanics
  • Co-op progression is fully synced, including achievements and unlocks

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Keep the group laughing while quietly engineering the round-ending play.
  2. 02If someone is struggling, hand them the support role; it is usually the most valuable one.
  3. 03Assign roles based on what each player enjoys, not on who is objectively best.
  4. 04Use voice or proximity chat — these games are built on communication.
  5. 05Keep sessions to ninety minutes or less; comedy has diminishing returns.
  6. 06Screenshot the results screen. You will want them later.
  7. 07Try one full round with default rules before adding modifiers.
