Resident Evil 2

Capcom|Capcom|2019|Horror · Action · Story Rich · Third Person
9.193,298 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Resident Evil 2 is the rare horror release that treats survival horror and detective as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Capcom returns with Resident Evil 2, a horror title that leans hard into survival horror and detective. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Finite resources that make every decision count
  • Multiple endings driven by in-run choices
  • Optional one-life mode for the committed
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Ammo scarcity and inventory management under pressure
  • Evidence board with deduction linking

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  3. 03Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  4. 04Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  5. 05Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  6. 06Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  7. 07Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  8. 08Platform note — 5 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
