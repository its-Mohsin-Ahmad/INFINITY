Astral Chain

PlatinumGames|Nintendo|2019|Action · Adventure · Story Rich
8.753,222 reviews
Switch

Astral Chain is what happens when PlatinumGames commits fully to action and hack & slash and sci-fi. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. In Astral Chain, PlatinumGames uses action conventions as a foundation and hack & slash and sci-fi as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Combo chains with style-ranking feedback
  • Speculative technology with in-world justification

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  3. 03Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  4. 04Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  5. 05Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  6. 06Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  7. 07Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
