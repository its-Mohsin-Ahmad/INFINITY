Octopath Traveler II

Acquire|Square Enix|2023|RPG · Story Rich
8.667,015 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

From Acquire, Octopath Traveler II is a role-playing game where turn-based rpg and fantasy drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Octopath Traveler II is the rare role-playing release that treats turn-based rpg and fantasy as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Director commentary and behind-the-scenes features
  • Performance-captured dialogue with a full ensemble cast
  • Narrative difficulty options for combat-averse players
  • Turn-order manipulation and status stacking
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  3. 03Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  4. 04Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  5. 05Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
  6. 06Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  7. 07Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
