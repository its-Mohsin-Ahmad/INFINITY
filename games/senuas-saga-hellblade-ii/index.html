Senua's Saga: Hellblade II

Ninja Theory|Xbox Game Studios|2024|Action · Adventure · Story Rich
8.396,919 reviews
PCXbox X|S

First launched in 2024, Senua's Saga: Hellblade II remains a reference point for action design built on psychological horror and narrative adventure. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Senua's Saga: Hellblade II is what happens when Ninja Theory commits fully to action and psychological horror and narrative adventure. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Unreliable narration and shifting environments
  • Exploration-first pacing with no fail states

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  3. 03Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  4. 04Learn which attacks are unblockable; those are the ones you must dodge, not parry.
  5. 05Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  6. 06Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  7. 07Keep a ranged or throwable option for enemies that punish pure melee aggression.
