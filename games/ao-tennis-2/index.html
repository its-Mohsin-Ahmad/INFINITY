AO Tennis 2

Big Ant Studios|Nacon|2020|Sports · Simulation · Multiplayer
7.142,780 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

Released in 2020, AO Tennis 2 is Big Ant Studios's take on modern sports design with management and party at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. From Big Ant Studios, AO Tennis 2 is a sports game where management and party drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Licensed clubs, athletes and authentic competition structures
  • Skill-based timing mechanics that separate the ranks
  • Online seasons with promotion and relegation ladders
  • Dynamic events and disasters that force replanning
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Financial modelling with real consequences
  • Custom rules and round modifiers

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control tempo, keep shape and take the highest-percentage option available in each phase of play.
  2. 02Play the training drills before your first competitive match so the timing windows are clear.
  3. 03Master one skill move or set-piece routine and use it until it becomes automatic.
  4. 04Move online once the fundamentals feel routine, and expect the pace to jump.
  5. 05Rotate your squad deliberately; stamina and form matter more than raw ratings.
  6. 06Adjust sliders gradually, one value per match, to find your preferred balance.
  7. 07Use the simpler difficulty for one full season to learn the systems without heavy punishment.
