Returnal

Housemarque|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2021|Action · Third Person
8.557,723 reviews
PCPS5

Housemarque returns with Returnal, a action title that leans hard into roguelike and bullet hell. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. In Returnal, Housemarque uses action conventions as a foundation and roguelike and bullet hell as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Cover-driven combat with contextual vaults, slides and dodges
  • Photo mode with pose, lens and lighting controls
  • Cinematic camera work that never takes control away
  • Procedurally arranged runs built from hand-authored templates
  • Pattern reading with tight, forgiving hitboxes

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  3. 03Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  4. 04Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  5. 05Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  6. 06Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  7. 07Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
