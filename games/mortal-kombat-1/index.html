Mortal Kombat 1

NetherRealm Studios|Warner Bros. Games|2023|Fighting · Multiplayer · Action · Story Rich
8.3110,914 reviews

From NetherRealm Studios, Mortal Kombat 1 is a fighting game where arena shooter and monster hunt drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Mortal Kombat 1 is a fighting experience from NetherRealm Studios, built around arena shooter and monster hunt. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Rollback netcode with region-aware matchmaking
  • Extensive customisation across outfits and colour channels
  • Tutorial and combo challenges for every character archetype
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Dedicated server architecture with a 60Hz+ tick rate
  • Short match formats built for ranked play
  • Monster AI with sensory perception and stalking behaviour

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control spacing and frame advantage, then punish predictable behaviour with a full combo.
  2. 02Set a ranked goal per session and stop when you hit it, win or lose.
  3. 03Complete the tutorial and the character-specific combo trials before ranked.
  4. 04Watch your own replays and count how often you press a button while minus.
  5. 05Play long sets rather than first-to-one; adaptation is the real skill.
  6. 06Learn one character's normals, one anti-air and one reliable punish.
  7. 07Practise blocking low attacks on reaction, then add delayed tech.
