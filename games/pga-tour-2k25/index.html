PGA Tour 2K25

HB Studios|2K|2025|Sports · Simulation · Multiplayer
7.968,190 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

PGA Tour 2K25 marks another sports entry from HB Studios, and golf and management is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. PGA Tour 2K25 is a sports experience from HB Studios, built around golf and management. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Broadcast-quality presentation with commentary packages
  • A full career mode with transfers, training and press duties
  • Configurable tactics, formations and set-piece routines
  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Shot shaping with wind, lie and ball physics
  • Financial modelling with real consequences
