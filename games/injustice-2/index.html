Injustice 2

NetherRealm Studios|Warner Bros. Games|2017|Fighting · Multiplayer · Action · Story Rich
8.847,585 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

Injustice 2 is what happens when NetherRealm Studios commits fully to fighting and superhero and arena shooter. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. First launched in 2017, Injustice 2 remains a reference point for fighting design built on superhero and arena shooter. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Tutorial and combo challenges for every character archetype
  • Extensive customisation across outfits and colour channels
  • Rollback netcode with region-aware matchmaking
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Skill-based matchmaking with transparent rating tiers
  • Full stat tracking with match history and replays
  • Dual hero and villain narrative beats
  • Short match formats built for ranked play

single playercampaignmultiplayeronline pvpranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control spacing and frame advantage, then punish predictable behaviour with a full combo.
  2. 02Watch your own replays and count how often you press a button while minus.
  3. 03Set a ranked goal per session and stop when you hit it, win or lose.
  4. 04Learn the matchup for the character you lose to most before learning a new fighter.
  5. 05Use training mode's frame data display to check whether moves are safe on block.
  6. 06Practise blocking low attacks on reaction, then add delayed tech.
  7. 07Play long sets rather than first-to-one; adaptation is the real skill.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
