Company of Heroes 3

Relic Entertainment|Sega|2023|Strategy · Action
8.264,566 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Company of Heroes 3 is what happens when Relic Entertainment commits fully to strategy and historical and real-time strategy. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Company of Heroes 3 marks another strategy entry from Relic Entertainment, and historical and real-time strategy is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Doctrines and tech trees with genuine opportunity costs
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Historically grounded settings, equipment and tactics
  • Control groups, hotkeys and formation orders

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  3. 03Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  4. 04Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  5. 05Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  6. 06Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  7. 07Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
