Layers of Fear

Bloober Team|Bloober Team|2023|Horror · Adventure · Story Rich
7.444,991 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Layers of Fear is a horror experience from Bloober Team, built around psychological horror and narrative adventure. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Layers of Fear marks another horror entry from Bloober Team, and psychological horror and narrative adventure is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Sound design that prioritises spatial cues over cheap jumps
  • Persistent threats that learn and reposition
  • Finite resources that make every decision count
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Unreliable narration and shifting environments
  • Diegetic UI and in-world documentation

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Manage light, noise and inventory so that every encounter is one you chose to pick.
  2. 02Set brightness deliberately — too dark hides mechanics, too bright kills the atmosphere.
  3. 03Note where threats patrol; routes that were dangerous early often become quiet later.
  4. 04Learn the safe room locations and treat them as planning points, not just save points.
  5. 05Play with headphones. Spatial audio is the primary information channel in this game.
  6. 06Take breaks. Tension degrades decision-making faster than it degrades the game.
  7. 07Check every room for resources, but never linger once you have what you need.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
