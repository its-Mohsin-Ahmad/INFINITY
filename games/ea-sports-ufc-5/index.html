EA Sports UFC 5

EA Vancouver|Electronic Arts|2023|Sports · Fighting · Simulation
7.648,738 reviews
PS5Xbox X|S

EA Sports UFC 5 is a sports experience from EA Vancouver, built around combat sports and management. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. From EA Vancouver, EA Sports UFC 5 is a sports game where combat sports and management drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Online seasons with promotion and relegation ladders
  • Licensed clubs, athletes and authentic competition structures
  • Broadcast-quality presentation with commentary packages
  • Extensive customisation across outfits and colour channels
  • Tutorial and combo challenges for every character archetype
  • Frame data and training tools built into the client
  • Career ladder across sanctioned promotions
  • Financial modelling with real consequences

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control spacing and frame advantage, then punish predictable behaviour with a full combo.
  2. 02Play long sets rather than first-to-one; adaptation is the real skill.
  3. 03Practise blocking low attacks on reaction, then add delayed tech.
  4. 04Complete the tutorial and the character-specific combo trials before ranked.
  5. 05Set a ranked goal per session and stop when you hit it, win or lose.
  6. 06Use training mode's frame data display to check whether moves are safe on block.
  7. 07Watch your own replays and count how often you press a button while minus.
