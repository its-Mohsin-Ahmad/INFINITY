Released in 2025, EA Sports College Football 26 is EA Orlando's take on modern sports design with football and management at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. EA Sports College Football 26 marks another sports entry from EA Orlando, and football and management is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Skill-based timing mechanics that separate the ranks
  • Licensed clubs, athletes and authentic competition structures
  • Online seasons with promotion and relegation ladders
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Squad building with chemistry and evolution
  • Financial modelling with real consequences
