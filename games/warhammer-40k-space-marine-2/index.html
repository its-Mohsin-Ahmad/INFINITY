Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2

Saber Interactive|Focus Entertainment|2024|Third Person · Action · Co-op
8.6104,018 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 marks another third-person action entry from Saber Interactive, and cover shooter and sci-fi is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Saber Interactive built Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 as a third-person action sandbox first and a showcase second, with cover shooter and sci-fi as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Gear progression that changes the visual loadout as well as stats
  • Photo mode with pose, lens and lighting controls
  • Camera tuned for both close-range melee and long-range shooting
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Blind fire, snap cover and flank-happy AI
  • Hardware, ship and station design language

single playermultiplayerco oponline coop

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  3. 03Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  4. 04Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  5. 05Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  6. 06Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  7. 07Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
