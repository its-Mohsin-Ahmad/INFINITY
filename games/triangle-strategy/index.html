Triangle Strategy

Artdink|Square Enix|2022|Strategy · RPG · Story Rich
8.359,204 reviews
PCSwitch

Artdink built Triangle Strategy as a strategy sandbox first and a showcase second, with tactical rpg and fantasy as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. From Artdink, Triangle Strategy is a strategy game where tactical rpg and fantasy drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Doctrines and tech trees with genuine opportunity costs
  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Grid-based tactics with height and cover
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  3. 03Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  4. 04Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  5. 05Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  6. 06Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  7. 07Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
