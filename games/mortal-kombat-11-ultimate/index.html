Mortal Kombat 11 Ultimate

NetherRealm Studios|Warner Bros. Games|2020|Fighting · Multiplayer · Action · Story Rich
8.856,166 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Mortal Kombat 11 Ultimate is a fighting experience from NetherRealm Studios, built around arena shooter and monster hunt. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. NetherRealm Studios returns with Mortal Kombat 11 Ultimate, a fighting title that leans hard into arena shooter and monster hunt. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Ranked ladders with per-character rating
  • Tutorial and combo challenges for every character archetype
  • Full story mode with hand-animated cinematics
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Full stat tracking with match history and replays
  • Dedicated server architecture with a 60Hz+ tick rate
  • Short match formats built for ranked play
  • Creature design grounded in practical-effect horror

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control spacing and frame advantage, then punish predictable behaviour with a full combo.
  2. 02Learn the matchup for the character you lose to most before learning a new fighter.
  3. 03Use training mode's frame data display to check whether moves are safe on block.
  4. 04Watch your own replays and count how often you press a button while minus.
  5. 05Practise blocking low attacks on reaction, then add delayed tech.
  6. 06Set a ranked goal per session and stop when you hit it, win or lose.
  7. 07Play long sets rather than first-to-one; adaptation is the real skill.
