Payday 2

Overkill Software|Starbreeze Studios|2013|FPS · Co-op · Action
8.546,080 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

Payday 2 takes the first-person shooter blueprint and rebuilds it around heist and stealth. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. In Payday 2, Overkill Software uses first-person shooter conventions as a foundation and heist and stealth as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Gunplay tuned for controller and mouse alike, with no aim-assist tax
  • Full-spectrum audio with occlusion and distance falloff
  • Destruction and bullet penetration modelled on real materials
  • Friends Pass access so a squad only needs one copy
  • Proximity voice with optional speech-to-text
  • Drop-in and drop-out with join-in-progress checkpoints
  • Loud or quiet approaches with different payouts
  • Detection cones, disguises and social stealth

single playermultiplayerco oponline coopcross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  3. 03Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  4. 04Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  5. 05Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  6. 06Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  7. 07Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
