Stumble Guys

Scopely|Scopely|2021|Family · Multiplayer
6.980,891 reviews
PCAndroidiOS

Stumble Guys marks another family-friendly entry from Scopely, and party and platformer is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. In Stumble Guys, Scopely uses family-friendly conventions as a foundation and party and platformer as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Online play is the main event, with dedicated servers and skill-based matchmaking. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Family-friendly audio and avatar customisation
  • Assist modes that any player in the room can enable
  • Cross-generation play across living-room platforms
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Full stat tracking with match history and replays
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Custom rules and round modifiers
  • Collectibles and time trials per stage

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Keep the group laughing while quietly engineering the round-ending play.
  2. 02Assign roles based on what each player enjoys, not on who is objectively best.
  3. 03Rotate hosts so everyone keeps their progression.
  4. 04Try one full round with default rules before adding modifiers.
  5. 05If someone is struggling, hand them the support role; it is usually the most valuable one.
  6. 06Screenshot the results screen. You will want them later.
  7. 07Use voice or proximity chat — these games are built on communication.
