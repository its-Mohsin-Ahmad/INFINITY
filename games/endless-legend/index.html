Endless Legend

Amplitude Studios|Sega|2014|Strategy
8.135,952 reviews
PC

Amplitude Studios returns with Endless Legend, a strategy title that leans hard into 4x and fantasy. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Released in 2014, Endless Legend is Amplitude Studios's take on modern strategy design with 4x and fantasy at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • AI opponents that commit to strategies, not scripted pushes
  • Explore, expand, exploit and exterminate across eras
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  3. 03Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  4. 04Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  5. 05Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  6. 06Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  7. 07Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
