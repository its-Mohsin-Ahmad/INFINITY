Deadlock

Valve|Valve|2024|Third Person · Multiplayer
8.6130,173 reviews
PC

Deadlock is a third-person action experience from Valve, built around moba and cover shooter. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Deadlock is what happens when Valve commits fully to third-person action and moba and cover shooter. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Camera tuned for both close-range melee and long-range shooting
  • Squad and companion commands on a dedicated radial
  • Photo mode with pose, lens and lighting controls
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Dedicated server architecture with a 60Hz+ tick rate
  • Draft and ban phases with role queue
  • Blind fire, snap cover and flank-happy AI

single playermultiplayeronline pvpranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  3. 03Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  4. 04Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  5. 05Review one lost match per session and identify the single decision that cost the round.
  6. 06Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  7. 07Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
