EVE Online

CCP Games|CCP Games|2003|MMORPG · Simulation · Strategy
8.372,972 reviews
PC

In EVE Online, CCP Games uses massively multiplayer conventions as a foundation and space sim and management as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. CCP Games returns with EVE Online, a massively multiplayer title that leans hard into space sim and management. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Group content from small dungeons to large-format raids
  • Seasonal story arcs with full voice performance
  • Faction, guild and territory systems with real stakes
  • Dynamic events and disasters that force replanning
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Long-tail progression measured in hundreds of hours
  • Newtonian flight with docking and trade routes
  • Financial modelling with real consequences

Modes

single playermultiplayer

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  3. 03Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  4. 04Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  5. 05Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  6. 06Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  7. 07Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
