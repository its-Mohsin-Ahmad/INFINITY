Steelrising

Spiders|Nacon|2022|Action · RPG
7.044,363 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

First launched in 2022, Steelrising remains a reference point for action design built on soulslike and historical. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Spiders built Steelrising as a action sandbox first and a showcase second, with soulslike and historical as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Stamina-driven combat with precise dodge timing
  • Historically grounded settings, equipment and tactics

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Chain offence and defence mid-combat, reading enemy tells before committing to a heavy attack.
  2. 02Start on standard difficulty and use early encounters to learn the dodge window rather than trading hits.
  3. 03Practise cancelling heavy attacks into a dodge until the timing feels automatic.
  4. 04Use the environment — ledges, hazards and choke points — to control multi-enemy fights.
  5. 05Unlock the first tier of the skill tree and commit to one damage path instead of spreading points.
  6. 06Set aside time for optional challenges: they usually unlock the strongest tools in the game.
  7. 07Upgrade one weapon fully before buying a second; scaling beats variety early on.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
