State of Survival

FunPlus|FunPlus|2019|Strategy · Survival · Multiplayer
7.073,626 reviews
AndroidiOS

FunPlus built State of Survival as a strategy sandbox first and a showcase second, with tower defense and post-apocalyptic as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. State of Survival takes the strategy blueprint and rebuilds it around tower defense and post-apocalyptic. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • Mod support with exposed data files and tooling
  • Doctrines and tech trees with genuine opportunity costs
  • Co-op scaling that keeps the challenge intact for a full squad
  • Environmental threats ranging from weather to predators
  • Base building with structural and defensive layers
  • Wave planning with upgrade branching
  • Scavenging in ruined landscapes under faction pressure

single playermultiplayeronline pvpranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Play the tutorial campaign, then restart on a higher difficulty once you understand the economy.
  3. 03Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  4. 04Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  5. 05Use the speed controls — pausing and fast-forwarding is part of the toolkit.
  6. 06Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  7. 07Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
