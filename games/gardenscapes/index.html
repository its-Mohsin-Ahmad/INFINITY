Gardenscapes

Playrix|Playrix|2016|Puzzle · Family
7.557,275 reviews
AndroidiOS

Released in 2016, Gardenscapes is Playrix's take on modern puzzle design with idle and hidden object at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. From Playrix, Gardenscapes is a puzzle game where idle and hidden object drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Level editor with sharing and curated picks
  • Mechanics introduced one at a time, then combined
  • Leaderboards, ghosts and speedrun timers
  • Family-friendly audio and avatar customisation
  • Cross-generation play across living-room platforms
  • Generous checkpointing for younger players
  • Prestige loops with permanent multipliers
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement
