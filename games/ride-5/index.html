Ride 5

Milestone|Milestone|2023|Racing · Simulation · Sports
7.844,840 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

In Ride 5, Milestone uses racing conventions as a foundation and sim racing and management as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. From Milestone, Ride 5 is a racing game where sim racing and management drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Replay tooling with camera rigs and telemetry overlays
  • Tuning suite for gear ratios, suspension, camber and aero
  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Blueprint, layout and automation tooling
  • Detailed statistics and analytics dashboards
  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Tyre temperature and fuel-load strategy across race distance
  • Financial modelling with real consequences

single playerranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  3. 03Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  4. 04Drive a single car for ten laps on a single circuit before changing either variable.
  5. 05Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
  6. 06Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
  7. 07Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
