Old School RuneScape

Jagex|Jagex|2013|MMORPG · RPG · Adventure
8.9106,661 reviews
PCAndroidiOS

Old School RuneScape is the rare massively multiplayer release that treats fantasy and idle as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Jagex returns with Old School RuneScape, a massively multiplayer title that leans hard into fantasy and idle. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Deep character progression with respec flexibility
  • Faction, guild and territory systems with real stakes
  • Persistent world with a genuinely player-driven economy
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Magic schools with distinct resource economies
  • Offline progress with meaningful return rewards

Modes

single playercampaignmultiplayer

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  3. 03Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  4. 04Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  5. 05Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  6. 06Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  7. 07Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
