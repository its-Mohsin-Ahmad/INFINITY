Subnautica: Below Zero

Unknown Worlds Entertainment|Unknown Worlds Entertainment|2021|Survival · Adventure · Open World
8.256,040 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

First launched in 2021, Subnautica: Below Zero remains a reference point for survival design built on crafting and sci-fi. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Subnautica: Below Zero marks another survival entry from Unknown Worlds Entertainment, and crafting and sci-fi is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Day-night and seasonal cycles that change what is available
  • Base building with structural and defensive layers
  • Deep inventory and weight management
  • Accessibility suite covering navigation, timing and colour
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Speculative technology with in-world justification
  • Cross-platform play and progression across every supported storefront

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  3. 03Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  4. 04Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  5. 05Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  6. 06Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  7. 07Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  8. 08Platform note — 6 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
