Enshrouded

Keen Games|Keen Games|2024|Survival · Action · Co-op · Open World
8.392,555 reviews
PC

Enshrouded pairs survival systems with crafting and fantasy, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Enshrouded is the rare survival release that treats crafting and fantasy as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Day-night and seasonal cycles that change what is available
  • Crafting trees spanning tools, shelter, medicine and vehicles
  • Co-op scaling that keeps the challenge intact for a full squad
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours
  • Co-op progression is fully synced, including achievements and unlocks

Modes

single playercampaignmultiplayerco oponline coop

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  3. 03Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  4. 04Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  5. 05Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  6. 06Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  7. 07Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
