Split Fiction

Hazelight Studios|Electronic Arts|2025|Co-op · Adventure · Action · Story Rich
9.0139,404 reviews
PCPS5Xbox X|S

First launched in 2025, Split Fiction remains a reference point for co-operative design built on platformer and physics puzzle. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Split Fiction marks another co-operative entry from Hazelight Studios, and platformer and physics puzzle is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Proximity voice with optional speech-to-text
  • Drop-in and drop-out with join-in-progress checkpoints
  • Asymmetric roles that demand genuine coordination
  • Companion and dialogue systems with branching responses
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Collectibles and time trials per stage
  • Systems-first puzzles with many valid solutions

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Keep the group laughing while quietly engineering the round-ending play.
  2. 02Play the tutorial stage with everyone in the room so nobody is learning alone.
  3. 03Try one full round with default rules before adding modifiers.
  4. 04Rotate hosts so everyone keeps their progression.
  5. 05Screenshot the results screen. You will want them later.
  6. 06Use voice or proximity chat — these games are built on communication.
  7. 07Keep sessions to ninety minutes or less; comedy has diminishing returns.
