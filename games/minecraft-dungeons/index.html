Minecraft Dungeons

Mojang Studios|Xbox Game Studios|2020|Action · RPG · Co-op · Family
7.640,665 reviews
PCPS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Minecraft Dungeons is the rare action release that treats hack & slash and roguelite as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Minecraft Dungeons marks another action entry from Mojang Studios, and hack & slash and roguelite is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Endgame progression with ascending difficulty tiers
  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Mid-combo weapon and ability switching
  • Mutators for player-authored difficulty
