PUBG Mobile

LightSpeed Studios|Level Infinite|2018|Battle Royale · FPS · Multiplayer
7.9119,885 reviews
AndroidiOS

First launched in 2018, PUBG Mobile remains a reference point for battle royale design built on military and extraction. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. LightSpeed Studios built PUBG Mobile as a battle royale sandbox first and a showcase second, with military and extraction as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Cross-play with input-based matchmaking pools
  • Cosmetics-only monetisation for competitive modes
  • Large-scale matches with drop-in, respawn and revival systems
  • Sensitivity, FOV and controller response fully configurable
  • Full-spectrum audio with occlusion and distance falloff
  • Weapon sandbox with distinct recoil, handling and TTK per class
  • Risk-and-reward raids where death costs your kit
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

Modes

single playermultiplayeronline pvpranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  3. 03Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  4. 04Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  5. 05Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  6. 06Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  7. 07Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
