Forza Motorsport

Turn 10 Studios|Xbox Game Studios|2023|Racing · Simulation
8.495,547 reviews
PCXbox X|S

Turn 10 Studios built Forza Motorsport as a racing sandbox first and a showcase second, with sim racing and management as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. From Turn 10 Studios, Forza Motorsport is a racing game where sim racing and management drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Rewind, ghost and rival-lap systems for repeat attempts
  • Replay tooling with camera rigs and telemetry overlays
  • Tuning suite for gear ratios, suspension, camber and aero
  • Simulation depth with difficulty modifiers and sandbox toggles
  • Detailed statistics and analytics dashboards
  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Tyre temperature and fuel-load strategy across race distance
  • Financial modelling with real consequences

Modes

single player

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  3. 03Drive a single car for ten laps on a single circuit before changing either variable.
  4. 04Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
  5. 05Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  6. 06Race shorter events first so mistakes cost fewer laps.
  7. 07Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
