Tactics Ogre: Reborn

Square Enix|Square Enix|2022|Strategy · RPG · Story Rich
8.650,322 reviews
PCPS5PS4Switch

Tactics Ogre: Reborn is a strategy experience from Square Enix, built around tactical rpg and fantasy. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. Tactics Ogre: Reborn marks another strategy entry from Square Enix, and tactical rpg and fantasy is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Multi-layered economy, logistics and supply modelling
  • Terrain, elevation and line-of-sight driven combat maths
  • Fog-of-war intelligence, scouting and counter-scouting
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Companion arcs with approval states and unique questlines
  • Crafting, enchanting and upgrade loops tied to exploration
  • Class change and promotion systems
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Convert economy into tempo, then commit to an attack only when the intelligence supports it.
  2. 02Hold terrain that compounds value: resources, chokepoints and supply routes.
  3. 03Scout before committing — information is cheaper than armies.
  4. 04Save before major offensives so you can experiment with different approaches.
  5. 05Group units by role and bind them to control groups you can reach quickly.
  6. 06Read the opponent's composition and counter it rather than out-producing it.
  7. 07Build a stable economy first; early aggression only works if losses can be replaced.
  8. 08Platform note — 4 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
