Sons of the Forest

Endnight Games|Newnight|2024|Survival · Horror · Co-op · Open World
8.6123,129 reviews
PC

Sons of the Forest takes the survival blueprint and rebuilds it around crafting and monster hunt. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Sons of the Forest is what happens when Endnight Games commits fully to survival and crafting and monster hunt. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Co-op scaling that keeps the challenge intact for a full squad
  • Day-night and seasonal cycles that change what is available
  • Base building with structural and defensive layers
  • Finite resources that make every decision count
  • Sound design that prioritises spatial cues over cheap jumps
  • Multiple endings driven by in-run choices
  • Creature design grounded in practical-effect horror
  • Co-op progression is fully synced, including achievements and unlocks

Modes

single playercampaignmultiplayerco oponline coop

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  3. 03Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  4. 04Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  5. 05Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  6. 06Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  7. 07Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
