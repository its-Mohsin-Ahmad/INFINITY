Ghost of Tsushima Director's Cut

Sucker Punch Productions|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2021|Action · Open World · Adventure · Story Rich
9.191,969 reviews
PCPS5PS4

Ghost of Tsushima Director's Cut takes the action blueprint and rebuilds it around historical and stealth. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Sucker Punch Productions returns with Ghost of Tsushima Director's Cut, a action title that leans hard into historical and stealth. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Boss encounters built around readable tells and punish windows
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • A living map of dynamic events, convoys and emergent encounters
  • Distinct biomes with their own wildlife, weather and factions
  • Discovery-led design — landmarks are the map marker
  • Historically grounded settings, equipment and tactics
  • Detection cones, disguises and social stealth

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  3. 03Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  4. 04Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  5. 05Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  6. 06Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  7. 07Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
