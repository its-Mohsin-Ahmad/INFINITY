inFAMOUS Second Son

Sucker Punch Productions|Sony Interactive Entertainment|2014|Action · Adventure · Open World
8.133,675 reviews
PS4

Sucker Punch Productions built inFAMOUS Second Son as a action sandbox first and a showcase second, with superhero and beat 'em up as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. From Sucker Punch Productions, inFAMOUS Second Son is a action game where superhero and beat 'em up drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Environment-driven storytelling with no cut-scene interruptions
  • Traversal powers with city-scale freedom
  • Crowd-control combat with juggles and throws

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  3. 03Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  4. 04Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  5. 05Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  6. 06Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  7. 07Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
