Splatoon 3

Nintendo EPD|Nintendo|2022|Third Person · Multiplayer · Family
8.581,723 reviews
Switch

Nintendo EPD built Splatoon 3 as a third-person action sandbox first and a showcase second, with arena shooter and party as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Splatoon 3 marks another third-person action entry from Nintendo EPD, and arena shooter and party is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Camera tuned for both close-range melee and long-range shooting
  • Cover-driven combat with contextual vaults, slides and dodges
  • Cinematic camera work that never takes control away
  • Full stat tracking with match history and replays
  • Anti-cheat, reporting and spectator review tooling
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Map control, pickups and respawn economy
  • Four-player chaos with rubber-banded stakes

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
  3. 03Use audio cues — footsteps, reloads and ability calls — to pre-aim before contact.
  4. 04Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  5. 05Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  6. 06Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  7. 07Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
