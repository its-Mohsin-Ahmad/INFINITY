Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Sonic Team|Sega|2025|Racing · Family · Multiplayer
8.382,574 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

Sonic Team returns with Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds, a racing title that leans hard into kart racing and party. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. From Sonic Team, Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds is a racing game where kart racing and party drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Replay tooling with camera rigs and telemetry overlays
  • Dynamic time of day and weather across the full calendar
  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Family-friendly audio and avatar customisation
  • Simple onboarding that teaches without tutorials
  • Generous checkpointing for younger players
  • Grand Prix cups and time-trial leaderboards
  • Custom rules and round modifiers

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
  3. 03Race shorter events first so mistakes cost fewer laps.
  4. 04Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  5. 05Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  6. 06Drive a single car for ten laps on a single circuit before changing either variable.
  7. 07Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
