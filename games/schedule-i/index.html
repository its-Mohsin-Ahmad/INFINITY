Schedule I

TVGS|TVGS|2025|Simulation · Co-op
8.7100,983 reviews
PC

Schedule I is the rare simulation release that treats management and life sim as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Schedule I marks another simulation entry from TVGS, and management and life sim is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Interlocking systems where every resource has a supply chain
  • Detailed statistics and analytics dashboards
  • Dynamic events and disasters that force replanning
  • Difficulty that scales with player count
  • Designed from the ground up for two to four players
  • Friends Pass access so a squad only needs one copy
  • Financial modelling with real consequences
  • Build and interior design tools

Modes

single playermultiplayerco oponline coop

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Balance throughput against cost, and expand only as fast as logistics can sustain.
  2. 02Keep spare capacity in power, storage and transport so growth is never gated by infrastructure.
  3. 03Use blueprints for anything you will build more than twice.
  4. 04Set up the first resource chain end to end before adding a second.
  5. 05Save often, and label saves before large redesigns.
  6. 06Play one long session with a single goal in mind; simulation games reward clear objectives.
  7. 07Watch the analytics panels to find the bottleneck rather than expanding everything at once.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
