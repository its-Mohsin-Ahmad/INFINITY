Dark Souls III

FromSoftware|Bandai Namco Entertainment|2016|RPG · Action · Horror
9.160,282 reviews
PCPS4Xbox One

FromSoftware built Dark Souls III as a role-playing sandbox first and a showcase second, with soulslike and fantasy as the spine. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Dark Souls III marks another role-playing entry from FromSoftware, and soulslike and fantasy is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Hand-authored side quests with consequences beyond the reward
  • Dialogue choices that persist long after the conversation ends
  • Deep build crafting across skill trees, gear stats and passives
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Weighty melee and firearm combat with animation-driven hit reactions
  • Escalating set pieces that always hand control back to the player
  • Stamina-driven combat with precise dodge timing
  • Bestiary of mythical species with unique behaviours

Modes

single playercampaign

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Manage light, noise and inventory so that every encounter is one you chose to pick.
  2. 02Learn the safe room locations and treat them as planning points, not just save points.
  3. 03Conserve ammunition by disabling or avoiding enemies where the layout allows.
  4. 04Play with headphones. Spatial audio is the primary information channel in this game.
  5. 05Note where threats patrol; routes that were dangerous early often become quiet later.
  6. 06Keep your inventory light enough to pick up anything you find.
  7. 07Check every room for resources, but never linger once you have what you need.
  8. 08Platform note — 3 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
