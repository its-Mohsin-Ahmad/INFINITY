Homescapes

Playrix|Playrix|2017|Puzzle · Family
7.356,783 reviews
AndroidiOS

From Playrix, Homescapes is a puzzle game where idle and hidden object drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Homescapes marks another puzzle entry from Playrix, and idle and hidden object is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. The core loop rewards planning: scout the situation, commit resources, then adapt when the plan collapses. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is free to play, with cosmetics-first monetisation rather than pay-to-win shortcuts.

Highlights

  • Mechanics introduced one at a time, then combined
  • Leaderboards, ghosts and speedrun timers
  • Colour-blind safe palette and audio cue alternatives
  • Local split-screen and pass-and-play support
  • Family-friendly audio and avatar customisation
  • Cross-generation play across living-room platforms
  • Prestige loops with permanent multipliers
  • Full offline single-player support with no always-online requirement

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Keep the group laughing while quietly engineering the round-ending play.
  2. 02Assign roles based on what each player enjoys, not on who is objectively best.
  3. 03If someone is struggling, hand them the support role; it is usually the most valuable one.
  4. 04Keep sessions to ninety minutes or less; comedy has diminishing returns.
  5. 05Rotate hosts so everyone keeps their progression.
  6. 06Screenshot the results screen. You will want them later.
  7. 07Use voice or proximity chat — these games are built on communication.
