Resident Evil 3

Capcom|Capcom|2020|Horror · Action · Story Rich · Third Person
7.747,446 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox One

Resident Evil 3 pairs horror systems with survival horror and monster hunt, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Resident Evil 3 is the rare horror release that treats survival horror and monster hunt as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Multiple endings driven by in-run choices
  • Optional one-life mode for the committed
  • Sound design that prioritises spatial cues over cheap jumps
  • Adaptive difficulty that scales aggression, not enemy health
  • Photo mode with depth-of-field, focal length and filter controls
  • Ability progression that reshapes how encounters are approached
  • Ammo scarcity and inventory management under pressure
  • Creature design grounded in practical-effect horror

Modes

single playercampaigncross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Hold angles, manage ammunition and convert map control into an objective rather than a body count.
  2. 02Rebind grenade, melee and ping to inputs you can reach without releasing movement.
  3. 03Pick one weapon class and stay with it for your first ten matches so recoil becomes muscle memory.
  4. 04Play the objective. Kills create space, but objective progress wins matches.
  5. 05Practise hip-fire versus aim-down-sights at three distances: close, mid and long.
  6. 06Learn two maps by concentrating on one objective route rather than roaming the whole layout.
  7. 07Complete the opening training range so sensitivity and field of view are locked in before matchmaking.
