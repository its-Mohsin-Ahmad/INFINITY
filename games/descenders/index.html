Descenders

RageSquid|No More Robots|2019|Sports · Racing · Indie
8.141,005 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

Released in 2019, Descenders is RageSquid's take on modern sports design with arcade racing and roguelite at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. From RageSquid, Descenders is a sports game where arcade racing and roguelite drives almost every decision. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. Controls are readable within minutes and deep enough that advanced players keep finding new tech. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • A full career mode with transfers, training and press duties
  • Configurable tactics, formations and set-piece routines
  • Licensed clubs, athletes and authentic competition structures
  • Ranked racing with licences and a clean-driving rating
  • Handling model with per-surface grip and tyre wear simulation
  • Rewind, ghost and rival-lap systems for repeat attempts
  • Boost, drift and nitro mechanics tuned for instant fun
  • Run-based upgrades that persist between attempts

single playerrankedcross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  3. 03Race shorter events first so mistakes cost fewer laps.
  4. 04Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  5. 05Turn off driving assists one at a time, starting with automatic braking, then traction control.
  6. 06Drive a single car for ten laps on a single circuit before changing either variable.
  7. 07Brake in a straight line, then release and turn — trail braking comes later.
