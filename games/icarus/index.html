Icarus

RocketWerkz|RocketWerkz|2021|Survival · Co-op · Sandbox
7.236,609 reviews
PC

In Icarus, RocketWerkz uses survival conventions as a foundation and crafting and extraction as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. Icarus marks another survival entry from RocketWerkz, and crafting and extraction is the thread that ties it together. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. Difficulty is a dial rather than a wall — assists can be layered without ever feeling like training wheels. Session length is flexible, so a run can be fifteen minutes or a full evening without either feeling wrong. Online play is the main event, with dedicated servers and skill-based matchmaking. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Deep inventory and weight management
  • Crafting trees spanning tools, shelter, medicine and vehicles
  • Day-night and seasonal cycles that change what is available
  • Difficulty that scales with player count
  • Proximity voice with optional speech-to-text
  • Friends Pass access so a squad only needs one copy
  • Player-market pricing on every extracted item
  • Co-op progression is fully synced, including achievements and unlocks

Modes

multiplayerco oponline coopsandbox

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  3. 03Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  4. 04Mix quest types to avoid fatigue: a main mission, a side story, then free exploration.
  5. 05Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  6. 06Talk to every named NPC once. Many open side quests and reward chains.
  7. 07Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  8. 08Platform note — 1 storefront tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
