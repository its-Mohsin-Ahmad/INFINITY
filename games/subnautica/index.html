Subnautica

Unknown Worlds Entertainment|Unknown Worlds Entertainment|2018|Survival · Adventure · Open World · Sandbox
9.1122,570 reviews
PCPS5PS4Xbox X|SXbox OneSwitch

First launched in 2018, Subnautica remains a reference point for survival design built on crafting and monster hunt. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. In Subnautica, Unknown Worlds Entertainment uses survival conventions as a foundation and crafting and monster hunt as the differentiator. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Post-launch support has been steady, with content drops that add rather than dilute. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Crafting trees spanning tools, shelter, medicine and vehicles
  • Co-op scaling that keeps the challenge intact for a full squad
  • Day-night and seasonal cycles that change what is available
  • Interconnected level design that rewards curiosity
  • Puzzle systems that combine two or more mechanics at once
  • Optional lore collectibles that fill in the world's history
  • Creature design grounded in practical-effect horror
  • Cross-platform play and progression across every supported storefront

single playercampaignsandboxcross platform

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Set your own route through a living map, treating exploration itself as progression.
  2. 02Follow the main path for the first few hours — it usually unlocks key traversal or ability upgrades.
  3. 03Upgrade inventory or carrying capacity early; it compounds for the entire game.
  4. 04Clear nearby points of interest before moving to a new region so fast travel is always covered.
  5. 05Save before entering unknown territory; open worlds punish overconfidence late at night.
  6. 06Return to earlier regions once abilities open up — gated content is often the best content.
  7. 07Use map markers to note places you cannot access yet.
  8. 08Platform note — 6 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
