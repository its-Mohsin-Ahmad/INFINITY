Street Fighter V

Capcom|Capcom|2016|Fighting · Multiplayer · Action
7.437,580 reviews
PCPS4

Street Fighter V pairs fighting systems with arena shooter, and it is a stronger game for it. Progression is generous but earned, with new tools arriving exactly when the challenge curve demands them. Released in 2016, Street Fighter V is Capcom's take on modern fighting design with arena shooter at its centre. Systems interlock cleanly, so mastery in one area visibly improves outcomes in another. Performance scales well across hardware, with sensible presets and no pointless bloat in the settings menu. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Tutorial and combo challenges for every character archetype
  • Frame data and training tools built into the client
  • Rollback netcode with region-aware matchmaking
  • Full stat tracking with match history and replays
  • Skill-based matchmaking with transparent rating tiers
  • Cross-platform parties and shared progression
  • Short match formats built for ranked play
  • Ranked seasons with visible rating changes and placement matches

single playermultiplayeronline pvpranked

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Control spacing and frame advantage, then punish predictable behaviour with a full combo.
  2. 02Use training mode's frame data display to check whether moves are safe on block.
  3. 03Complete the tutorial and the character-specific combo trials before ranked.
  4. 04Play long sets rather than first-to-one; adaptation is the real skill.
  5. 05Learn the matchup for the character you lose to most before learning a new fighter.
  6. 06Watch your own replays and count how often you press a button while minus.
  7. 07Learn one character's normals, one anti-air and one reliable punish.
  8. 08Platform note — 2 storefronts tracked on INFINITY, each with its own install size and requirements.
