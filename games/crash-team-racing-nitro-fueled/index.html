Crash Team Racing Nitro-Fueled

Beenox|Activision|2019|Racing · Family · Multiplayer
8.545,374 reviews
PCPS4Xbox OneSwitch

Crash Team Racing Nitro-Fueled is what happens when Beenox commits fully to racing and kart racing and party. Every mechanic feeds the fantasy: if it looks like it should work, the simulation generally allows it. Crash Team Racing Nitro-Fueled is the rare racing release that treats kart racing and party as a core mechanic rather than a bullet point. The presentation is confident — strong art direction, an adaptive score and clean, uncluttered HUD work. Accessibility is treated as a first-class feature, covering remapping, subtitles and timing tolerance. The pacing alternates between tense build-up and explosive payoff, which keeps long sessions from flattening out. A full single-player campaign is included and can be finished without ever touching the online modes. It is a premium release with no live-service grind bolted on.

Highlights

  • Rewind, ghost and rival-lap systems for repeat attempts
  • Replay tooling with camera rigs and telemetry overlays
  • Dynamic time of day and weather across the full calendar
  • Family-friendly audio and avatar customisation
  • Simple onboarding that teaches without tutorials
  • Local split-screen and pass-and-play support
  • Grand Prix cups and time-trial leaderboards
  • Custom rules and round modifiers

How it plays

  1. 01Objective — Find consistent lap time through braking discipline and clean exits, then use it where overtaking is possible.
  2. 02Adjust tyre pressures before aero; grip is worth more than top speed for consistency.
  3. 03Once lap times stabilise, add fuel and tyre strategy to your planning.
  4. 04Race shorter events first so mistakes cost fewer laps.
  5. 05Watch replays from the chase camera to spot where time is lost under braking.
  6. 06Use the ghost of your fastest lap to compare entry speed rather than exit speed.
  7. 07Drive a single car for ten laps on a single circuit before changing either variable.
